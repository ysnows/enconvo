# Enconvo 2.5.7 Beta 6

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.7 Beta 6. This build focuses on reaching you at
the right moment with Updates, along with faster voice calls and a deeper
Dictation.

## Highlights

- **Updates waits until you're free**: agent questions, approvals, Mail Alerts
  and finished tasks hold while you type or are in a meeting, go to your iPhone
  once you leave the Mac, and can be paused or quieted from the menu bar.
- **Voice calls get everyday things done faster**: music, reminders, notes and
  look-ups run on the call's own fast model, several at once, with shorter
  replies that arrive sooner.
- **Dictation learns your words**: edit selected text by voice, paste your last
  dictation again, teach the Dictionary what it mishears, set rules for each
  app, and see an Overview of your dictation.
- **On-device dictation and cleanup**: Fluid Speech Mini on the Mac and iPhone,
  Parakeet TDT V3 for 25 European languages on iPhone, and On-Device Cleanup on
  the Mac, all offline and without points.

## Updates and Notifications

- Added Updates, which holds agent questions, approvals, Mail Alerts and
  finished tasks while you type or are in a meeting, sends them to your iPhone
  right away when you have left the Mac, and lists what is waiting in a new
  Updates submenu in the menu bar.
- Added Pause Updates for 1 Hour and Quiet Hours to the menu bar and
  Settings › Notifications, with options to let approvals or agent questions
  through during quiet hours.
- Added Approve and Deny buttons to agent approval notifications on the iPhone,
  and Approve, Deny and Open Chat buttons to the Mac notification an agent
  question uses when the Dynamic Island is turned off.
- Changed Mail Alerts to show its alert as a Mac notification with Open and
  Dismiss buttons when the Dynamic Island is turned off, instead of not showing
  it at all.
- Fixed the meeting-record reminder asking again when you switch away from a
  Microsoft Teams or FaceTime call and back while the call is still going.

## Voice Command and Voice Calls

- Changed Voice Command and voice calls on the Mac and iPhone to handle
  everyday things such as playing music, adding reminders and notes, and
  look-ups on the call's own fast model, each in its own Task chat and several
  at once, handing only long or complex work to a full Agent chat.
- Changed voice call tasks to know the whole conversation and how the call's
  other tasks are going, so a correction you make during the call reaches the
  task, and a task starts from what another one already found.
- Changed voice calls to tell the results of several requests running at once
  in the order you asked for them, instead of the order they finished.
- Changed Voice Command and voice calls to answer sooner, by reusing the
  model's cached instructions across presses and calls within the hour and
  getting them ready while you hold the key to speak.
- Changed Voice Command and voice call replies to be shorter and more to the
  point, while still sounding warm.
- Changed Voice Command answers to say the result itself, such as the names in
  a short list, instead of showing a card, so they're spoken and shown sooner.
- Changed progress updates in Voice Command and voice calls to come at most
  every 15 seconds while a task runs, instead of every few seconds.
- Changed Voice Command on the Mac to know which app you're using when you
  speak, so requests like "pause this" or "summarize this page" are about that
  app.
- Changed voice calls on the Mac and iPhone to recognize the words in your
  Dictation dictionary, such as names and product terms, the way Dictation
  does.
- Changed voice calls on Mac and iPhone to wait up to 3 seconds when you pause
  mid-sentence, such as after "um" or "and", and still answer quickly once you
  finish.
- Changed voice calls to try what you ask before saying it can't be done, and
  to try another way when an action fails instead of asking whether to retry.
- Fixed voice calls sometimes saying a task was handled, a song was playing, or
  a reminder or note was added without doing it, or handing an everyday request
  to a full Agent chat after agreeing to do it.
- Fixed voice calls not following a correction, such as switching from the
  weather to train times, or telling you the result of the task you had just
  corrected; the call now stops the task you no longer want and starts the
  right one.
- Fixed voice calls treating what you add right after a request as small talk
  or a new request, or leaving out a lookup the call said it was still doing;
  both now go along with the task the call just started.
- Fixed voice calls reading the local weather when you ask about the weather in
  another city; that now starts a task for the city you named.
- Fixed voice calls sometimes giving a task the wrong date when you name a day
  such as "the day after tomorrow" or "this Sunday".
- Fixed voice calls and Voice Command sometimes talking about you instead of to
  you, such as reading out the assistant's own notes about your request or
  retelling a message you had just typed, before answering.
- Fixed voice calls ending when speech recognition dropped mid-call; the call
  now reconnects it and goes on without greeting you again.
- Fixed voice call tasks telling you to click, finish a step or look something
  up yourself, such as playing the first YouTube result or checking train times
  on the booking site; they now do it in the browser or hand it to the Agent.
- Fixed Voice Command and voice calls only opening an app when you ask them to
  open it and do something there, such as "open the browser and search for…",
  and then saying the rest was in progress; they now do the whole request.
- Fixed voice calls setting a volume you name, such as 30%, to the nearest
  fixed step instead, and answering in English when a Chinese request names an
  app in English.
- Fixed voice calls sometimes staying silent, instead of going on, when a sound
  briefly interrupted the answer right after you finished speaking.
- Changed voice call tasks to answer look-ups from web search results and open
  the browser only when a page can't be read any other way, so simple questions
  finish faster.
- Changed voice call replies to be shorter: a few words that your request is
  underway, without repeating it, and a short answer when it is done, without
  describing the page or naming sources unless you ask.
- Changed voice calls to get ready for your first request as the call connects,
  so the first task starts sooner.
- Changed voice calls to know which skills each task used, so you can ask about
  a task without opening it.
- Fixed voice calls only stopping a task you dropped for something else, or
  still telling its result; they now start the new request in its place.
- Fixed voice call tasks sometimes answering for another route or date when a
  site ignored what they typed, and leaving gaps where they named their
  sources.
- Fixed voice call task cards sometimes showing a whole Chinese request, or the
  name of another request you made in the same breath, instead of a short name.
- Fixed voice calls sometimes reading out, in English, why a task was replaced
  when you switched to another request.

## Dictation

- Added Edit selected text by voice: select text, start Dictation and say how
  to change it, such as "make this more formal", and Dictation rewrites the
  selection.
- Added Paste Last Dictation, Control+Command+V by default, to paste your most
  recent dictation again, even after a restart.
- Added Heard as to Dictionary words so Dictation replaces what it keeps
  mishearing, Dictionary suggestions from words you fix by hand right after
  dictating, and an Add to dictionary button on each item in Dictation History.
- Added the original transcript next to the final text in Dictation History,
  with Re-process to run a dictation through another post action and Transcribe
  again to transcribe its recording anew.
- Added an Overview to Dictation History with your words, dictations, average
  speed and time saved for the last 30 days or all time, and a chart of the
  words you dictated each day.
- Removed the refresh button from Dictation History, which now updates on its
  own when you dictate or delete an item.
- Added Save dictation history and Keep history for to Dictation settings, to
  stop saving dictations or delete older ones and their recordings
  automatically.
- Added App Rules to Dictation for choosing the post action, language and
  whether to press Return for each app or website.
- Added a Dictation Language setting, and a language switch in the Voice Input
  window's right-click menu.
- Added a notice when a Dictation post action fails, so you know the original
  transcript was inserted instead.
- Added On-Device Cleanup, a Dictation post action that removes filler words
  and repeats and fixes punctuation offline with Spokenly Cleanup 1 by
  Spokenly, with its model downloaded, cancelled or deleted right in
  Settings › Dictation; it works best in English and needs an Apple silicon Mac
  with macOS 15 or later.
- Changed Settings › Dictation to follow the order of a dictation (Start,
  Listen, Clean up, Insert), with a summary of your current choices at the top
  and an index beside it that jumps to any step.
- Changed Clean up in Settings › Dictation to a list of options, such as Polish
  and Translate, where each one opens to show and edit its prompt and model or
  restore its default prompt, and adding your own takes only a name, a prompt
  and a model.
- Fixed the Clean up switch hotkey skipping options: it now cycles through Off,
  Polish, Translate, On-Device Cleanup and your own options in the order
  Settings › Dictation lists them, including an option you add while dictating.
- Fixed Settings › Dictation showing a new Clean up option with the model's
  default settings instead of the ones it will use, and showing an outdated
  Clean up choice after you switched it with the hotkey.
- Added Fluid Speech Mini by FluidVoice as an on-device English transcription
  model on macOS 15 and later, downloadable from the Parakeet (CoreML) models,
  with a Realtime version for live dictation and captions that shares the same
  download.
- Changed Dictation and Voice Command to show your words sooner and to stay
  ready for five minutes after you finish or cancel, on the Mac and in iPhone
  Dictation, so dictating again after a short break starts right away.
- Changed Dictation to put the text on the clipboard and tell you to press ⌘V
  when no text field is focused, instead of losing it.
- Changed the Polish post action to keep only what you settled on when you
  correct yourself mid-sentence, such as "three pm, no wait, two pm".
- Changed Silence other audio while recording to pause music and videos and
  resume them when you finish, muting only what keeps playing, with How to
  silence options to only pause or to mute the system volume as before.
- Fixed modifier-only hotkeys, such as a Right Command hotkey for Dictation,
  starting by mistake when you Command-click or press a shortcut with that key.
- Fixed Dictation dropping a real "Thank you" with models other than Whisper,
  so only Whisper's silence filler is removed now.
- Fixed the accuracy and speed ratings on dictation and transcription model
  cards being invisible in the light appearance.

  open-microphone voice call, so you say what to play and it plays, pauses,

- Added the linked app's icon and a send button to the Voice Command view you

- Changed the Voice Command Listening view to shrink to the compact pill when

- Added a speaker button at the right end of the Voice Command view that mutes

  Voice Commands until you turn it back on, while voice calls keep their

- Changed the Avatar button in the Dynamic Island's voice call view to a small
  button at its top left, so the call keeps just two buttons on each side.
- Fixed hovering over or clicking the Dynamic Island after a Voice Command: it
  opens Home once the command is done, brings back the Voice Command view while
  its task is still running, and opens the conversation only when you click
  that view or pick it in Recent chats.
- Fixed the Dynamic Island's Spotify and Apple Music buttons staying dimmed for

## Spotify and Apple Music

- Fixed the Mac's Play/Pause, Next Track and Previous Track actions doing
  nothing, so Voice Command, voice calls and agents can pause, resume or skip
  whatever is playing in Spotify, Music, a browser or another player, and tell
  you what was left playing or that nothing is playing.
- Fixed playing a song by name in Spotify taking 10 to 25 seconds and then
  failing when Enconvo had opened Spotify, so agents and voice calls now play
  it in a few seconds, and answer within seconds when Spotify can't be read.
- Fixed playing or searching for a song in Spotify sometimes taking minutes
  when Spotify stopped responding: Enconvo now notices within seconds and looks
  the song up on the web instead, and other Computer Use actions no longer wait
  behind Spotify.
- Changed Spotify pause, resume, skip, seek and volume commands to finish in
  about half a second instead of up to one and a half seconds, and playlist
  changes to finish faster.
- Added adding or removing the song playing now in a Spotify playlist without
  naming it, such as "add this song to my workout playlist".
- Changed agents to leave Spotify's volume alone unless you ask, and to tell
  you Spotify is muted instead of turning it up.
- Changed Voice Command and agents to go straight to Spotify when asked to
  change a playlist, instead of trying Apple Music first and checking which
  music app was playing.
- Fixed adding songs to Spotify playlists with Spotify's new Add to playlist
  panel, including while Spotify is still loading search results, without ever
  taking out a song that's already in the playlist.
- Fixed Spotify playlist changes failing while Spotify's window was covered by
  other windows: Spotify now comes forward for a moment, and the app you were
  in comes back when it's done.
- Fixed Spotify playlist changes saying they worked when Spotify added a
  different song or still listed the song, and listing or removing songs right
  after one was added.
- Fixed Spotify playlist and song names not matching across simplified and
  traditional Chinese, such as 爱错 for 愛錯, and a song named with its artist in
  another language picking a cover or another version.
- Fixed renaming, opening or deleting a Spotify playlist failing after you had
  edited a playlist's details in Spotify.
- Fixed Spotify's volume being set one lower than asked, and going back a track
  only restarting the current song when asked a few seconds into it.
- Fixed Computer Use sometimes reading or pressing the wrong element in apps
  such as Spotify.
- Changed Dance Along with Music on the Avatar to be on by default: she dances
  while Spotify or Music plays once Enconvo is allowed Screen Recording, and
  stays still across restarts once you turn it off from her menu.
