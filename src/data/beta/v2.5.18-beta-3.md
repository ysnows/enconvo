# Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 3

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 3. This build lets a Project's team members work on your other computers, shows a Team mode run on its flowchart and ends it with what the team delivered, gives each chat its own Permissions, moves Friends to a place of its own with a new composer and picture viewer, and adds voice replies and Mail Triage to Email, Dictation Draft, and Enconvo Keyboard on the iPhone. Please test a Team mode run with a member set to run on another paired computer, a run's List and Flow, Permissions in a chat that starts subagents or member tasks, Friend Chat pictures and files, replying to a mail by voice, Dictation Draft in a chat app, and Enconvo Keyboard.

## Highlights

- **Team members on your other computers**: Runs on lets Mavis or a Coding agent such as Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity work on another paired computer with that computer's files and sign-ins, its code coming back into this Mac's copy as changes you review, and Try again or Run on This Mac instead whenever it can't go on there.
- **Team mode in any chat**: a Team switch at the top of a chat outside a Project turns Team mode on with your Personal team, led by your default Agent, with Todos for your Agenda in place of Board.
- **Ask the Lead to change the team**: turn Team mode on or off, change the team's Instructions or switch teams from the chat, each change with Undo, and a Project with no team starts at once with the All-round team.
- **A run's Flow**: List | Flow on the run's card and in the background tasks bar shows the run on the team's flowchart, each step wearing the faces of who does it and the step at work lit.
- **The team shows what it delivered**: when the work ends, the Lead draws the results right in the chat with Intelligent UI, as cards or an app with its files ready to open.
- **Each member's row opens its whole conversation** in a tab beside the chat, and the background tasks bar stays after the run with each member marked done or failed.
- **Permissions belong to each chat**: the mode you pick applies to that chat and everything it starts, subagents and member tasks included, at once, even in work already running.
- **Device management for agents**: ask in a chat to list the computers signed in to your account, sign one out to free a place under the Device limit, set the primary computer, or rename and unpair your phones and computers.
- **Friends has its own place** in the Chat Window's rail, with a blue unread count, each chat's newest message, and a composer like the AI chat's.
- **The Friend Chat picture viewer** steps through a chat's pictures with the arrows or the arrow keys and zooms to actual size with a click, and text files open with Find.
- **Reply to mail by voice**: press the mic on an Email reply and speak, then Done, Draft or Revise turns your words into the reply, with the new Email Draft AI command and your own AI Commands in Rewrite.
- **Mail Triage reads every new mail**, so a suspended account or a failed payment no longer hides among promotions, and its model is set in Settings › AI Model › Task models.
- **Email mails read like Today's events**, with Open in Gmail and Open in Mail, Delete in the top bar, and Got it with Undo.
- **Dictation Draft** grows out of the Dictation pill, writes in the conversation's language to the right person, and folds long instructions so the draft stays in view.
- **Enconvo Keyboard on the iPhone**: a full English and Chinese pinyin keyboard, 26-key and 9-key, with dictation on Enconvo Cloud Fast from its mic or by holding the space bar.
- **Remote Screen Touch** on iPhone and Android: hold a moment and drag to move a window or select, let go to click, and hold longer to right-click.
- **SSH hosts keep working after a restart**: Remember in Keychain for passphrases and passwords, Use a key for a host that signs in with a password, and Unlock right on the chat step.
- **Temporary chats by default** in the App Sidebar and on the Dynamic Island with the New Chats setting.
- **Your company's certificates**: Enconvo now trusts the certificates your Mac trusts in Keychain, so a model server behind your company's own certificate authority works.
- **OpenAI-compatible servers without a key**: the API Key and API Base URL are optional, and built-in providers can be renamed.
- **Dictation says why a connection failed**, such as a rejected API key or a used-up quota, and offers the fix in place.
- **Agent Phone over Your iPhone** goes ahead while a meeting or a recorder uses the microphone.
- **Artifacts open at once and say what went wrong**: the Library, whiteboards, spreadsheets, docs, dashboards and media offer Try again where they used to fail without a word.
- **Clickable screenshot key hints**: Ask AI, Copy, Save, Undo and Exit at the bottom of a screenshot now work with a click too.
