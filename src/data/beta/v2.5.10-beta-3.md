# Enconvo 2.5.10 Beta 3

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.10 Beta 3. This build gives each request in a Project its own chat with the lead, adds Finish to worktrees, brings Codex chats Plan mode and most of Codex's terminal commands, puts the side panel with Files, Changes, Terminal and Doodle in the iPhone app, and adds /loop, chat archiving, scheduled tasks that report back to their chat, an Activity log and a redesigned Memory page.

## Highlights

- **A new chat with the lead for each request**: New chat in a Project with members starts a fresh chat with its lead, each with its own Work plan, you can @ mention members to hand them tasks, and the lead does quick jobs itself.
- **Finish a worktree**: commit with a drafted message, merge or open a pull request, and archive the worktree once its work is merged, with a Worktrees section in Project settings and branches named after the task.
- **Codex as in its terminal app**: Codex chats get Plan mode, Approve for me, `$` mentions and more than 20 slash commands such as /status, /diff, /mcp, /usage, /fork and /export, and Codex's settings add Hooks, Skills, Plugins, Apps, Memories and Personality.
- **The side panel on the iPhone**: web pages open beside or over the chat with history and Find, and a Mac chat brings its Files, Changes, Side chats, MCP Apps, a Terminal in its folder, and Doodle.
- **Compare answers on the iPhone**: send your next message to a saved set, Second Opinion or 2 to 6 agents from a Mac chat or a new one.
- **/loop**: run a prompt again in the chat on an interval, a set number of times, or whenever the agent decides to come back.
- **Scheduled tasks report back to the chat**: a task's result comes back to the chat you set it up in, where its agent tells you what it found, and Automations are now called Scheduled.
- **Archive chats**: archive chats from the sidebar with E, a swipe or ⌘-click, or on their own after 3, 7 or 30 days, and restore them from Settings › Archived Chats.
- **A Changes review**: the side panel's Changes tab shows one turn at a time with a file tree and every change in one scroll, and the files changed card gets a Review button.
- **Activity log**: Settings › Usage Statistics lists every AI request, tool call and scheduled task with the files and accounts it touched, kept only on your Mac.
- **Memory, redesigned**: the Memory page shows every Memory in full and opens from the Chat Window sidebar, and each Agent can choose which Memories it reads and whether it saves new ones.
- **GPT-6.1 Terra and plan limits in the model picker**: OpenAI's new mid-range model on your key, ChatGPT sign-in and Enconvo Cloud, and your subscription's 5-hour and weekly limits right under the account.
- **A hotkey for each app**: Settings › Applications gives any app a hotkey that opens it, brings it to the front or hides it.
- **The PopBar in the Chat Window**: select text in a reply, a message or the composer to act on it as in any other app.
- **Chats open whole and faster**: switching chats shows a chat all at once at its latest message, without pieces or jumps, and long chats open faster.
- **Telnyx for Agent Phone**: Telnyx joins Twilio as a phone provider for calls to you and calls agents make for you.
- **Free Pollinations images**: make images with no key on Pollinations' free tier, or sign in with Pollinations instead of pasting a key.
