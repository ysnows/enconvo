# Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 4

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 4. This build brings Team mode to the iPhone and iPad, with a task card sized for a finger, a run's Flow, the Team sheet, each member's task chat and a Live Activity that answers from the Dynamic Island. Email shows a conversation's earlier mail as one Earlier list, a Screen Doodle capture goes to the Enconvo window in front, and Settings › Account activates this Mac in place when a device slot is free. Please test a Team mode run in a Project chat on the iPhone or iPad, its Live Activity, Email conversations with earlier mail, and Ask AI or New Chat on a capture taken with Screen Doodle's shortcut while the App Sidebar shows.

## Highlights

- **Team mode on the iPhone and iPad**: the task card shows each member's face, what they're doing and Stop, Flow opens the team's flowchart in a sheet, and each member's task chat slides in over the Lead chat on the iPhone or opens as a tab beside it on the iPad.
- **The Team sheet on the iPhone and iPad**: tap the team's faces under a Project chat's title to turn Team mode on or off, change each member's role, model, effort and note, add or remove members, switch teams, and edit the team's instructions.
- **The Team mode Live Activity** shows the team's faces, how long the run has gone and who needs you, and answers a two-choice question right from the Dynamic Island.
- **Earlier mail in Email**: a conversation's earlier mail reads as one Earlier list under the newest mail, newest first, in place of Show quoted text.
- **Screen Doodle captures go to the window in front**: Ask AI and New Chat send a capture to the Chat Window, Smart Bar, open Dynamic Island or App Sidebar you have in front, not the window used last.
- **Activate this Mac in place**: when a device slot is free, Settings › Account claims it with one click.

## Team Mode on iPhone and iPad

- Fixed the Team mode task card in iPhone and iPad chats, which couldn't show how each member's task stood or stop one.
- Changed the Team mode task card on the iPhone to rows sized for a finger, with each member's face, what they're doing, and Stop on the task at work.
- Added Flow to Team mode runs on the iPhone, opening the team's flowchart in a sheet with each member's task inside its step.
- Added the Team sheet to Project chats on the iPhone and iPad, opened from the team's faces under the title, to turn Team mode on or off, change each member's role, model, effort and note, add or remove members, switch teams, and edit the team's instructions.
- Changed how a member's task chat opens from a Team mode chat on the iPhone and iPad: it slides in over the Lead chat with Back on the iPhone and opens as a tab named by the member beside the chat on the iPad, with the task, the member and how it stands in its header.
- Changed the Team mode Live Activity on the iPhone to show the team's faces, the time it has run, and who needs you and what they ask, and to answer a two-choice question right from the Dynamic Island.
- Fixed the Team mode Live Activity on the iPhone not starting for a run begun after coming back from a task chat or for a new chat's first team run, and showing an earlier run's start time and faces for a later run in the same chat.

## Email

- Changed how Email shows a conversation's earlier mail: in place of Show quoted text and its raw quote marks, Earlier lists each mail before the newest one once, newest first, with who wrote it and when, read as paragraphs and numbered lists, including mails that only appear inside a quote.
- Changed Email to select words the way a Mac app does: a selection stays in the part it starts in, so a drag from the Mavis summary no longer runs on into the mail under it, and a double-click on the summary's title folds it once without selecting the words.
- Fixed the sender line of a mail you sent in Email on a phone-width page, where a long recipient address ran under the date; the address now shortens first.

## Screenshots and Account

- Fixed Ask AI and New Chat in a capture taken with Screen Doodle's shortcut going to the window used last: the capture now goes to the Enconvo window in front, the Chat Window, Smart Bar, open Dynamic Island or App Sidebar, and New Chat starts its new chat there.
- Fixed Settings › Account for a Mac that isn't activated while a device slot is free: Activate this Mac now claims the slot right there, without opening Manage devices.

## Android

- Fixed new chats in the Android app on a computer reached remotely: the first message, with its photos and files, shows the moment it is sent with the working state under it, instead of vanishing behind Loading chat until the reply ended.
