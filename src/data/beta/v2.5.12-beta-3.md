# Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 3

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 3. This build adds friend calls, Conference Home, captions with live translation and a new conference dock, on the Mac and the iPhone, and puts Friends and Friend Chat in the sidebar. Each Agent gains a Sandbox, Full access asks before more harmful commands, and agents show a card for each phone call they make. The Chat Window and the Smart Bar move to new default shortcuts, and Obsidian, Tailscale and Cloudflare now come from the Plugin Store. Workflows, Automations, paired computers and conferences also get a long list of fixes.

## Highlights

- **Friend calls on the Mac**: a call with one friend is just their picture with quiet round controls, a Message drawer shows your chat with them, and Add someone turns it into a conference without a break.
- **Conference Home in the Chat Window**: one box pastes a link, calls a friend by name or starts a conference now, with Earlier for the past month's calls and recordings, and Call your Team.
- **Captions with live translation** are one press away from the new Captions button, in a band that shows the translation large and the original small.
- **A new conference dock and tiles**: a floating bar with labels, separate Mic, Camera and Captions buttons, a green ring on whoever is talking, and a wrap-up page when the conference ends.
- **A sharing bar on the Mac** with Draw, Mute, Stop sound and Stop sharing, while the Conference window steps aside to its mini window.
- **Agents and Notes are out of conferences for now**, while calls with people get our full attention.
- **Friends in the sidebar**: your friends and Team members sit above your Projects, with unread counts, requests, and a call or a message one click away.
- **Friend Chat** messages a friend or a Team member, with files up to 1 GB that keep sending after a dropped connection, and Report to tell us about someone.
- **Send to** shares an AI answer or an Artifact with your friends, with a note.
- **Profile photos**: your Google photo by default, or one you upload and crop in Settings > Account.
- **A Sandbox for each Agent**: with it on, an Agent's commands can change only its working folder and temp, can't read your keys or saved credentials, and can't control other apps.
- **Full access and Approve for me ask at the right moments**: harmful commands ask first however they are spelled or hidden, many harmless ones no longer ask, and a command the rules can't judge is checked against what you asked for.
- **Agent call cards**: when an agent calls someone for you, the chat shows what the call will say, dials only after you tap Place call, follows the call live and ends with a result card, and Record calls saves calls to Recordings.
- **PipeLLM** joins as a provider for chat, Image Create and Video Create, on the Mac, iPhone and Android, with one API key.
- **The Dictionary learns your words**: it suggests "Heard as" spellings, and Learn from my fixes adds a word you correct by hand after Dictation.
- **New default shortcuts**: the Chat Window opens with ⌃⌘X and the Smart Bar with ⌥Space, so your Dock, Finder's Go to Desktop and Mail's Send work again, and Pause/Resume Audio moves to ⌥P.
- **Enconvo stays in the Dock** after you close the Chat Window, and the Dynamic Island stays on screen over full-screen apps.
- **Obsidian, Tailscale and Cloudflare move to the Plugin Store**, and a new Shopify plugin manages your Shopify store with Shopify's official CLI.
- **The workflow editor is calmer and easier to read**, with Make a copy for built-in workflows and a workflow picker for Subworkflow steps.
- **Automations gain Duplicate and a Run limit**.
- **Agents can mark Apple Reminders done or not done outright**, find completed ones, and move a reminder to another list.
- **Screen Recording asks through Enconvo's own permission window and guide**, instead of a macOS alert that stayed on screen after you allowed access.
- **Friend calls on the iPhone**: full screen with a slim row of buttons, sideways too, with Message and Captions, from a new Video Conference home page.
- **Conferences on the iPhone run as phone calls**, so music or a video in another app no longer cuts off your microphone, with a Ready page before you join, the system floating window when you leave the app, and a wrap-up page with Rejoin.
- **Friends on the iPhone**: Friends in the sidebar, Friend Chat with files up to 1 GB, Send to, Report, Artifact copies from friends, and profile photos.
