# Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 2

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 2. This build brings Team mode with built-in teams and roles Mavis takes, Todos for everything you mean to do, a full screen capture with recording, an App Sidebar that works with each app, a rebuilt Email, image search in knowledge bases, and a tab bar on the phones. Please test Team mode with the General team and the Software team, a Project whose Standard workflow you had changed (those choices should now be lines in its team's Instructions), Todos reminders on the Dynamic Island and your phone, screen capture and recording, Email with a large Apple Mail inbox, knowledge base search while EmbeddingGemma 2 downloads, and command links from a web page or a message.

## Highlights

- **Team mode in Project chats**: turn on Team and the Lead splits your request among the team's members, such as the General team's Planner, Advisor, Executor and Reviewer, who work at the same time, talk on a shared team board and pass a review before the Lead answers.
- **Built-in teams with Instructions**: pick the General team, Software team, Research Desk and more in one step, and each team is just its name, description, Instructions and Members, with a flowchart drawn from the Instructions.
- **Mavis in a role**: Coder, Designer, Reviewer, Researcher, Writer, Planner and Advisor are now roles Mavis takes, the same Agent can join a team more than once, and you can make roles of your own.
- **Todos**: one place in the Chat Window's left rail for everything you mean to do, your own and every Project's, where Mavis writes down what you mention in a chat or call, reminders come due on the Dynamic Island and your phone, and each item opens as a page with its chat beside it.
- **Project budgets and Spend**: a monthly budget in Enconvo Cloud points warns you at 80% and pauses the Project at the limit, Spend shows where the points went, and an Automation can belong to a Project.
- **A setting for each Connector action**: set any action a Connector or MCP server offers to Allowed, Ask first or Off, choose Always allow on an approval card, and undo any of them from Saved approvals.
- **Screen capture and recording** replace Screen Doodle, with Area, Window, Full Screen, Scrolling, Text and Record Screen modes, a Capture menu in the menu bar and a Screenshot & Recording page in Settings.
- **App Sidebar works with each app**: it types in the Chat Window's composer and brings cards for Calendar, Reminders, Music, Spotify, Mail drafts, tables, threads and proposed changes, plus support for Preview and Photos.
- **Email, rebuilt around Mavis**: Today lists each matter as one row, an event's page shows a card Mavis lays out for that mail, and you can select several mails, use keyboard shortcuts and delete with Undo.
- **Apple Mail stays responsive**: Email and the App Sidebar no longer make Mail slow or freeze, even with a large inbox.
- **Spaces show where their data stands**: when they last updated, Refresh and Auto-refresh, and a fix right there when a source needs you to sign in again or add a key, on the Mac, iPhone and Android.
- **Image search in knowledge bases** finds pictures by what they show, from a description or another picture, and Agents see the pictures they find.
- **EmbeddingGemma 2** is the search model on Apple silicon Macs and in Enconvo Cloud, and your knowledge bases, Memory and past chats move over on their own.
- **Whiteboard and Sheets artifacts**: agents draw boards of stickies, shapes and arrows you can draw on, and build spreadsheets with formulas, tabs and charts you export to Excel or CSV.
- **Regenerate and /learn**: write the latest reply again and switch between the answers, or have an Agent chat propose the rules and Skill fixes worth keeping from your corrections.
- **Backup** in Settings backs up your chats, Projects, Agents, knowledge bases, Memory and settings on this Mac, free for everyone, and restores any of them with an undo.
- **Phones**: a tab bar with Home, Mac, Projects and All on iPhone and Android, a Today card on Home, and a model chip above the message box that sets the model, its reasoning effort and its speed.
- **App Fit in Mac Screen** fills your phone with one Mac app at a readable text size.
- **New models**: GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, Gemini 4 Neon with Fast mode, Meta's Muse models, Qwen-Audio 3.1 speech recognition and Cloudflare Workers AI images.
- **The Device limit, fixed in place**: when it refuses a message, the chat says why, offers the fastest fix for your plan and sends the message by itself once it's fixed.
- **Affiliate replaces Invite Friends**: one link per person, and 20% of a friend's first purchase as points, or as cash once you're an approved affiliate.
- **Font Size and pinyin search**: five sizes for chat text in the windows you choose, and Chinese names found by their pinyin or initials.
- **AI Command chats**: an Open in Chat Window button on the small Smart Bar, an AI Commands group in the sidebar, and follow-ups that continue the conversation.
- **Lighter and faster**: Enconvo no longer slows down with a long chat history, its background service holds about half the memory after it starts, and chats open and save without waiting on each other.
- **Safer by default**: command links from web pages fill in the text instead of running, web images in a reply wait behind a Show image tile, and IM channel bots only answer people you haven't approved.

## Performance and Reliability

- Fixed Enconvo slowing down until it stopped responding after a while on Macs with a long chat history: saving a reply while it was being written searched the whole history every time.
- Fixed Enconvo using more memory and CPU with every chat you open: each chat started its own copies of the background helpers behind the model button, settings and chat list; chats now share them.
- Fixed the Chat Window using more memory with every chat you opened: it now keeps the last ten chats and any that are still running, and older ones load again when you open them.
- Fixed the Chat Window slowing down and taking more and more memory over time when you have a long chat history and chats keep running, even with search closed.
- Fixed the Chat Window using more memory over time as Todos, the Board, the Hub, chat lists and reply details redraw their dates and times.
- Changed search, MCP servers and Email to share code and load parts only when needed, so Enconvo's background service holds about 120 MB instead of 210 MB after it starts, and about 270 MB instead of 380 MB after a couple of chats.
- Changed model, speech and search providers to load once and be shared by every setting that uses them, and knowledge base features to share one copy of the vector search engine, so Enconvo's background service uses less memory.
- Fixed Enconvo's background service holding on to memory for features used once since it started: what has sat unused for ten minutes is now let go, and features it had already set aside no longer stay in memory with their open databases.
- Changed Enconvo to use less memory while it starts up.
- Fixed long chats getting choppier the longer they got while a reply streams: earlier messages are no longer laid out again for every chunk.
