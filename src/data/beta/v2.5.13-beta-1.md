# Enconvo 2.5.13 Beta 1

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.13 Beta 1. This build adds Spaces, pages of your own that the Agent builds and keeps in your sidebar and on your phones, Team Knowledge Bases, Workspaces for each client or job, redesigned Projects with a Board for every task, Image and Video artifacts, and agents that call your friends. The iPhone and Android apps connect to your Mac directly on the same network, load faster, and can send while your Mac is offline. Update the iPhone and Android apps to try the phone changes.

## Highlights

- **Spaces**: ask the Agent for a page of your own, such as a live board of deals or a tracker, and it builds it as a plugin that stays in the Chat Window sidebar, on the Plugins page and in your phone apps.
- **Spaces on the iPhone and Android**: open them full screen, ask about the one you're looking at, drag them into order, and see them open in a few seconds from where you left them.
- **Share plugins and Spaces**: send a link anyone can install, or list it in the Plugin Store, with every version reviewed before it installs.
- **Team Knowledge Bases**: share a knowledge base with people or your whole Team as Can view, Can add or Can manage, with additions reaching every Mac within seconds.
- **Workspaces**: keep each client or job's chats, Memory and knowledge apart, and switch between them from the top of the Chat Window sidebar with ⌃⌘1 to ⌃⌘9.
- **Team members by Workspace**: put each teammate in the Workspaces they work in, so they see and call only the people who share one with them.
- **Projects, redesigned**: Chat, Board and Team & settings tabs, a to-do's own board, a Needs you / Working on / New results panel, and Smart start for ready plans.
- **Archive a Project**: take it out of the sidebar and pause its scheduled tasks without deleting anything, and restore it from Settings.
- **Board**: one Board under Memory shows the tasks of every chat, Project and team, with a count of what needs you.
- **Project members on your other computers**: add an Agent from a paired computer to a Project, and the Lead hands it the tasks that need that computer.
- **Agent control for paired computers**: let another computer's agents use this Mac's screen and browser, with an overlay that shows who is in control and lets you stop it.
- **Image and Video artifacts**: keep editing an image or video an agent made beside the chat, with each model's settings and a version for every edit.
- **Agents call your friends**: ask an agent to call an Enconvo friend or teammate, and it talks with them for you and brings back their answer and the transcript.
- **Voice Clone**: record or upload a voice to clone for ElevenLabs, Fish Audio, Gemini and Qwen3 in Settings › Text-to-Speech.
- **Translation Style in Dictation**: Sound Native or a Custom style for Dictation's Translate, like the Translator's.
- **Smarter tool finding**: agents now find skills, plugins, Connectors and MCP servers for requests worded differently or written in another language.
- **Links, CLI and API for AI Commands**: `files=` in Enconvo links, `enconvo ai-command run`, and a row in each AI Command's and Agent's settings with the link, command and API call ready to copy.
- **Retry with another model**: when Enconvo Cloud points run out, switch the chat's model and run the refused message again in one click.
- **Faster phone apps**: the iPhone and Android apps connect to your Mac directly on the same network and load your chats sooner over the internet.
- **Send from your phone while your Mac is offline**: your phone answers what it can, and messages that need your Mac go to it once it connects.
- **Reply from the notification**: a finished reply's notification shows the start of the answer and lets you reply without opening the app.
- **Mac Screen as a trackpad on the iPhone**: slide to move the pointer and tap to click in the floating window over a Live Talk call, with a keyboard button and smoother pinch to zoom.
- **Live Talk word by word**: calls on the iPhone and Android now show what was said in the chat as it is heard and spoken.
- **Fewer freezes on the Mac**: reading selected text, copying, and several checks at launch no longer hold Enconvo up when another app or the disk is slow.

## Spaces

- Added Spaces: ask the Agent for a page of your own, such as a live board of deals gathered from several forums or a tracker, and it builds it as a plugin that stays in the Chat Window's sidebar under Spaces, on the Plugins page's new Spaces tab and in the Enconvo apps on your phones; press New space in either place to start one.
- Added Spaces to the iPhone app: they sit in the sidebar above Friends, open full screen with a box to ask about the one you're looking at, and can be held and dragged into a new order that your Mac keeps too.
- Added sharing for plugins and Spaces: choose Share… to send a link that anyone you give it to can install, or list it in the Plugin Store; Enconvo reviews each version before the link installs it, the link opens its page on enconvo.com, and you can update it, change who can install it or stop sharing at any time.
- Added Share to a Space's menu in the iPhone and Android apps, which sends its link through the system share sheet, or opens Share on your Mac for a Space you haven't shared yet.
- Changed how the Agent builds Spaces that show live values such as stock quotes: they show when the source set each value and whether the market is closed, color rises and falls the way your region does, and keep a list you edit on the page showing after you add or remove an item.
- Changed how the Agent lays out Spaces with lists of numbers on a phone: each item takes two short lines instead of a squeezed table, so names no longer wrap one character at a time.
- Changed how the Agent builds Spaces you fill in yourself, such as a log, a tracker or a daily score: a request like that becomes a Space without asking where to import the data from, every change saves at once, an empty day says nothing is logged instead of showing a score, the score shows how it was worked out, the page no longer repeats the Space's title, and it gets an icon of its own.
- Changed how the Agent builds Spaces that track dates and bills, such as subscriptions: it can add a reminder to Apple Reminders before a renewal and read receipts from mailboxes you let agents read, and a form field it hides on the page stays hidden.
- Changed opening a Space you opened before in the iPhone app: it shows its page as you last saw it as soon as the page loads, then brings in the newest data from your Mac, so it opens in a few seconds instead of about ten.
- Changed how the Agent builds Spaces that gather from several sources: a source it can't reach is named at the top of the page while its earlier items stay, and Ask about can answer from what the Space shows.
- Changed Ask about on a Space in the Chat Window to tell the Agent which Space you mean and where its files are, so it can change it without looking for it first.
- Changed Ask about on a Space in the iPhone and Android apps to tell the Agent which Space you mean and where its files are, as the Chat Window does, so your question goes in on its own.
- Changed Refresh in a Space's menu in the iPhone and Android apps to gather the Space's newest items on your Mac before showing it again.
- Changed the iPhone and Android apps to keep the Spaces you opened last ready, so going back to one shows it at once and brings in what changed.
- Changed new Spaces to keep separate data in each Workspace, so a Space used in a client Workspace doesn't show or overwrite your own records.
- Fixed app and Space icons drawn as SVG showing a generic icon in the iPhone and Android apps.
- Fixed a Space made in Create plugin being labeled an Enconvo plugin on its card; it now says Space.
- Fixed new Spaces opening in the Chat Window without the Ask about box, which changes the Space by talking to it.
- Fixed New space in the iPhone app starting in the Project you last chose, which put the new Space's files in that project; it now starts without one and leaves your choice for new chats as it was.
- Fixed a Space you just made landing at the end of the Spaces list, behind See all in the phone apps; new Spaces now show at the top until you drag them somewhere else.
- Fixed a Space in the Chat Window sidebar showing old entries after you went to another page and came back, when they had changed from a chat or a phone meanwhile.
- Fixed Share saying a plugin's link was up to date after the review declined its newer version; it now shows why and offers Fix in chat.
- Fixed a Space you took out of the sidebar no longer opening from a chat's view of it in the iPhone and Android apps.
- Fixed the iPhone app's sidebar losing your Spaces when it opens while your Mac is away; it shows the ones it last saw, and a Space's own close button now takes you back Home.
- Fixed a Space's file upload buttons doing nothing in the Android app, and a Space staying open after you removed its Mac.
