# Enconvo 2.5.9 Beta 2

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.9 Beta 2. This build gives Enconvo a Memory that learns from your chats, lets you watch and take over the agent in your browser tabs and apps, gives Live Talk and Voice Command their own settings with Enconvo Fast, redesigns Recordings with free summaries, and brings the Smart Bar a Liquid Glass look.

## Highlights

- **Memory that learns from your chats**: Enconvo keeps your preferences, people and plans as Memories once a chat goes quiet, tidies them, pins the ones every agent must see, and recalls across your chats, recordings and knowledge bases, all managed in Settings › Memory.
- **Watch and take over the agent**: a glow, a cursor and a control center show the agent at work in a browser tab or an app window, with Take over, Stop and handoffs for sign-ins, CAPTCHAs and payments.
- **Faster, sturdier browser and app tasks**: browser scripts, App scripts and workflows with Judge and Classify checks and a repair agent get long tasks done in fewer steps.
- **Live Talk and Voice Command settings**: each has its own page to pick how it hears you, the model it talks with and its voice, with Enconvo Fast (Claude Haiku 5.5, free up to $5 a day) as the default, and Fish Audio voices.
- **Recordings redesigned**: free automatic summaries with action items, a transcript with a player, Live Translation, and Transcribe on this Mac.
- **Chats that read cleaner**: an agent's work folds behind one "Worked for …" line, each step reads as one line, and a suggested next message waits in the message box.
- **Smart Bar in Liquid Glass**: a new look in Light and Dark Mode, and list Mini Apps such as Clipboard and Bookmark Search that open and search at once.

## Memory

- Added Memory Review: once a chat has been quiet for a few minutes, Enconvo keeps what will matter in later chats, such as your preferences, the people you mention and your plans, as Memories, and updates or forgets the ones the chat changed, without asking first. It never learns from what you said while Memory or Memory Review was off, never learns again from the turns a branched chat copied, and leaves alone a Memory you change while it works.
- Added Memory Tidy, which goes over your Memories once a day or once a week to merge duplicates, settle plans whose date has passed, write relative dates as real dates and tidy tags.
- Added Memory Review for Live Talk calls: what you tell Enconvo on a call is remembered once the call has been quiet for a few minutes, as in a chat.
