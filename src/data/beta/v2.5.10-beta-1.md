# Enconvo 2.5.10 Beta 1

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.10 Beta 1. This build opens Artifacts and Docs beside the chat, lets more Live Talk models hear you as you speak, gives the iPhone app a full AI Model page and iPhone Apps, adds an Airmail plugin and Agent teams, and fixes several causes of freezes and crashes on the Mac.

## Highlights

- **Artifacts and Docs beside the chat**: pages, small apps, diagrams and programs the agent makes open beside the chat with numbered versions, and docs it writes for you to edit with comments, Suggesting and @Enconvo.
- **Live Talk hears you as you speak**: Soniox with your own key and the other real-time Listen models now stream each sentence while you talk, instead of sending it once you stop.
- **A full AI Model page on the iPhone**: Phone chats get 13 more providers, OpenAI and Anthropic Compatible addresses, Sign in with ChatGPT, models added by ID, context compaction and reasoning effort.
- **iPhone Apps**: Phone chats can check and add events, reminders and contacts, find places, control your Home and set alarms on the iPhone, and agents on your Mac can ask the iPhone too.
- **Kimi K3.1 Pro**: Moonshot's newest models with a 1M-token context, on Enconvo Cloud and with your own key.
- **Spreadsheets beside the chat**: Excel, Numbers and OpenDocument sheets open with their cells, sheets and merged cells, and you can copy cells into another document.
- **Airmail plugin**: dictate or type what an email should say, and Enconvo writes it and opens it in Airmail's composer with the recipients, subject and body filled in; it can also search, summarize, reply to and file your Airmail mail.
- **Submit Diagnostics**: send recent logs and crash reports with personal details taken out from the menu bar menu, and see, withdraw or delete each report in Settings › Diagnostics.
- **Agent teams (Beta)**: put 2 to 6 of your agents and coding agents in a team with a lead, ask the team for something, and the lead shows a plan of who does what, runs it when you press Start, and keeps every task on the team's Board.

## Artifacts and Docs

- Added Artifacts: pages and small apps, diagrams and programs the agent makes for you to keep now open beside the chat with numbered versions, so you can switch between versions, restore an old one, edit it yourself, copy or download it, and send a page's error back to the agent with Fix; pages keep what they save on your Mac, and the Artifacts item in the Chat Window sidebar lists every artifact and doc, even after its chat is deleted.
- Changed Auto mode so asking for a page, app, document, diagram or program to keep runs as Agent and becomes an artifact or a doc, while questions and short answers still get a quick Chat reply.
- Added Docs: ask the agent for a report, plan, proposal, letter or other writing to keep and edit, and it writes a doc that opens beside the chat and fills in section by section, where you can edit it with headings, lists, tables, checklists, dates, mentions, images and charts, add tabs, comment, switch to Suggesting so changes wait for you to accept or reject, browse and restore earlier versions, choose fonts, page width and language, and copy or download it as Markdown.
- Added asking Enconvo from inside a doc open beside the chat: type @ on an empty line or choose Ask Enconvo to have it write there and watch the answer appear in that line as it's written, select text and choose Edit with Enconvo to change just that part, dictate the request if you like, press Cancel on the line to take an ask back, and press Try again if Enconvo stopped before writing it, while messages you send from the chat now tell Enconvo which part of the doc you're looking at or have selected.
- Added @Enconvo in doc comments: mention Enconvo in a comment or a reply and it answers in that thread, or makes the change you ask for and says what it changed; a comment that couldn't reach Enconvo says so, with Send again.

## Live Talk and Voices

- Fixed Live Talk recognizing speech slowly when Listen uses Soniox with your own API key: a real-time Soniox model now hears your words as you speak, as Enconvo Cloud's Soniox does, instead of sending each sentence as a file once you stop.
- Fixed Live Talk recognizing speech slowly with the other real-time Listen models (AssemblyAI Universal Streaming, Azure Realtime on Enconvo Cloud, ElevenLabs Scribe v2 Realtime with your own key, Volcengine, and xAI Realtime, including an xAI account you signed in to): each sentence now streams as you say it, with Dictation's real-time recognition, and your words show while you speak, instead of being sent as a file once you stop. xAI and Azure keep a connection ready, so a sentence doesn't wait for one to open, and Settings › Live Talk › Listen now says when a model recognizes your words as you speak.

- Fixed OpenRouter Text-to-Speech voices in Settings: Fish Audio models now list Fish Audio's voices with a Refresh button instead of only Default, the model list's Refresh fetches OpenRouter's current models, and the voice preview in Live Talk speaks with the model Live Talk uses instead of failing with an OpenRouter 400 error.

- Fixed the Dynamic Island, Smart Bar and Chat Window sometimes doing nothing when you send a message and not showing the model list until you relaunched Enconvo, which happened when an Agent's or Project's folder couldn't be read right away, such as while another app's privacy prompt was open or a file there was still downloading from iCloud.
- Fixed Enconvo staying unresponsive until you relaunched it after its engine got stuck or lost track of the messages sent to it: Enconvo now restarts the engine on its own.
- Fixed Enconvo freezing for seconds at a time while another app was not responding, such as when it read the selected text or the focused window of that app, or when you copied something from it.
- Fixed Enconvo not responding while an invite prompt was open.
- Added Submit Diagnostics… to the menu bar menu, which sends Enconvo's recent logs, crash reports and a snapshot of what it is doing to the Enconvo team with names, file names, addresses, keys, links, chat text and other sentences taken out and no tie to your account, and a Diagnostics page in Settings that shows the upload, the report ID to quote, exactly what was sent, and lets you withdraw or delete any report; Save to Finder still keeps the zip on your Mac without sending it.
- Fixed a cause of Enconvo crashing some time after you opened the Chat Window with its shortcut while another app was in front, such as later during an Avatar call.
- Changed crash reports on the Mac: while Share anonymous product analytics is on, an internal error that macOS catches in one of Enconvo's windows now ends the app with a crash report saying what went wrong, instead of leaving it running half-broken until a later crash that doesn't say why.
- Fixed a cause of Enconvo crashing when a Live Talk or Avatar call started right after another ended, such as when you changed the Avatar's Live-Talk voice during a call or called again to try new Listening, Thinking or Speaking settings.
- Fixed Enconvo keeping about 250 MB of memory in use after its first launch or after installing a skill from GitHub.
- Fixed Enconvo loading its local embedding model a minute after every launch even when no knowledge base needed its search index rebuilt, which kept the model's memory in use.
- Fixed Keep Awake keeping the Mac awake after you turned it off, when Enconvo's extension runtime had restarted while it was on.
- Fixed a Recordings page showing only the first lines of a meeting's live transcript while another Recordings page was open.
- Fixed Settings › Account sometimes showing Get Started with Sign Up and Log In for a couple of seconds after you signed in from the browser, before your account appeared; it now stays on Signing in… until your account is loaded.
