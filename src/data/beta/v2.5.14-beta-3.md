# Enconvo 2.5.14 Beta 3

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.14 Beta 3. This build lets you choose which chat gets a share on your phone, shows your subscription limits in the phone's model sheet, brings the model picker to chats on a paired computer, and fixes the Dynamic Island taking your typing from the Chat Window.

## Highlights

- **Send to on your phone**: something shared into Enconvo from another app can go to a new chat or any recent chat.
- **Subscription limits on the phone**: the model sheet shows your plan, the 5-hour and weekly limits and when they reset.
- **Model picker for paired computers**: pick the model, effort and speed for an agent on another computer.

## iPhone and Android

- Added a Send to sheet on iPhone and Android: content shared into Enconvo from another app can go to a new chat or to any recent chat, with an Always share to new chat switch in the sheet and in Settings.
- Added your subscription limits to the model sheet on iPhone and Android when you chat with your Mac: the plan, the 5-hour and weekly limits with what is left and when they reset, and a model's own limit on its row.
- Fixed charts in chat flashing and redrawing while a reply is still being written, most visibly on iPhone and Android.
- Fixed stat tiles on a phone letting a long value or note run outside the tile; a row of stats now wraps to fewer columns when the screen is narrow.

## Mac

- Added the model picker to chats on a paired computer: pick the model, reasoning effort and speed for an agent there, and see that provider's subscription limits, the same as in your own Chat Window.
- Fixed the Chat Window becoming unclickable and your typing landing in the Dynamic Island while the island stayed open and an agent was working in the background.
- Fixed the Dynamic Island cutting off a message at an `@` when you press Return, so an email address and the text after it are sent whole.
