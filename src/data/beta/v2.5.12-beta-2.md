# Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 2

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 2. This build turns calls into Video Conference, with Friends, scheduling, recording, captions, backgrounds and private messages. Email learns to write and polish replies, Automations gets a new design, and Enconvo agents gain Response Styles. On the iPhone, Projects get Ideas, the Board and Project settings. Local models, paired computers and Email also get a long list of fixes, and Share anonymous product analytics now stays off until you turn it on.

## Highlights

- **Friends** let you add someone by email or QR code, answer requests and call them straight away, with your Team members listed beside them.
- **Video Conference replaces calls**, with a new window: controls across the top, a gallery that keeps its shape as people join, and one side panel for Chat, People, Transcript and Notes.
- **Speaker view, pinning and Spotlight** keep one person large, for you or for everyone.
- **Conferences can be set for later**, once or repeating, with a calendar invite by email, changes on the same link, and every device ringing when one starts.
- **Recording** saves a conference to a video file on the organizer's computer, and everyone sees that it's being recorded.
- **Organizers get real controls**: mute, remove, lock, end for everyone, a waiting room, Stop sharing, and more than one organizer.
- **Private messages and files in a conference's chat**, up to 1 GB each, with @ mentions to ask an agent by name.
- **Captions and translated captions**, in the language each person chooses.
- **Backgrounds and masks** on a conference: blur, scenes, patterns or your own picture, and masks that blur you if they lose your face.
- **Screen sharing with sound**, from the Mac app or a browser, and a tray with pictures of each screen and window.
- **Agents on a conference** join turned off, answer whoever turns them on, can look at your camera when you allow it, and leave finished reports in the chat.
- **Automations has a new design**: Today shows what comes next and what ran, and each Automation reads as When, Steps and Then.
- **Response Styles** let an Enconvo agent answer as Normal, Concise, Explanatory, Formal or a style you make from your own writing.
- **Intelligent UI levels** set how readily an agent answers with charts, tables and cards.
- **Links and maps in replies** now show as cards, with a real map for places and routes.
- **Email writes with you**: suggested replies, Write a Reply, Polish for rough notes, and a check before a reply goes out with a blank left in it.
- **Email works with your agents**: name it in the @ menu, and a mail card in chat lets you change a mail before you press Send.
- **Email now comes pre-installed**, updates from the store and can be uninstalled.
- **Share dashboards and decks** with people or your Team, as viewer, commenter or editor.
- **Dashboards stay up to date** from a file on your Mac or a web address, once you allow it.
- **Decks gain icons and an audience window** for Presenter view, and an agent can bring a design, deck or dashboard back to an earlier version.
- **Work plans end in a result card** that leads with what it came to and your next step, with a Merge button.
- **Boards follow your chats**: filter by chat, group by chat, and see a Project chat's tasks under its row in the sidebar.
- **Chat folders** let writing tasks of two chats in a Project outside git run at the same time.
- **The Dynamic Island's History has an Unread filter**, with Mark all as read and Undo.
- **Check for Updates** now sits in Settings > General > Updates, with the current version and the result right there.
- **Share anonymous product analytics stays off** until you turn it on in the first-run guide or in Settings.
- **The Dictation hotkey now defaults to Hold**, so right ⌘ records only while you hold it. To tap instead, choose Hold or Tap under Dictation Hotkey in Settings.
- **Pairing computers works on the Free plan** for one computer with another.
- **Local models hold on to your chat**: Enconvo now reads the context size llama.cpp, LM Studio, Ollama and KoboldCpp really run with, and compacts to fit.
- **Projects on the iPhone**: Ideas, the Board, Project settings, New Project and the Work plan result card, so you can answer, merge, start or try again from your phone.
- **Decks and dashboards on the iPhone**: open them from a chat or the Artifacts library, comment, ask @Enconvo, share, export and present.
- **Friends and conferences on the iPhone**: add people, see what's Coming up, schedule conferences, send private messages and files, and use backgrounds and masks.
- **Voice chats on the iPhone** move to the system's Liquid Glass.
