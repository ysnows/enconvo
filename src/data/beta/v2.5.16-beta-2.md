# Enconvo 2.5.16 Beta 2

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.16 Beta 2. This build adds Media, one place to make and keep your images, videos and audio, lets you choose which apps Computer Use may see, asks for macOS permissions only when a feature needs one, and lets a chat on your phone move to a paired computer and carry on there.

## Highlights

- **Media**: every image and video you make in one place, with saved subjects and styles, multi-shot videos, music and voice-overs, one-click edits and node workflows.
- **App Access for Computer Use**: block the apps Computer Use may never see, or allow only the ones you list.
- **Permissions when you need them**: Enconvo no longer asks for permissions at launch, only when you use a feature that needs one.
- **Continue on Computer**: move a phone chat, even one in progress, to a paired computer and carry on there.
- **Dictation App Rules**: a Translate language per app, rules that cover each app's website, and one-click rules from the Dictation HUD.
- **Fluid Speech Pro**: offline speech recognition for 25 European languages on your Mac and iPhone.

## Media

- Added Media to the Chat Window sidebar: one place for every image and video you make, with folders, favorites and search, and a composer at the bottom that shows the model, aspect ratio, quality, number of images and what to avoid at a glance.
- Added saved subjects and styles to Media: save a few photos of a person, product or character and pick them when you generate, so they look the same in every picture and video.
- Added start and end frames, several shots with their own prompts and lengths, and your own audio to videos in Media.
- Added a detail view in Media with the prompt and every setting, Reuse settings, Make a video from a picture, and one-click Upscale, Remove background, Relight, Restore old photo, Change angle, Expand canvas and Variations.
- Added music, sound effects and voice-overs to Media, and Add audio to put them on a video.
- Added node workflows to Media: wire text, pictures, subjects, image and video generation, enhancements and audio into steps, run them all at once, or ask Enconvo to build one for you.
- Added a negative prompt, labelled Avoid, to the image and video editor's settings for models that take one.
- Changed the new chat's Image and Video modes to show the model, settings and what to avoid as pills under the composer, with your recent images or videos and a shortcut to Media below them.
- Changed the new chat's Make something kinds to show a short caption under each name, so Film (animated with code) and Video (AI footage) are easy to tell apart.

## Privacy and Permissions

- Added App Access to Settings › Computer Use: block the apps Computer Use may never see or use, or allow only the apps you list and answer "Let Computer Use use this app?" when an agent needs another one; blocked apps are left out of screenshots and show as "not shared" in chat, and password managers and system sign-in prompts are always blocked.
- Changed macOS permission requests so Enconvo no longer asks at launch: it asks only when you use a feature that needs a permission, explains which feature asked, shows a small Dynamic Island hint at most once a day after you decline, and stops asking for a feature when you tick Don't ask again.
- Added a Used by list to Settings › Permissions that shows which features need each permission and whether each one will ask, so you can turn asking on or off per feature.

## Dictation and Speech

- Added a Translate language to Dictation's App Rules, so one app can translate what you say into English while Translate keeps your usual language everywhere else.
- Changed Dictation's App Rules so an app rule for Discord, Slack, Notion, Telegram, WhatsApp, ChatGPT, Claude, Linear, Figma or Teams also covers its website.
- Added a last row to Dictation's App Rules that shows what every other app uses, and suggestions to add a rule for the apps you dictate into most.
- Added Always use … in this app to the Dictation HUD: after switching Clean up in an app, one click keeps that choice for the app, with Undo.
- Changed the Dictation HUD so Clean up can still be switched in an app that has a rule: the switch applies to that dictation only, with Make it the rule and Back to rule.
- Changed dictating with the composer's microphone to follow Enconvo's own app rule for Clean up and language.
- Fixed the Dictation HUD's Clean up chip showing the global choice instead of the one an app rule picks.
- Added Fluid Speech Pro by FluidVoice to the speech models that run on your Mac: it transcribes 25 European languages offline with fewer mistakes than Parakeet, and its Realtime version shows your words as you speak (macOS 15 or later).
- Fixed transcripts from the Parakeet speech models that run on your Mac or iPhone showing "<unk>" in the text, most often in Greek, where words ending in ς are now written in full.

## Projects and Agents

- Added Lead acts while you're away to Project settings › Board: in a Project at Full access, when a task reports back and nobody is in the chat, the lead now fixes, retries and merges on its own, within 6 turns an hour and 100,000 points a day, and never sends, pushes, publishes, pays or deletes outside a task's worktree.
- Added a While you were away card at the top of the Lead chat that lists what the lead did while you were gone and the steps it left for you.
- Added a pause to acting while away: after two failed turns in a row or at a limit, the lead stops acting on its own, Needs you asks whether to resume, and the Dynamic Island shows an amber dot.
- Added a merge gate to Projects: turn on Merge work that passes in Project settings › Board, approve a plan's merge step at Start, and each task merges into the Base branch on its own once its check and a review by another vendor's Agent pass, with conflicts sent back to its member, a banner and card progress on the Board, and every merge paused, never silently skipped, when the reviewer can't run.
- Added sign-in warnings for Claude Code and Codex: their settings page, the Updates list and a Project's Board say when the sign-in ends within ten days, with Sign in again, and list the Projects that use the agent.
- Changed Project leads to stop giving tasks to a member that isn't signed in, and a check whose review CLI isn't signed in now counts as a setup problem rather than failed work.
- Fixed a Project task whose Coding agent was signed out failing and being tried again: it now waits as Held, says the agent is signed out, and runs again by itself once you sign in.
- Fixed Board cards treating every failure the same: a setup problem shows in amber with how to fix it and isn't retried by the Lead, and an Enconvo error says it is safe to try again.
- Fixed a Project's Lead reading a long Board task result cut at about 12,000 characters without knowing it: it now gets up to 24,000 characters with the task's Closing note kept, is told when an answer was cut, and can read the whole answer from a file, and the task's Result on the Board says when only part of the answer shows, with a link to its chat.
- Fixed Board tasks in a Project kept in iCloud with Optimize Mac Storage on failing after five minutes with only "git worktree add failed": a task now checks first, says how many files are still in iCloud, and its card guides you to Keep Downloaded in Finder, while a Work plan warns before it starts.
- Fixed a Coding agent task sometimes failing with "pending_run timed out after 30000 ms" before it started while the Mac was busy reading files from iCloud.
- Changed Fallback in Settings › AI Model so each backup model has its own Reasoning Effort.
- Changed subagents so a message to a finished subagent starts it again right away, even while another subagent is changing files in the same folder, instead of failing with Couldn't message the subagent.
- Fixed chat titles, next-message suggestions, recording titles and summaries sending about 5,000 extra tokens with every request when OpenAI is signed in with a ChatGPT subscription, so they come back faster and use less of your plan.

## Picture in Picture and Artifacts

- Changed the Picture-in-Picture window on Mac when several tasks are open: a bar across the top now says how many are running and holds Collapse and Close, in place of the small arrow that used to hide in the bottom card.
- Changed ✕ on a Picture-in-Picture card to close the whole window; the button that hides just that one card now appears on it when you point at it.
- Added each Picture-in-Picture card's app icon beside its title, with the task's state as a dot on the icon's corner.
- Fixed the Picture-in-Picture window and its cards drifting apart while the stack opens and closes: they now move together, and the cards follow one another out from the corner.
- Fixed the Artifacts library filling up with empty "Untitled" entries: a blank one you start from New or Make something and never write into is no longer listed, is thrown away when you close it, and any left from before go after a day.
- Added a right-click menu to the Artifacts library's cards and rows, also under ⋯ when the pointer is over one: Open, Rename, Copy link, Open the chat it was made in, and Delete.
- Added picking several artifacts in the library with ⌘ click, or a run with ⇧ click, to delete them together, with Undo for ten seconds after.
- Changed a video attached in the chat input to show only its first frame, without the file name bar under it.

## Phones

- Added Continue on Computer to Phone chats on iPhone and Android: a chat, even one in progress, moves to a paired computer and goes on there with that computer's agent, files and tools, and opening it on any phone goes to the computer's chat from then on.
- Added a choice of where to continue a shared chat's link on iPhone and Android: on a paired computer, with the primary one picked first, or on the phone.
- Added Fluid Speech Pro by FluidVoice to the speech models the iPhone can run: Dictation and Live Talk can hear 25 European languages on the iPhone itself, live as you speak or once you tap Done (iOS 18 or later).
- Added videos to the Android composer's recent photos and to the iPhone's photo library in Mac chats, sent to the Mac as they are.
- Changed the Android composer's recent photos so a second tap on a ticked photo takes it out of the message again, and a photo removed from the message is no longer shown ticked.
- Removed Compare answers from the iPhone and Android composers.
- Removed the swipe from the right edge of a Mac chat on iPhone and Android that opened the side panel by accident; open it with the Side Panel button in the chat's header.
- Fixed the iPhone chat's messages staying dark while the rest of the app turned light (or the other way around) after the phone switched appearance, such as at sunrise with Automatic appearance on.

## Other Fixes

- Fixed Computer Use picking files in an Open panel so it stays in the background for files in hidden folders, several files at once, and folders with more items than fit on screen, instead of bringing the app to the front.
- Fixed calls placed through Your iPhone on macOS 27 failing or hanging up when macOS showed its call prompt late: Enconvo now waits up to 30 seconds for the prompt, follows a call you place from it yourself, cancels a prompt that comes after it gave up, and says whether nothing was dialed or the call was dialed but no answer was detected.
- Fixed the Twilio Connector refusing API keys from the IE1 (Ireland) and AU1 (Australia) regions: Enconvo finds the region a key is from by itself, and messages, calls and recordings use it. Balance and usage still need a key from US1, where Twilio keeps them.
