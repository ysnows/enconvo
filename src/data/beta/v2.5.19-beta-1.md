# Enconvo 2.5.19 Beta 1

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.19 Beta 1. This build gives every Project a chat of its own and rebuilds Todos around it: what you ask a Project starts as Todos that run in chats of their own, say in words what needs you, and bring their results back to the Project's chat. Agents now ask whoever gave them the work before they ask you, Computer Use and Browser Use ask in the chat when they need you, and AI apps such as Claude Code can use them over MCP. Also new: Video Maker Studio, TheWhisper on-device speech, a redesigned Live Captioning, Email's Today page, Temporary chat on ⌥⌘N, and Russian and Ukrainian on Enconvo Keyboard. Please test a Project's own chat with a request for its team, answering a Todo's question from Todos, Computer Use on a step that needs you, Live Captioning, and Email's Today page.

## Highlights

- **The Project's own chat**: click a Project's name and ask for anything; the work starts as Todos in chats of their own, followed live on the card that started them, with each result coming back to the Project's chat.
- **Todos rebuilt**: a Needs you lane first, how far each item is in words, List or Columns, and a Todo's questions answered right on its row.
- **Flow and Board on every Todo**: open the team run's Flow or its tasks in five columns from a Todo's top bar, and a task's chat opens beside the Todo.
- **Questions go up the chain**: an agent asks whoever gave it the work, and a question reaches you only when the Lead passes it on with its note and recommendation.
- **A Task chat from the start**: every team task has its own chat as soon as it is added, and what you send there before it starts joins its request.
- **Agents finish what they start**: an agent that stops partway through a task with several steps goes back once to finish it or to say what stopped it.
- **One Project Settings page**: the Project's Memory and automations open in place, with its sections down the side.
- **No more Boundaries**: a Project's chats and team use all your folders, Connectors, MCP servers, knowledge bases and Mac controls, and nothing asks you to widen one.
- **Video Maker Studio**: plan a short film's shots, give each a keyframe, animate them with any image and video model and export the cut, with the Director doing any step for you.
- **TheWhisper Large V3 Turbo**: a more accurate on-device Whisper for 39 languages, free for Dictation and Live Talk on the Mac and the iPhone.
- **Computer Use and Browser Use ask in the chat**: when a step needs you, such as a password, Take over hands you the app and Not now lets the agent go on, instead of the app jumping to the front.
- **Connect AI Clients**: let Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other AI apps on your Mac use Enconvo's Computer Use and Browser Use over MCP, with a setup guide for each app.
- **Live Captioning redesigned**: one see-through glass pill, sentences in joined bubbles, a Chat alignment, and an Auto theme that follows the screen behind the captions.
- **The meeting recording bar in the same pill**: discard, sound level, time, Live Translation, Transcript, Ask AI, Pause and Save in one place.
- **Email's Today page**: Urgent, Can wait and Handled, rows that read like the inbox, a mailbox filter, deadline tags, and a Summary language of your own.
- **Temporary chat on ⌥⌘N**: start one in the Chat Window or the Smart Bar, or turn it on from the Smart Bar's new button.
- **Settings pages in the Chat Window sidebar**: pin any Settings page, such as MCP Servers or Dictation History, and open it in place with a ⌥⌘ shortcut.
- **Models in use**: Settings › AI Model lists the features that run on a model of their own and fixes one whose model can't run in one click.
- **Interpret in Video Conference**: Captions and voice speaks what each person says in your language, free with an Enconvo account.
- **Dictation Draft in the Chat Window**: the draft is written with the chat on screen in mind and goes into the message box.
- **`enconvo run`**: have an agent do a task in a folder from the command line, with its answer and usage printed when it is done.
- **Recordings play full window**: a screen recording's video fills the window with the line being said as captions.
- **Russian and Ukrainian on Enconvo Keyboard**: two new layouts on the iPhone, a language key that goes through every layout, and Swipe Space to Switch Language.
- **A scrolling Emoji panel and a 123 Key setting**: every emoji category in one list with Recent first, and a Numbers & Symbols page like the iPhone's own.
- **Keyboard Translate keeps up as you type**: the translation follows while you type, and an edit retranslates only the sentence you changed.
- **Phones reach your computer sooner**: a message sent right after opening the iPhone or Android app goes straight out, or waits in the outbox while your computer is offline.
- **Team members' work in a sheet on phones**: tap a Team member in a phone chat to follow its work over the chat, with Stop while it works.

## Projects and Todos

- Added the Project's own chat on the Mac: clicking a Project's name opens it, what you ask there for the Project starts as Todos in chats of their own with Teamwork on or off as that chat has it, each followed live on the card that started it and opened beside the chat from there, and each Todo's result comes back there when its chat ends; questions get answered in place, Open on one in the Needs you tray shows its Todo's chat beside it, and Clear history in the Project's ⋯ menu empties it.
- Changed work given to a Project's team in its chat to show up in Todos on its own as an item in progress, with the team's tasks under it.
- Changed work a Project's chats gave the team before this version to get its chat's item in Todos once, so those tasks leave "Not on the Agenda" and sit under the item, done ones in Done.
- Fixed team tasks added in a Project chat whose team run started before the chat had its Todo showing under no Todo: the run and its tasks now go under that chat's Todo.
- Changed a Project's chats in the Chat Window sidebar to list the chat you last wrote in first, so a chat moves up only when you send it a message, not while its agent works, and to give each Todo's own chat a row under its Project and in Active chats with its status.
- Changed a Project's Settings page into one page with its sections down the side, where the Project's Memory and its automations now open in place: search, add, edit, pin and forget the Project's Memories, and turn automations on or off, run, edit, or delete them, without leaving the page.
- Removed Boundaries from Projects and agent teams: a Project's chats, its team and their agents now use all your folders, Connectors, MCP servers, knowledge bases and Mac controls, as a Boundary set to Uses everything did, and nothing asks you to widen one any more.
- Changed a Project's Todos to show only All, as a list or as side-by-side Columns, with the list showing finished items; the Project's Board view is gone, and its Review before Done pause notice and its tasks Not in Todos now show in the list.
- Changed the Todos list to start with a Needs you lane, where Answer or Review on a row opens the item at the task that waits, and to say how far each item is in words, such as 3 working · 12 of 20 done, beside the faces of the roles working on it.
- Changed an item in progress in Todos' Columns to one status line with its button, Answer, Review or Try again, saying what waits on you, why the work stopped, or who is working on what, with the roles' faces and the counts in words below it instead of a progress bar and a grid of task numbers.
- Added Who does it to the Todos list's Display, to show only the items you do or the ones the lead does.
- Changed a task number search in Todos, such as T-642, to show the matching task under its item, opening the item with that task picked.
- Changed a Todo's page to list its team's tasks grouped by where each stands, Needs you, Running and To review first, with To start and Done folded to one line and Done newest first, and to say how many are done and how many need you.
- Added Flow and Board to a Todo's top bar, which fill the page with its team run's Flow or its tasks in five columns, opening at the first column with tasks, Needs you first, when the five don't fit; Back, Esc or ⌘[ return to the page, with the side chat kept as it was.
- Changed a click on a team task to open its Task chat beside the Todo instead of a drawer over the Board, with the task's branch, changed files and Merge, Open pull request or Send back on the card above that chat.
- Changed a task opened from a Project's chat, its start page or its Needs you tray to open its Todo's Board at that task, with the task's chat beside it.
- Removed Sub-items, Related chats and Split from a Todo's page.
- Removed the separate task Boards: the Chats Board, the sidebar's Board of every Board, a Project's Board page and a Project chat's Board tab; a task now opens on its Todo's Board, or in its own chat when it is on no Todo, and a new chat outside a Project opens on a plain "What can I help with?".
- Changed a Todo's status in Todos and on its page to say only what is happening on it, In progress from when a member starts until its team run ends, even when something there needs you, with what waits on you, such as Asks you, Needs your approval or Plan to review, in a separate amber mark beside it that opens what waits; a lead that only replied, a usage limit that resumes by itself and Goal not met no longer put a Todo in Needs you, and Planning is no longer amber.
- Added answering a Todo's question where it shows: its card in Today's Needs you, its Todos row, which says who asks and what, and its page offer the question's options as buttons, with a field for your own answer where the question takes one.
- Changed the Needs you count in Today, the sidebar's Today and a Project's Todos to count each Todo once, however many of its tasks wait for you.

- Added which agent answered a member's question, and why, to the question card in the member's Task chat.
- Changed each task of a Project's team to have its own Task chat from the moment it is added, before it starts, and to start in that same chat; what you send there before it starts is added to its request.
- Changed a team task that hasn't started to show its request and what was added to it, in its tab and in its Task chat, with a box there that adds to the request; a message sent to that chat from elsewhere stays with a note that it was added to the request.

- Changed the bar above the message box that shows a team run or background tasks to fold up when you click anywhere outside it or press Esc in it, while a click in the message box keeps it open, on the Mac.
- Changed a Project team run's Flow so each step's tasks sit right beside that step, a step with four or more tasks shows as one line with how many are done and what needs you, the step being worked on opens by itself and the chart scrolls to follow it, and a narrow window shows the run top to bottom instead of cutting task names short.

- Fixed a team run ending right after Resume, before the tasks waiting in it started.

## Agents, Computer Use and Browser Use

- Fixed the agent stopping partway through a task with several steps, such as Computer Use work, without telling you: when a turn ends without saying where the task stands, the agent now goes back once to finish or to explain what stopped it and what you can do, and the chat says so if it still stays silent.
- Changed Computer Use and Browser Use to ask in the chat when they need you for a step, such as a password or a sign-in, instead of bringing the app or browser to the front on their own: Take over hands you the app with a button back to it, Not now lets the agent go on without that step, and the request reaches your phone and notifications if you don't answer.
- Changed Browser Use uploads of a folder, or of more files than one click takes, so the agent picks them in the browser's Open panel without switching to that page's tab first.
- Added Connect AI Clients back to Settings › Computer Use and Settings › Browser Use: turn it on to let Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other AI apps on this computer use Enconvo's Computer Use and Browser Use over MCP, with a setup guide that shows how to add Enconvo to each app and which apps have connected; they read Enconvo's skill for the tools before using them.
- Added `enconvo run`, which has an agent do a task in a folder from the command line and prints its answer and usage when its turn ends, with the task from the arguments or stdin, a choice of model, effort and permission mode, and `--json` for scripts; the chat stays in Enconvo, in the folder's Project.

## Video Maker Studio, Models and Speech

- Added Video Maker Studio, a plugin in the Chat Window's sidebar for making short films: plan the shots, give each a keyframe, animate them with any image and video model, keep the best takes and export the cut, with the Director doing any step for you and asking before it spends points.
- Added TheWhisper Large V3 Turbo, TheStage AI's more accurate Whisper for 39 languages, as a free on-device speech model for Dictation and Live Talk on the Mac and the iPhone; a language hint, or a language other than English among your preferred languages, helps it keep speech that mixes languages in the language it was spoken.
- Added Models in use to Settings › AI Model, which lists only the features that run on a model of their own instead of their default, marks the ones whose model can't run with a one-click fix, opens a page with every feature and the model it runs on, and holds Task models as Background tasks; the Enconvo Guide can show the same list when you ask which models your features use and propose putting one back for you to apply.

## Email

- Changed Email's Today page to three groups, Urgent, Can wait and Handled, with every urgent item listed newest first instead of some folded under a count, and mail due today now listed in Urgent.
- Changed Email's Today rows to read like the inbox: the sender's picture on the left, the time on the right with Archive and Got it in its place when you point at a row, the mailbox it came in as a chip with its logo and name, and a mail's deadline as a small tag beside who sent it, such as Overdue 1 day or Due today, shown only when the mail names one.
- Changed Read Them on Email's Today page to read only the mail Mail Triage found needs you, so mail it already sorted as not needing you no longer goes to the model.
- Added a mailbox filter to Email's Today page, beside Search, that shows the mail of one mailbox or a few you pick, names them on the button while it is on, and keeps your pick the next time you open Today.
- Fixed mail marked Urgent or Needs reply in the Email list missing from Today: Today now keeps it under Urgent until you reply or press Got it, instead of a quicker check that saw only a preview moving it to Handled.
- Changed Mail Triage to read up to 2,000 characters of each new mail, and Today to judge where a conversation stands from each message's own words instead of its first line, so mail is sorted and settled more accurately.
- Added a Summary language setting to Email, on the Mavis summary's own header and in Automation's Reports tab, so mail summaries are written in the language you pick while suggested replies stay in the mail's language.
- Changed Got it on an Email event's page from Today to close the mail and open the next one still to do, or go back to Today when it was the last.
- Changed sending a reply on an Email event's page from Today to stay on that mail and say when the reply was sent, with Next to open the next mail still to do, by a click or Return, instead of moving on by itself.
