# Enconvo 2.5.6 Beta 13

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.6 Beta 13. This build improves local model
responses, keeps conversations with the right Agent, and makes sharing easier.

## Highlights

- **Local models can take the time they need**: LM Studio and Ollama no longer
  stop after five minutes, and you can choose your own response timeout.
- **Your chat stays with your chosen Agent**, even while it uses tools or reads
  files in a Project.
- **Share from SmartBar and Dynamic Island** with a new Share button, clearer
  shared pages, and smoother scrolling.

## Models and Agents

- Added a Response Timeout setting for LM Studio and Ollama, with no limit by
  default and options up to 30 minutes.
- Changed most other model providers to wait up to 10 minutes for a response.
- Changed the model picker to show clearer account icons and subscription tags.
- Fixed the ChatGPT Pro Lite label and long provider names in the model picker.
- Fixed custom Agents unexpectedly switching to Mavis during a reply.
- Changed Mac tools and MCP servers to use Enconvo's bundled Node.js 26.

## Chat and Sharing

- Added a Share button to SmartBar and Dynamic Island, with a menu that closes
  when you click it again or click elsewhere.
- Changed new shared chat links to open at app.enconvo.com in the reader's
  language, while existing links keep working.
- Fixed scrolling over tables, code blocks, and wide views in Chat Window and
  shared chat pages.
- Removed Export as Markdown from the Share menu; Copy as Markdown remains.

## Other Improvements

- Changed iPhone Companion notifications for Mac activity to play an alert sound.
- Changed Enconvo Feedback to make recently updated posts easier to find.
- Fixed missing items in the Feedback home page's Planned and In progress lanes.
