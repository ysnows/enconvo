# Enconvo 2.5.15 Beta 3

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.15 Beta 3. This build adds Picture in Picture to Remote Screen on iPhone and Android, lets you pick which free limit reset to use on a Claude or ChatGPT subscription, keeps an agent's work going while you send a follow-up, and fixes a set of phone chat and sidebar problems.

## Highlights

- **Remote Screen in Picture in Picture**: keep your computer's screen floating over your chats on iPhone and Android, and over other apps when you leave Enconvo.
- **Pick a free limit reset**: a Claude or ChatGPT subscription card lists each reset with how many it has left and when it expires.
- **Follow-ups that don't throw away work**: the agent keeps writing and running tools while Enconvo checks whether your new message should stop it.
- **Steadier phone chats**: your messages and the replies to them now show in the right place and the right chat.

## iPhone and Android

- Added Picture in Picture to Remote Screen on iPhone: tap the button at the top left, or pinch in, to keep your computer's screen floating over your chats and settings on the same session, and when you leave Enconvo it keeps playing in the system's floating window until you close it.
- Added picture in picture to Remote Screen on Android: tap the new button at the top left of full screen, or pinch in, to keep your computer's screen floating while you go back to your chats, with the same session and no reconnecting; leaving the app keeps it in a small window over your other apps, and only Close ends it.
- Changed the iPhone and Android apps to call the paired computer “computer” instead of “Mac” and Mac Screen “Remote Screen”, so the wording fits Windows and Linux computers too.
- Fixed the phone chat so a message you send into an existing chat shows above its reply, instead of only the reply appearing, including one sent from the Android dictation bar.
- Fixed the phone chat sometimes showing another chat's messages after its page reloaded, with the messages you then sent never showing.
- Fixed the phone chat sometimes losing the previous reply when you sent a new message, leaving your two messages side by side with no reply between them.
- Fixed Wrap Code on a phone chat's code block springing back off right after you tapped it.

## Chats and Agents

- Changed sending a message while an agent works: the reply it is writing and the tools it is running keep going while Enconvo checks whether your message should stop them, so a follow-up that can wait no longer throws away the step; with a model that runs on your Mac, your message still stops the step at once.
- Fixed a sign-in, install or question card blocking the chat when you sent a message instead of answering it: the card now closes and the agent goes on from your message.
- Fixed agents failing to price or start Higgsfield video runs in a mode that needs reference media, such as Seedance's reference mode: they now bring in the reference images first, or pick a text-to-video mode when there are none.
- Fixed agents seeing some settings of a connected app's tool as an unknown type after a failed call, so they now see every field the tool takes and fix the call.
- Fixed an empty card showing under a step in a chat, on the Mac and on the phone, when an app tool such as Higgsfield turned down the request.

## Chat Window Sidebar

- Changed the Chat Window's Active chats list so a chat you open stays in the list, marked done, until the next time you open the window, instead of disappearing the moment you look at it.
- Added folding to the Chat Window sidebar's Pinned group: click its heading to collapse or expand it.
- Fixed Subagent and team member chats showing in the sidebar's Active chats and as rows of their own while they run: the chat that started them shows them, and they get a row only while open, pinned or waiting on you.

## Settings and Stability

- Added a list of your free limit resets to a Claude or ChatGPT subscription card in Settings when you have more than one: see each reset's name, how many it has left and when it expires, and use the one you pick, with the one that runs out first marked.
- Fixed Enconvo sometimes quitting unexpectedly when a window that shows its Dock icon opened, such as a video conference.
