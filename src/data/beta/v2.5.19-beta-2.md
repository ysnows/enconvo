# Enconvo 2.5.19 Beta 2

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.19 Beta 2. This build adds Running now, a third layout of a Project's Todos that shows every chat at work grouped by its Todo, and lets the Lead change a Todo when you change your request instead of starting a second one. Also new: Install as App for dashboards, decks, films and docs, a prompt of its own for each chat app in Draft, a sidebar with Back and Forward in a Space's own window, and a one-row header for what you open. Please test Running now on a Project whose team is at work, changing a request in a Project's chat, Install as App, and Draft in a chat app such as Slack or WeChat.

## Highlights

- **Running now in Todos**: a third layout beside List and Columns shows every chat at work in a Project, grouped by its Todo, the Lead's chat first, with what needs you on top.
- **Act from Running now**: answer a question in place, open any chat beside the list, stop a member's task or the Lead's own turn, and see each task tick as it finishes.
- **The Lead changes the Todo, not a new one**: change or widen what you asked in a Project's chat and the Lead edits that Todo while its chat carries on.
- **Install as App for what you make**: dashboards, decks, films, Whiteboards, Sheets, designs and docs install from a button beside their name and open in a window of their own.
- **A prompt for each chat app in Draft**: Discord, Slack, Telegram, WeChat, WhatsApp, Messages, Microsoft Teams, Lark, LINE and Signal each get their own, and any other app or website can have one.
- **Switch Draft prompts from the panel**: click the app's icon in the Draft panel to move between its own prompt and General, edit either in place, or manage them all in Settings › Dictation › Draft.
- **A sidebar in a Space's own window**: a Space opened from the Dock or installed as an app shows its places in a sidebar, with Back, Forward and a sidebar button beside the window's buttons.
- **⌘[ and ⌘] go back and forward**: in the Chat Window and in a Space's own window, and they and ⌘B work while you're clicked into a Space's page.
- **A one-row header for what you open**: Share now holds inviting people, the link, Send to a friend, publishing and every download for a dashboard, deck, film or page.
- **Links in designed emails open**: links in newsletters and other designed emails open in your browser from Email on the Mac and the iPhone.
- **Phones speak your language everywhere**: the primary computer's menus and cards on the iPhone and Android, and the iPhone's Calls, call button and Add Model sheet, are no longer in English.

## Projects and Todos

- Added Running now to a Project's Todos, a third layout beside List and Columns that shows every chat at work in the Project grouped by its Todo, the Lead's chat first and then each member's task, with what needs you first: answer a question in place, open any chat beside the list, stop a member's task or the Lead's own turn, and see each task tick as it finishes.
- Changed a Project's chat so its Lead edits a Todo when you change or widen the request, and its chat carries on, instead of opening a second Todo; a Todo that a new request replaces is deleted and its work stopped.
- Fixed Todos whose Lead does the work itself, with Teamwork off, showing Planning while their chat was already at work; they now show In progress.
- Fixed a member's task reading Wait for Tool Call while the member waited for a long command of its own to finish, on a Todo's page and in the Lead's chat; it now shows the task as working.

## Chat Window, Spaces and Apps

- Added Install as App for dashboards, decks, films, Whiteboards, Sheets, designs and docs, from an Install button beside the name of what you open instead of the ··· menu; each opens in a window of its own that keeps up with your changes, with a dashboard's, deck's or film's tools in its title bar.
- Changed a Space's own window, opened from the Dock or installed as an app, so a Space with places to move between shows them in a sidebar with Back, Forward and a sidebar button beside the window's buttons, as the Chat Window does, and any other Space or installed page shows its name centered in the title bar instead of a second header strip with its icon.
- Added ⌘[ and ⌘] to go back and forward in the Chat Window and in a Space's own window, and made them and ⌘B work while you're clicked into a Space's page too.
- Changed the header of a dashboard, deck, film or page you open so it fits in one row: Share now holds inviting people, its link, Send to a friend, publishing a page and every download, View code moved into ···, a narrow panel folds the other tools into ··· too, and a check of the numbers or slides shows only when it finds problems.
- Changed Add a Settings page in the Chat Window sidebar's ··· menu so picking a page pins it to the sidebar as it opens.

## Dictation Draft

- Added prompts for each app to Draft: Discord, Slack, Telegram, WeChat, WhatsApp, Messages, Microsoft Teams, Lark, LINE and Signal come with their own, written to answer the person or group in the chat in wording a native speaker would use, and any other app or website can get one; click the app's icon in the Draft panel to switch an app between its own prompt and General, or edit either one in place, and manage them all in Settings › Dictation › Draft.

## Email

- Fixed links in designed emails, such as newsletters, doing nothing when tapped or clicked in Email on the iPhone and the Mac; they now open in your browser, and Email's keyboard shortcuts work after clicking into such an email.

## iPhone and Android

- Fixed the iPhone and Android apps showing parts of the primary computer in English in every other language: making another computer the primary computer, the New chats go to menu, the card shown while the primary computer is offline, and the sheet after pairing another computer, as well as the iPhone's Running in background label.
- Fixed the iPhone app showing the Calls list's call-back count and Likely scam badge, the call button's hold menu, the Avatar settings' call button notes, and the Add Model sheet's provider line in English in every other language, and VoiceOver reading a stray bracket after an upload's progress.
