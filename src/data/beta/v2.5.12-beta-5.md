# Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 5

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 5. This build adds Motion films, a notes panel for conference calls, team workflows and Ideas with goals in Projects, native lists for Clipboard History, Bookmark Search and File Search, and a new Dictation pill with a reworked Draft. The iPhone and Android apps get new call controls, Motion films, and friend calls on Android. Please ask for a short Motion film, take notes in a call, run a goal through a Project with members, and try Draft from the new Dictation pill, and tell us what feels off.

## Highlights

- **Motion films**: ask for a short animated video and it plays in the panel and the Artifacts library, with a scene timeline you can scrub, frame stepping, loop and full screen.
- **Motion films with sound and export**: music, narration in your text-to-speech voice with captions, and downloads as MP4 or GIF.
- **A rough cut first**: the agent lays a film out as labeled boxes for you to confirm before it draws it, and checks every scene before it calls the film done.
- **Share and comment on Motion films**, with comments pinned to a moment on the timeline.
- **Motion films on the iPhone and Android**, with a Films filter in the Artifacts library and export to the share sheet.
- **Thumbnails in the Artifacts library**: it opens on a grid of pictures of each page, document, design, deck, dashboard and film.
- **A notes panel in conference calls on the Mac**, with the transcript so far, Ask AI and your own notes and bookmarks (⌘B), and calls you start now take notes and record from the start by default.
- **Open notes on the iPhone's call**, where Ask AI summarizes the call and answers questions with the iPhone's own model.
- **New call controls on the iPhone and Android**: one row of five named buttons, an Audio sheet to pick the phone, speaker or headset, and a Camera sheet to flip, mirror or hide your picture.
- **Profile photos in conference calls**, on tiles, in People, chat and captions, and while a friend call rings.
- **Friend calls on Android**: call a friend or teammate from the app, and your phone rings when they call you, also with the app in the background.
- **Agents message and manage your friends**: ask Mavis or any agent to message, call or send files to a friend, or to read a Friend Chat or answer a friend request, and approve each one.
- **Run in the cloud for iPhone chats**: replies on Enconvo Cloud models keep going after you leave the app or lock the phone.
- **Sign in with Apple on the iPhone**, and link or unlink it in Settings › Account.
- **Team workflows in Projects**: Standard plans each goal, builds it and reviews it, and you pick who plans, who builds and who reviews.
- **Ideas work toward their goal**: the lead plans from an idea's goal, Where it stands shows how far each part has come, and ideas get kinds such as Feature or Bug.
- **A Steps view in the workflow editor** that lists a workflow's steps top to bottom, with an Add menu between them.
- **Sandbox for a Project's Lead chat**, and the Sandbox switch in the Smart Bar, the Dynamic Island and the App Sidebar.
- **Clipboard History, Bookmark Search and File Search as native Mac lists** that open faster, in a window of their own by default, with an alias for Smart Bar search.
- **A new Dictation pill**: a smaller pill with the live transcript above it opens into a card to switch the microphone, start a Draft or pick a Clean up option, and shows Clean up at work after you stop.
- **Draft writes as you watch** in a panel above the Dictation capsule, refines with the Dictation key alone, continues your text, and lets you edit its prompt.
- **GPT Image 2.6 Flare and Sunburst** in Image Create, through OpenAI, your ChatGPT sign-in, Enconvo Cloud, PipeLLM and OpenRouter.
- **Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash in Video Create**, with videos from 3 to 15 seconds up to 4K.
- **Agents press keys on phone menus**: calls an agent places to other people get through automated phone menus.
- **The Chat Window, Settings and phone chats open faster**.

## Motion Films and Artifacts

- Added Motion films: ask for a short animated video and it plays in the panel and the Artifacts library, with a timeline of its scenes you can scrub, frame stepping, a scene list, loop and full screen.
- Added Export to Motion films: download a film as an MP4 video or a GIF from the panel, and the agent can take stills of any moment to check how its film looks.
- Added camera moves, a cursor that glides and clicks, hand-drawn marks, caption chips, word-by-word text, a spotlight, light effects and video clips to Motion films.
- Added a check the agent runs before it calls a Motion film done: it plays every scene out of sight and catches scenes that fail or draw a moment differently when played back.
- Added sound to Motion films: music the agent writes plays with the picture, each scene can be narrated in your text-to-speech voice with captions you can turn off, and MP4 exports carry the sound with captions saved beside the video or drawn into it.
- Added a rough cut to Motion films: the agent first lays a film out as labeled boxes for you to confirm before drawing it, picks from a few named looks, and can set its words in bundled typefaces that look the same in the panel, stills and exports.

- Fixed a narrated Motion film's captions file going out of step with the picture after scenes were moved or removed.

- Fixed a design, deck or Motion film page that never finishes drawing leaving a background process running at full speed after a thumbnail, still or check gave up on it; the process now really ends, and a film's check names the scene that hangs.
- Fixed the Artifacts library's filter tabs scrolling sideways at the panel's width, which hid Films and the kinds after it; the tabs now wrap onto a second line.
- Fixed shared documents never getting a thumbnail in the Artifacts library; one whose sharing service can't be reached is drawn once it answers.
- Added a behind-its-source note to a design's Theme tab and a deck's Design system menu when the design system it uses has changed since, with a Sync button that brings it up to date.
- Changed a design to open with its Properties panel closed every time, so the canvas has the room, instead of remembering it open from the last time.
