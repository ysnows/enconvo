# Enconvo 2.5.13 Beta 4

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.13 Beta 4. This build turns a portrait into a Live Photo character, keeps API keys and sign-ins encrypted and out of the conversation with a Connect card and website and app logins, adds goals to agent chats, and brings Quick access and a device switcher to the Chat Window sidebar.

- Added Live Photo characters to Settings › Avatar: open the Live Photo tab and choose Create to turn a portrait, or one made from a description you type, into an Avatar character that stands and breathes on your desktop in a rounded frame with the photo's own background, or cut out if you choose Remove under Backdrop, and appears as live video in calls.
- Fixed Live Talk calls taking about ten seconds longer to connect when Fish Audio can't be reached from your network.
- Changed Full access so it no longer asks before an agent calls or messages your friends and teammates: it now asks only before changes that could harm your computer.
- Changed the App Sidebar handle to show and hide the moment the pointer reaches or leaves a window's right edge, without a delay or a fade.
- Fixed choosing an item in the App Sidebar handle's right-click menu taking focus away from the app you were working in.
- Changed Your iPhone's setup to guide you through each setting on this Mac step by step, showing where to click and opening the right place, instead of clicking through System Settings for you.
- Changed an agent's call to a friend so the agent greets them within a few seconds of their answer, instead of after about ten.
- Fixed the person an agent called seeing an empty conference for a moment when the agent hung up; the call now just ends.
- Fixed an agent calling a friend losing its greeting when the friend spoke over it, and letting talk near the friend keep a silent call going.
- Fixed the iPhone's incoming call screen staying up for good when Answer couldn't connect after the caller had already hung up; it now shows the call as missed.
- Fixed an agent's call to a friend going quiet for the rest of the call when its voice provider stopped responding.
- Fixed an agent's call to a friend losing its report in the chat, or leaving the friend's last words unanswered, when Enconvo restarted during the call.
- Fixed an agent's call report showing a friend's declined call as unanswered, and a card number the friend said now never reaches the chat.
- Fixed Android's Enconvo call ring screen staying up after a lost hang-up, Decline doing nothing after the app was closed, and an ended call ringing again from a late notification.
- Changed an agent's calls so they reach only your friends and teammates, never people you only met on a call.
- Fixed a friend call's steps in the chat: a call that couldn't be placed no longer looks done, and the transcript now shows what people near your friend said, marked as not said to the agent.
- Fixed an agent's calls to friends recording the call while Settings › Agent Phone › Record calls was off; with it off, the agent no longer says the call is recorded.
- Fixed scheduled tasks below Full access messaging or calling friends and teammates they weren't set up to reach.
- Fixed a friend still being rung after the chat that placed the call was stopped, and a declined friend call showing as missed when the connection dropped while it rang.
- Changed an agent's calls to friends to keep no recording when the person called says they don't want the call recorded.
- Fixed an open Space not showing a change you asked for in its chat until you opened it again: the Space beside the chat and its own page now update within a few seconds of the agent's change.
- Fixed an SSH host you just added missing from the composer's @ menu for a few seconds: the @ menu now lists it right away.
- Fixed the App Sidebar handle on the Finder desktop: it now appears there with or without Finder windows open, follows switches between Finder windows, and stays on screen after Show Desktop.
- Fixed the App Sidebar handle and its preview staying on screen over Quick Look, Finder dialogs, or a minimized, hidden, or closed window, and kept the first-use tip from covering the handle on narrow windows.
- Fixed calls from your iPhone being tried while notifications for Phone or FaceTime are off, which kept macOS from showing the call: Agent Phone now says so and offers the setup guide, and calls go through your phone provider meanwhile.
- Fixed the Live Talk voice menu listing an old or empty set of xAI voices when Speak uses xAI on Enconvo Cloud: it now lists the voices that provider offers, as its Settings page does.
- Fixed a chat started with an @ SSH host or computer showing "$name" in its title: the title now reads the machine by its plain name, as its chip does.
- Added free limit resets to Claude and ChatGPT subscription usage in Settings: the card shows how many resets Claude or Codex granted you and until when, and Use reset refills your limits after you confirm.
- Fixed a command an agent ran on an SSH host or another computer being summed up as "Used a tool" in the chat: the line now says "Ran a command", and each step still names the machine.
- Added a way to quiet Live Talk and Voice Command: tell her to be quiet or to stop talking for a while and she answers nothing and holds her news until you call her by name, say she can talk again, type to her, or click the QUIET status.
- Fixed Read Aloud waiting several seconds per sentence before reporting that the voice's provider has no API key: it now stops at the first sentence and says so.
- Changed sending a message while a task runs: Return now steers the task at its next step, and ⌘Return queues the message for when it finishes.
- Changed Esc to stop a running task on its second press, so one stray press no longer ends a long task.
- Fixed a message sent while a task runs appearing several times when the app was slow to respond.
- Changed a message typed in the Smart Bar or Dynamic Island while a task runs to show right away.
- Added Quick access to the Chat Window sidebar: pin pages, Projects and links from the Settings menu's Sidebar Shortcuts, then drag to reorder, rename or remove them.
- Changed how saved API keys and sign-ins are kept on the Mac: each is now encrypted with a key that stays in this Mac's Keychain, so a copy of Enconvo's settings folder can no longer be read on another computer, and keys are entered again after going back to an older version.
- Changed who can read saved API keys and sign-ins on the Mac: another program that asks Enconvo's local API for settings now gets `[hidden]` in place of each key, and a plugin from the store can use only its own keys and the credential picked in its settings, while Settings shows and checks keys as before.
- Changed when saved API keys and sign-ins are opened on the Mac: only code that Enconvo itself installed and that is still unchanged gets them, so a program that rewrites one of Enconvo's built-in extensions or its runtime in Enconvo's settings folder gets no key that way, while the app and everything that needs no saved key keep working.
- Changed who can change where a saved API key is sent on the Mac: only Enconvo's own windows can change a provider's or credential's address, or make an MCP server send a saved key or connect somewhere else with one, while another program using Enconvo's local API, the phone and the agent are refused; the phone's Connect card still connects a Connector with an API key, to the server the Connector declares.
- Fixed a setting or a refreshed sign-in sometimes being lost when two tasks saved the same provider's settings at the same moment.
- Changed the Dynamic Island so two or more tasks fold into one Tasks chip, which no longer shows a spinner of its own while its list is open.
- Changed Account and Settings in the Chat Window sidebar to open the Settings window again, instead of covering the Chat Window.
- Changed the composer's response style to be picked only from the + menu's Use style, which now also names the Intelligent UI level; the chip beside the other controls is gone.
- Added the Make something row (Slides, Doc, Design, Film, Dashboard, Web page, Image, Video) under a new chat's composer inside a Project.
- Changed the Chat Window side panel to be open or closed per chat: opening it in one chat no longer opens it in the others, and each chat comes back the way you left it.
- Added a way for the agent to ask for an API key in the chat: when a task needs a service's key, a Connect card asks for it, the key goes to Credentials instead of the conversation, and later chats use it without asking again.
- Added support for services Enconvo has no provider for: the Connect card shows the host a key will be sent to, saves it under Other services on the Credentials page, and the agent can use it only in requests to that host.
- Changed how the agent uses saved keys: it names a credential instead of holding the key, and the key is replaced with [redacted] wherever a request or command returns it.
- Changed how paired computers show in the Chat Window: the sidebar now lists one computer at a time, and a device switcher at its foot switches between this computer and the paired ones, shows what is waiting on the others, and opens any of them in its own window.
- Added Add a device to the sidebar's device switcher: pair a computer, add an SSH host or pair a phone from there, also before anything is paired.
- Changed Settings and the help menu at the foot of the Chat Window sidebar into one menu.
- Added goals to agent chats: type `/goal` and what should be true when the work is done, or choose Set a goal from the composer's + menu, and the agent keeps working turn after turn until a check finds the goal met; the goal shows above the composer with its running time, where you can edit, clear, or resume it.
- Added website logins: when a browser task reaches a sign-in page, a card asks for the user name and password once, Enconvo fills them in on that site only, and the agent never sees the password; saved logins appear under Logins on the Credentials page, and unticking Remember this login uses one for that sign-in only.
- Added typing a saved login into a Mac app: when an app's sign-in needs a password, a card that names the app asks for the user name and password, and Enconvo types them into that app only, the password only into a field that hides what is typed, without the agent ever seeing it.
- Added taking a key out of the chat: when you paste an API key into a message anyway, Enconvo saves it in Credentials and replaces it with [redacted] in the saved chat, so later requests and your other devices no longer carry it; a new key you paste for a service that already has one replaces the saved key.
- Added the Connect card to phone chats: when the agent needs a key during a chat on your iPhone, the card shows there and the key is saved in Credentials on your Mac; in an IM chat the agent never asks for a key and sends a link that opens the chat in Enconvo instead.
- Fixed Enconvo keeping a copy of every AI request, with your messages and the system prompt, in its log folder on your Mac: these request logs are no longer written, and the copies earlier versions left are deleted.
- Fixed the Chat Window writing the start of what you typed into a diagnostics log each time you pressed Return.
- Fixed the App Sidebar showing an empty page beside an app installed while Enconvo was running, such as a browser copied in from a download: it now shows the app, its Conversation starters and, beside a browser, the page card.
- Fixed App Sidebar's key moments for a YouTube video whose caption track runs past the video's end: the transcript now stops at the video's length, so the timestamps stay within the video.
- Fixed Finder file jobs in App Sidebar answering without file cards when the files tool wasn't available: the answer now shows each new copy as a file card.
- Fixed times in an App Sidebar timeline about a video so each one jumps the video to that moment, as a cited time in the answer does.
- Fixed Dictation saying "No text field found" after the text was pasted into a web page or a remote screen: Dictation now pastes wherever the focus is, without the notice.
- Added selecting several messages in a Friend Chat: choose Select from a message's menu or Select messages from the chat's menu, click or Shift-click to pick, then copy, forward, delete them together, or start a new agent chat with them in the Project you pick, with a message telling the agent what to do.
- Added Gemini 4 Carbon: choose it in Enconvo Cloud (at its launch price, half the standard rate through November 30), with your own Gemini API key on a paid tier, or in Google Antigravity on a Google AI Ultra plan. Its thinking levels are Low, Medium (default) and High.
- Added ElevenLabs Scribe v3 and Scribe v3 Live: choose them for Dictation, Voice Input and file transcription with your ElevenLabs API key or Enconvo Cloud. Scribe v3 Live shows the first words in under a second, about twice as fast as v2 Realtime, with fewer errors in Chinese and noisy audio; Chinese and Japanese come out without spaces between words.
- Live Talk on the Mac, iPhone and Android now hears you with ElevenLabs Scribe v3 Live when it uses ElevenLabs: your words show about a second sooner, Chinese in a noisy room comes through more accurately, and Korean keeps the spaces between its words.
- Fixed Create Knowledge Base in the menu bar menu opening the Knowledge Base page without its New Knowledge Base dialog: the dialog now opens over the knowledge base you last had open.
- Fixed the Create Agent dialog showing its field names, hints and heading in English when Enconvo is set to another language.
- Fixed the new chat heading ("What can I help with?") showing in English when the app language is set to another language.
- AI Commands now run on Enconvo Fast by default (Settings › AI Commands › All Commands): writing tools, the PopBar, and Dictation's Polish and Translate use it unless you picked another model, and Use Recommended switches back to it.
- Fixed settings such as Model Name, Reasoning Effort, the provider fields, and the Smart Bar, PopBar, Dynamic Island, Pet, Dictation and Voice Command options showing in English when the app language is set to another language.
- Fixed the "Thinking…" and "Running" status shown while an answer starts showing in English when the app language is set to another language.
- Fixed a message sent from the iPhone or Android app coming back into the message box after it was sent, when its reply was still running or the app reconnected to the chat.
- Added your friends and teammates to the composer's @ menu: name someone in a chat and the agent knows whom you mean, so it can message them privately, read their replies, or call them when you ask.
- Fixed a Board task failing when the model's reply broke off partway on a closed connection; it now runs again by itself after a pause, as it does after other passing model errors.
- Fixed an agent messaging or calling someone whose address only looked like the one it was given (eve@example.com.au for eve@example.com): a full address now reaches only that exact person.
- Fixed a reply showing again below a message steered into a running task, after the iPhone or Android app opened the chat again or came back to it.
- Added chats that need you to the Dock icon's badge: a chat with a result or a failure you haven't opened yet, or one waiting for your approval or answer, now counts there, alongside unread messages from friends.
- The iPhone and Android apps now steer a running task with what you send, as Return does in the Mac chat, instead of queuing it; the box reads “Steer the running task…” while a reply runs.
- Changed the side panel of a chat that never opened it to start on a New tab instead of Files; the first thing opened there takes its place.
- Changed Video Conference in the Chat Window sidebar to scroll with Plugins and the other pages; only New chat and Search stay fixed above the list.
- Fixed the Chat Window sidebar still offering Log in after signing in on the website: a card above the device now follows the sign-in from the browser until your account loads, says when the network is slow, and offers Retry if the account doesn't load, while the Settings menu shows the sign-in finishing instead of Log in.
- Added the time so far beside “Thinking…” while a model thinks, and fixed OpenAI models showing “Thought for 1s” while they were still thinking, with the parts of their reasoning run together on one line: the parts now make one thinking step, each in its own paragraph, timed from start to finish.
- Removed the limits on how many calls your agent places and how often: calls to other people no longer wait 2 minutes apart or stop at 10 a day, calls to you no longer turn into a notification after 6 an hour or 20 a day, and calls to friends are no longer held to 10 a day on your Mac.
- Changed the Chat Window sidebar to leave out the chats your agent starts when it asks Codex, Claude Code or another agent for help: open one from its line in the chat that asked, and it still gets a row while it is open, pinned, or waiting for your answer.
- Removed the remaining limits on calls and voice: calls your agent places no longer end after 10 minutes or hang up after 2 quiet minutes, a friend can be rung again right after declining, and Live Talk no longer caps a sentence at 2,000 characters or 60 seconds of audio; Enconvo Cloud's realtime voices no longer close a connection at 30 minutes or 20,000 characters.
- Fixed the chat box keeping only the first character of text another app typed into it all at once, such as an automation or a text expander, when the box was empty or all its text was selected.
- Fixed the note on a call that came as a notification to say why: your other call ran long.
- Added each model's own limit to the model picker's account card: a Claude subscription now shows what is left of the weekly Fable limit beside the 5-hour and weekly ones.
- Changed the summary an agent gives when a task ends: it now opens with the outcome, then lists what changed, what was checked and what needs your attention, without the steps that led there, and draws the checks and changes as views as far as the chat's Intelligent UI level says.
- Added the weekly Fable limit to the native model picker's account card on macOS: a Claude subscription shows it beside the 5-hour and weekly limits, as any model's own limit does.
- Improved the native model picker on macOS: a click on a row lands on the first press even when the picker's panel isn't the key window, it opens with the providers, sign-ins and usage it last showed instead of loading them again, the account card keeps its height while usage is read so the models under it don't move, and an arrow key or a pick redraws only the rows it changes.
- Added tags to the models in the Chat Window's model picker: a Cloud model can show New (for its first 14 days), a discount such as 50% OFF, Free, Preview or the date it leaves, with the price behind a discount when you point at the row or reach it with the arrow keys. Enconvo Cloud decides the tags, so they change without an app update.
- Fixed reconnecting a Connector whose sign-in had expired: the Connect card in a chat and the Plugins page now show it connected as soon as you allow access, instead of waiting until the wait runs out.
- Removed the "Running outside the sandbox" and "Ran outside the sandbox" lines under a command in a chat: a command you allowed to run outside the Sandbox now shows like any other.
- Improved editing an earlier message in a chat: its images and files come back into the message box with its text, so you can remove one or add another before sending, and Cancel takes them out again.
- Fixed pasting from Clipboard History when it was opened over one of Enconvo's own windows: Return now pastes into the Chat Window's message box instead of sometimes closing the Chat Window and pasting nothing.
- Changed Clipboard History's Copy shortcut from Command-Return to Command-C. Command-C still copies the text you selected in the search field or in an item's preview.
- Removed the Tasks chip (such as "Tasks 6 · Failed") from above the message box while a chat is open in the Dynamic Island. Home without a chat open and the notch still show your tasks.
- Fixed Enconvo ignoring the system proxy with Use System Network Proxy Preferences on: model requests, web fetches, Connectors and the other requests its engine makes now go through the proxy, so Claude models no longer answer "not available in your country or region" when your proxy is in a supported country. A proxy you turn on, change or turn off while Enconvo runs now takes effect without restarting it. Servers on this Mac and your local network, such as LM Studio and Ollama, are still reached directly.
- Fixed a hotkey saved with short modifier names, such as "ctrl+alt+cmd+t" from the Local API or an Agent, registering its bare key: every T typed in any app opened the command. Short names (cmd, ctrl, alt, opt, fn) and the modifier symbols are now read as modifiers, a hotkey with a word Enconvo does not know is not registered, and a key that types is never registered as a hotkey without Command, Control, Option or Function.
