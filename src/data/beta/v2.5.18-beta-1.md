# Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 1

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 1. This build brings direct connections and one chat list across your devices, Mail Triage and mail alert boards in Email, a chat for each automation, Enconvo Guide with settings you can change in chat, and Projects whose work starts as their Permission mode says.

## Highlights

- **Paired computers connect directly on the same network**, so their screens open in about a second and a paired Mac's screen comes at up to 60 frames a second.
- **All devices**: one chat list with chats from this Mac, your phones, Enconvo Cloud and your paired computers, and Continue on to carry a chat to another computer.
- **Mail Triage in Email**: every new mail gets an urgency and a topic, with inbox filters, search and sorting rules built on them.
- **Mail alert boards**: a pinned Mail Alerts chat with a board for every alert, where you read, reply, snooze or mark mail handled on the Mac and on your phone.
- **A chat for each automation**, where every run's result arrives as a card with View run, and Retry for a failed run.
- **Enconvo Guide**: a built-in agent that answers from the live docs, shows settings right in chat and walks you through features on your own screen.
- **Lower CPU use** while several agents answer at once.

## Devices and Paired Computers

- Changed a paired computer to connect directly over your local network when both computers are on the same one, instead of through the relay, so its chats, files and Terminal respond faster; away from that network it uses the relay as before.
- Changed a paired computer's screen to open in about a second when both computers are on the same network, instead of waiting several seconds for the connection to be set up.
- Changed a paired Mac's screen to come at up to 60 frames a second and at your window's full resolution when both computers are on the same network, with text sharpening when the screen stops moving.
- Added a connection capsule to a paired computer's screen that always shows whether it is Direct or Relay and the latency; click it for the path, frame rate, resolution and bitrate.
- Added an inactivity timeout to Mac Screen and remote screens when they connect through a relay: after 10 minutes without use the viewer asks whether you are still watching, and after 20 minutes it disconnects; on the same Wi-Fi nothing is timed.
- Changed switching to a paired computer in the Chat Window to show its chats and Projects at once from a copy kept on this computer and update them in the background, with the connection's path (local network or relay) under its name; Settings › Connections has a new Keep lists for instant opening switch, and the copy is deleted when you unpair.
- Changed a paired computer's own window on the Mac to be a second Chat Window: Open in its own window now opens a window with the Chat Window's own sidebar, search and look that stays on that computer, and its Dock icon, Keep in Dock and shortcut work as before.
- Changed the primary computer to follow your account, so every phone and computer sends new chats to the same one, with Enconvo Cloud as primary when your account has no computer.
- Added a Devices list on the Mac, iPhone and Android showing every device on your account, whether it is online and which computer is primary, with Make primary.
- Added an All devices view to the Chat Window sidebar, and one chat list on iPhone and Android, with chats from this device, your phones, Enconvo Cloud and your paired computers, each marked with where it runs.
- Added Continue on to a chat's menu on the Mac to carry the chat to another of your computers, and Continue on Computer for Cloud chats on iPhone.
- Added live following of a chat's reply on every device, whichever device started it.
- Added a Projects limit per paired phone or computer, so it sees only the Projects you choose.
- Added a Tools permission per paired device, off by default, that lets it take a screenshot, read a file, list a folder or find files on this computer.
- Added approvals for a task or subagent handed to another computer to the computer that started it, where either computer can answer.
- Added a notification in the chat that handed a task to another computer when that task finishes, with its result.
- Added an outbox for a computer that is offline: tasks and messages wait, send when it is back, and can be cancelled.
- Fixed clicking a notification from a paired computer so it opens that chat on the computer.

## Email

- Added Mail Triage to Email: every new mail gets an urgency (Urgent, Important, Normal or Low) and a topic such as Work, Bills & Payments or Security, and only the mail that needs you goes on to a model.
- Added urgency and topic to the Email inbox: a red Urgent tag, an amber dot for important mail, the topic after the sender, and a ✦ line saying what the mail wants of you.
- Added inbox filters for Urgent, Important and Needs Reply, and a Topics menu with how many conversations each topic holds.
- Added a way to change a mail's urgency or topic from the reader; Mail Triage keeps your choice and learns from it.
- Added sorting rules on urgency and topic, and `level:` and `topic:` in Email search for you and your agents.
- Added Gmail sign-in with Google to Email: when adding a Gmail address you can now sign in with Google or use an app password, and every account signed in to the Gmail plugin shows up in Email by itself and leaves when you disconnect it there.
- Added Apple Mail to Email: once Enconvo may control Mail, every account in Mail shows up in Email by itself, read through Mail while it is open, and accounts like iCloud and Gmail can switch to a direct connection from Accounts.
- Changed Gmail and Apple Mail mailboxes in Email to stay, with their mail and settings, when a Google sign-in runs out, an account is turned off in Mail or Mail access is taken back: they say what to fix and leave Email only when you disconnect them in their plugin.
- Changed the Gmail and Apple Mail plugin pages to show that their accounts appear in Email, with Open Email, and to say before disconnecting that the mailbox leaves Email too.
- Changed agents to stop listing Gmail and Apple Mail separately for accounts that are already mailboxes in Email.
- Added marking mail read or unread and archiving to what agents can do in Email.
- Fixed agents reading only a connected Gmail account when asked about your mail: they now read every mailbox you added to Email first.
- Fixed Email summaries that sometimes answered the mail instead of summarizing it, and warnings that showed on safe mail just to say nothing was wrong.
- Fixed Email Today counting newsletters as notifications in Later, so newsletters now show as newsletters with Review Senders.
- Fixed Continue in Chat from Ask Mavis in Email, and other messages a Space sends to a new chat, flashing the empty Chats home before the chat opened.

## Mail Alerts

- Changed Mail Alerts into part of Email: Email › Automation › Alerts now decides which mail alerts you, for every mailbox in one list, with where alerts appear, what matters to you, senders to always or never alert about, and quiet hours, and your Mail Alerts settings move over the first time you open it.
- Added Enconvo Cloud's free judging to Email's Alerts, so mail is judged without a model of your own unless you pick one.
- Added a Mail Triage only choice under Judged by in Email Alerts, so no model reads your mail.
- Changed Email alerts to show one line on what the mail wants of you, such as what to do and by when, instead of the summary.
- Changed Email alerts to go out as soon as new mail is sorted, instead of waiting for a two-minute check, with a check every five minutes as a backstop and one catch-up board for mail that came while the Mac was asleep.
- Added a board in the Mail Alerts chat for every mail alert, showing each mail's urgency, topic, deadline, whether it needs a reply and what it wants, with Read, Reply, Draft reply, Snooze and Handled right on the board, on the Mac and on your phone; a deadline the mail gives as a span or a time of day, such as within 30 minutes or before the end of the workday, shows as the time it falls due.
- Added a Dynamic Island card for mail alerts that shows the sender and what the mail wants, with View, Draft reply and Later, and Why this alert in its menu.
- Changed mail alerts so new mail in the same thread updates its alert quietly for ten minutes, four or more at once arrive as one alert, and phones wait three minutes while you're at the Mac unless the mail is urgent.
- Changed mail alerts so a mail handled on one device, or read, archived or answered in Email, is taken off the other devices and folds on its board, and a board whose mail is all done folds into one line saying when it alerted and what became of its mail, such as 2 urgent · 1 replied · 1 handled.
- Added Why this alert to each mail on a board, with every step that led to the alert and a way to say it wasn't important or to stop alerts from the sender.
- Added a pinned Mail Alerts chat that shows how many alerts are waiting, in red for urgent mail.
- Added Alert settings, Mark all as handled and Clear history to the Mail Alerts chat's right-click menu.
- Removed the Important mail row from Automations and the Mail Alerts question after connecting Gmail, since alerts are set in Email now.

## Automations

- Added a fixed chat for each automation, where every run's result arrives as a card showing which run it was, how it started, how long it took and how it went, with View run, and Retry for a failed run.
- Changed the chat list to show each automation's own chat instead of its runs, marked with a blue dot for a new result and a red mark when the last run failed.
- Changed older result cards to fold into one line each, and cards from more than a week ago into one line per day.
- Added a Chat button to each automation in Scheduled, and a choice to send an automation's results back to the chat it was set up in.
- Added an Email automation row to Scheduled that shows Email's alerts with their own switch, opens Email's Automation panel in place and doesn't count toward your plan's automations.
- Changed phone notifications for an automation's result to open its chat at that result.
- Changed Mac notifications for mail alerts and automation results to open their chat at that board or result.
- Changed the Mail Alerts chat so Mavis knows the mail on its boards from the last day, and an automation's chat so Mavis knows its last three results.
- Changed Today in Scheduled to leave out Email's new-mail alerts.

## Help and Settings

- Added Enconvo Guide, a built-in agent for questions about Enconvo itself: it answers from the live docs, shows settings in chat and starts on-screen guides.
- Added settings in chat: ask how to change something in Enconvo and the answer shows the setting's own control, which you can change right there or open in Settings with the row highlighted.
- Added proposed setting changes in chat: when you ask Enconvo to change a setting, it lists the change with the old and new value, and nothing changes until you press Apply; Undo puts it back.
- Added pictures and videos from the Enconvo docs to help answers, read from the live docs so they stay current.
- Added guides that walk you through Dynamic Island, Screenshot OCR, Dictation, PopBar and SmartBar on your own screen, one step at a time, started from a help answer's Show me button.
- Fixed Settings › General › Updates staying on Checking… after a check had finished, and not showing a new version after the update channel was changed, until Settings was opened again; the Update buttons in the Chat Window and the Dynamic Island now both light up for the same update.
- Fixed Settings › Permissions › Used by leaving a feature out under its second permission: Computer Use and Quick Capture now show under Screen Recording as well as Accessibility.

## Projects and Agents

- Changed when a Project's plans start so it follows the Permission mode: at Full access a plan the lead proposes starts at once, at Approve for me a plan for work you asked for starts at once, and at Ask for approval every plan waits for Start.
- Changed new to-dos on a Project's Ideas board to start at once unless the Project works at Ask for approval.
- Changed plans for Agents you name with @ to wait for Start when the chat or Project works at Ask for approval.
- Changed plans for your other Agents in a chat outside a Project to start as that chat's Permission mode says, instead of always waiting for Start.
- Removed the Autonomy setting and Check in often from Project settings, the Team & settings tab and the phone apps, since the Permission mode now decides when work starts.
- Fixed plans in a Project without members waiting for Start even at Full access when you gave the work to a model by name.
- Changed a Project's lead to keep working on its own when a task reports while you're away, as far as the Project's Permission mode allows: at Full access it goes on as if you were there, including starting Subagents, and the separate Lead acts while you're away switch and its hourly and daily limits are gone.
- Added due dates to a Project's ideas, which you set and the lead can only suggest.
- Added the lead's suggested ideas, shown apart from your ideas until you adopt or decline them.
- Added a roadmap of a Project's ideas with the lead's estimated phases, dates and risk, and a way to agree to the low-risk steps at once.
- Added a Today view to a Project that lists what needs you, results to review, ideas due soon and what finished while you were away.
- Added a checklist to each Board task and a read-only look at a task's thread, and the merge gate now shows how its last pass ended.
- Changed the Board panel on a Project chat's start page to leave out a column with nothing in it: the columns that show keep their width, the panel narrows to them, and a column whose last task just left says so for a moment before it goes.
- Fixed approval cards running the reason and the setting that asked for approval together into one sentence.
- Fixed agents saving a correction of how a task was done as a Memory instead of teaching it to the Skill that covers the task, so the Learned note with Undo now shows.
- Fixed agents narrating a redone answer card in the chat after a card made of plain text was turned away.
- Fixed the agent drawing a design's artboards and a long deck's slides one after another: it now draws them at the same time, each appearing as it's ready.
- Removed the built-in Latin Teacher and Spanish Teacher agents.

## Chat Window

- Fixed high CPU use and a hot Mac while several agents answer at once: a long answer no longer costs more with every piece it adds, and its text reaches the chat in frames about 30 times a second.
- Fixed a tool call with a long input, such as writing a large file, using more CPU with every piece of the input the model wrote.
- Added Select chats to the ⋯ menus of the Chat Window sidebar's Chats and Projects sections, which shows every chat's check so several can be archived, marked as read or deleted at once, as Command-click and Shift-click do.
- Changed opening a subagent in the Chat Window: it now opens in a tab of the side panel beside its chat, which stays in view, instead of taking the chat's place; Open as chat still opens it on its own.
- Changed the Memory line in chat to read as one of the turn's quiet steps instead of a bordered chip: it says Remembered with a preview of the Memory, and opens in place to the full text, its tags and Undo.
- Changed the model picker to open, search and follow the pointer faster when many providers and models are set up: providers that answer while it opens now show together instead of redrawing the list one by one.
- Fixed Read Aloud under a chat reply reading the agent's notes folded under "Worked for" too: it now reads only the answer you see, and the sentence being read is highlighted in it.
- Fixed Customize sidebar closing right after it opened from the ··· menu or an icon's right-click menu in the Chat Window.
- Fixed Undo after archiving chats in the Chat Window sidebar moving them to the top of the list as just used: they now go back to their places, and archiving no longer changes a chat's time.

## Browser Use and Computer Use

- Changed the Picture-in-Picture window for Browser Use and Computer Use so going to the tab or app window no longer pauses the agent: it keeps working there, and Take over on the control center is what pauses it.
- Added a larger view to the Picture-in-Picture window: click its picture to enlarge it in its corner and click again to shrink it; a double-click, or the new button in its header, goes to the tab or window.
- Changed the Picture-in-Picture window so nothing covers its picture under the pointer, and added a one-time tip that shows what a click and a double-click do.

- Added Face ID, fingerprint or screen-lock confirmation on iPhone and Android before allowing an agent to delete, send, buy or run a command.
- Fixed a message sent from the iPhone or Android app while the Mac was offline sometimes running twice once the connection came back.

- Fixed a picture in Media changed by Relight, Restore, Change angle, Expand canvas or Variations showing the edit's internal instruction as its prompt, which Reuse settings and Turn into a video then used: it now keeps the picture's own prompt, subjects and words to avoid.
