# Enconvo 2.5.6 Beta 5

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.6 Beta 5. This build connects your apps to your
assistant, improves answers from your files, and makes Dictation, App Sidebar
and your desktop Avatar feel more responsive.

## Highlights

- **Connect your apps to your assistant.** New Connectors bring Gmail, Google
  Drive, Calendar, Notion, Slack, GitHub, Outlook and many more services into
  your conversations, with account controls in the redesigned Plugins page.
- **Better answers from your knowledge bases.** Find exact names, codes and
  spreadsheet rows more reliably, search inside recordings, and work with more
  document formats, including Pages, Numbers and Keynote.
- **More dependable Dictation and Voice Command.** Start speaking sooner,
  keep your words through connection interruptions, and get your original
  text even when a post-action fails.
- **App Sidebar can read the page you're on.** Ask about articles, PDFs,
  videos and Google documents, then follow citations back to the passage,
  page or video moment.
- **Make your Avatar your own.** Try and save looks and poses, ask for changes
  in your own words, and enjoy smoother movement and more expressive talking
  gestures.
- **Claude Sonnet 5.5 is available** through your own API key, your Claude
  subscription or Enconvo Cloud.

## Connectors and Plugins

- Added Connectors for Google Workspace, Microsoft services, productivity,
  developer tools, research, design and more, with sign-in directly from
  Plugins or a Connect card in chat.
- Added support for multiple Google accounts, with a default account for
  sending mail and creating events.
- Added Apple Connectors for Calendar, Reminders, Contacts, Notes, Mail and
  Messages, with access managed from their plugin pages.
- Changed agents to use the Connectors and Apple apps you have connected,
  while tools enabled by a mention stay with that conversation.
- Changed Connectors to download when first connected and update in the
  background, so new services can arrive without an app update.
- Changed Plugins to offer clearer categories, a Popular section, a Personal
  tab, example prompts and a single Add menu for plugins, skills and MCPs.
- Added in-chat discovery and installation of missing plugins, skills and MCP
  servers, with an install card for you to approve.
- Added an offer to save a useful, repeatable task as a skill after you agree.
- Added optional Computer Use and Browser Use MCP servers for connecting
  other AI apps on your Mac to Enconvo's tools.
- Added an Obsidian plugin for working with your vaults, daily notes, templates
  and the note you have open.
- Added plugin creation and validation commands, support for custom model and
  voice providers, and clearer store review and compatibility checks.
- Removed the old Gmail plugin in favor of the Gmail Connector.

## Knowledge Bases and Files

- Changed search to find exact phrases, names, codes and multilingual text
  more reliably, with useful surrounding context and fewer duplicate results.
- Changed spreadsheet and table search to keep column names with matching
  rows, improving answers and counts across large lists.
- Added support for Pages, Numbers, Keynote, saved web pages and email files,
  with better reading of Word, PowerPoint, OpenDocument and EPUB documents.
- Fixed garbled text in CSV and older document encodings, missing PDF pages,
  and unclear errors for damaged or password-protected files.
- Added transcript search to Recordings, Live Captions and Transcription,
  with matching passages highlighted in results.
- Changed the knowledge base filter to search inside content as well as titles.
- Changed answers to use the full knowledge base when it fits, count matching
  records accurately, and clearly distinguish web findings from your files.
- Improved indexing speed and memory use for large documents and scanned PDFs,
  and made large knowledge bases and linked folders more responsive.
- Fixed clearing the app cache resetting knowledge bases, memories, provider
  settings or chat history, and improved upgrades from older versions.
- Fixed offline linked folders losing indexed files, deleted items returning
  in search, and edited attachments continuing to show old content.
- Fixed reindexing recordings unnecessarily transcribing them again, and
  improved recovery of missing or outdated memory search entries.
- Added protection against overwriting external edits in the knowledge base
  Markdown editor.

## Dictation and Voice

- Changed Dictation and Voice Command to prepare the microphone and provider
  sooner, preserving the first words spoken after pressing the shortcut.
- Fixed live Dictation losing text during reconnects, microphone changes,
  pause and resume, or when stopping before the connection is ready.
- Fixed failed or empty post-actions losing your dictation; your original
  words are inserted with an explanation when processing fails.
- Fixed canceled dictations inserting late results or interfering with the
  next recording.
- Improved Dictionary and current-app context support across speech providers,
  including on-device Qwen3-ASR and Whisper.
- Improved Soniox and AssemblyAI response times and made provider errors easier
  to understand.
- Added Eleven v4 and Eleven v4 Turbo, restored ElevenLabs model and voice
  loading, and fixed supported voices ignoring the speed setting.
- Added ElevenLabs tools for dialogue, sound effects, music, voice creation,
  voice changing, isolation, alignment and dubbing.

## App Sidebar and Agents

- Added suggested first messages for the app, page or selected text you are
  working with, including a preview when hovering over the sidebar handle.
- Added removable context chips for selected text and the current window.
- Added whole-page reading with citations for articles, PDFs, YouTube and
  Bilibili subtitles, and Google Docs, Sheets and Slides.
- Added per-site controls for whether the assistant can read pages.
- Fixed long agent tasks staying on Responding when a model stops replying,
  and made Stop work reliably in Telegram and other connected chat channels.
- Fixed follow-up messages interrupting a task's live reply and visual answers
  appearing as empty cards or broken images.
- Improved video generation, cancellation and download recovery, and stopped
  charging Cloud points for supported media jobs or crawls that fail.
- Fixed Apple Mail searches freezing Enconvo or Mail on large inboxes.
- Reduced the context used by skill descriptions and improved reply-language
  handling.

## Avatar and Getting Started

- Added Looks, My Looks, a Pose Library and My Poses, with natural-language
  requests for outfits and poses and a Take a Photo action.
- Added talking-gesture controls and finger counting, with steadier feet,
  smoother hands and better movement on high-refresh displays.
- Fixed animation freezes, clipped gestures and repeated Avatar resizing
  that could make other app animations stutter.
- Added a guided setup and hands-on tour for SmartBar, Dictation, Voice
  Command, PopBar and App Sidebar, hosted by your AI character.
- Added naming your AI during setup and a Preferred Name for how it addresses
  you in conversations.
- Improved Dynamic Island transitions and app startup responsiveness.
- Fixed update-related keychain prompts and made sign-in recovery clearer.

## Account and Companion

- Added Invite Friends, with $5 in Cloud points for you and an eligible friend
  after they sign in on a device new to Enconvo.
- Added Redeem Code in Settings for trial and promotional codes.
- Expanded support for the iPhone companion, including Mac chat search, model
  and reasoning controls, context usage, mentions and Live Talk with your Mac
  Agent.
- Improved access to Mac files, images, documents and media from paired iPhone
  chats.
