# Enconvo 2.5.6 Beta 11

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.6 Beta 11. This build focuses on more reliable
syncing, more useful Memory, and smoother everyday chats and automation.

## Highlights

- **More reliable syncing across your Macs**, with faster chat and attachment
  transfers, better recovery after connection problems, and a Clear synced
  data option.
- **Memory that keeps up with you**, with more relevant recall, safer edits to
  long memories, and better handling of changing preferences and dates.
- **Save X posts to your knowledge base**, including threads and images, then
  read them as post cards and ask questions about them.
- **Smoother long conversations**, with faster streaming, code highlighting,
  and less stuttering when scrolling through replies.
- **More capable Computer Use and Browser Use**, with faster typing, better
  file uploads, and support for page dialogs and downloads.
- **More ways to make Enconvo your own**, with new Connectors, built-in and
  open source command icons, and improved Raycast-compatible extensions.
