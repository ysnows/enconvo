# Enconvo 2.5.15 Beta 2

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.15 Beta 2. This build brings agents into your calls, in Enconvo Conference and in Google Meet and Zoom, gives every call its own meeting page in Recordings, lets Live Photo characters go live on a stream with a voice of their own, and adds DeepSeek for speech, transcription and OCR, Parallel for web search, and Ask AI in the Settings search.

## Highlights

- **Agents in your calls**: bring an agent into an Enconvo Conference, or send it into a Google Meet or Zoom meeting, where it answers in the chat or out loud and asks you before doing any work.
- **A meeting page for every call**: Recordings shows each call's summary, action items with owners, what agents made, and the transcript, and you can ask questions about any meeting.
- **The Stage**: a doc or whiteboard an agent makes during a call opens beside it, and everyone on the call edits it together.
- **Live Photo characters go live**: put her on an OBS stream in one click, let her answer your Twitch, Kick or YouTube live chat, and give her a cloned or ElevenLabs voice.
- **DeepSeek for voice, transcription and OCR**: with your own API key or through Enconvo Cloud.
- **Ask AI in Settings**: ask a question in the Settings search and get cards that take you straight to the settings it names.
- **Parallel web search**: a new Web Search provider that ranks pages by the question behind each search.

## Agents in Calls

- Added agents to Enconvo Conference: bring one in from People and it joins as a visible participant, switched off until someone turns it on or @-mentions it, then answers and does work with your tools.
- Added a choice of how a conference agent answers, written in the chat by default or out loud.
- Added a choice of what a conference agent does when a guest joins: leave, stay quiet until they leave, or carry on.
- Added Always allow on a conference agent's approval card, so someone you trust can ask it for work for the rest of the call without asking you each time.
- Added conference agents to the iPhone app: bring one in from People with the same choices as on the web, and answer its approval cards with Always allow, Just this once or Not now.
- Added the Stage to Enconvo Conference: a doc or whiteboard a conference agent makes during the call is shared with everyone signed in on it and opens beside the call, so the whole call edits it together while talking.
- Added Bring my agent to my conferences in Settings › Video Conference: your agent joins the conferences you set up, and optionally ones you're invited to, switched off once you're on the call, including Enconvo conference links in your Mac's Calendar events except ones you declined.
- Added a Bring switch to each conference under Coming up on the Video Conference home, so you can bring your agent to one conference or keep it away from one.
- Added sending your agent into a Google Meet or Zoom meeting, from a chat or the Bring switch on a calendar event, where it joins as "<agent> (Enconvo)" with camera and microphone off, reads the captions and chat, answers your requests in the meeting's chat, asks you on your Mac or iPhone before doing any work, and files the meeting's notes in Recordings.

## Meetings in Recordings

- Added a meeting page to each Enconvo Conference call in Recordings, with the summary, action items with who took them, what agents made on the call, what was on screen, and the transcript.
- Added Add to Reminders to a call's action items in Recordings, which puts the item in Apple Reminders with its owner and due date.
- Added Ask this meeting to a call's page in Recordings, and let your agent answer questions about your earlier calls, saying which meeting each answer came from.
- Changed a call's notes to list each action item's owner and due date, the docs and whiteboards agents made on the call, and what agents saw on a shared screen.
- Fixed two calls in the same conference room more than 10 minutes apart being merged into one Recordings entry, which lost the first call's recording; each call now gets its own notes and its own Recordings entry, and a repeating conference keeps every time's entry instead of replacing the last one.
- Fixed a repeating conference's next time in Recordings mixing in the last time's recording, notes, voices, title or summary, and a room never getting a Recordings entry again after you deleted an earlier call's.
- Fixed your own notes from two calls in the same room close together landing in one Recordings entry; each call now keeps its own notes and its entry starts at the call's start.
- Fixed an Ask AI summary on a call landing in the Recordings entry of an earlier call in the same room; each Ask now goes to the call it was asked on, even when its answer comes back after the next call began.
- Fixed call notes sometimes ending their summary with a sentence saying the transcript was not cut.

## Live Photo Characters

- Added Go live to Live Photo characters in Settings › Avatar, with a stream panel that shows her stage, its cutout, captions and orientation switches, and OBS's stream state.
- Added a transparent stage page and a captions page for an OBS Browser source, showing a Live Photo character's live picture with her voice and what she says while she is in a call.
- Added one-click Add to OBS, which connects to OBS's WebSocket server, keeps its password with your other credentials, and creates an "Enconvo · <name>" scene with her stage and captions sized for the stream.
- Added live stream chat for Live Photo characters: she reads a Twitch, Kick or YouTube live chat and answers aloud, through her Agent, the viewers who mention her, ask with !ask, or pay, with per-viewer and overall cooldowns, blocked words and an optional approve-first queue.
- Added live mode to a Live Photo character's stream panel: start her Twitch or Kick live chat there and she says each answer on her OBS stage with her lips moving, out of a call, joining her live video only while replies come and leaving it a minute after the last one.
- Added a Voice step to Live Photo characters: clone a voice by recording in place or uploading the person's audio or video, pick an ElevenLabs voice, or follow the call voice; she then speaks with it in calls and reads replies aloud in it, and Change voice on her card changes it later.
- Added using your own Atmee account for Live Photo characters in Settings › Avatar › Live Photo: with your API key, new characters are made in your account without the daily minutes or the three-character limit, the avatars already there can be taken in, and a call that can only show her still says why.
- Changed Live Photo calls so her voice plays only from OBS while a stage shows her, unless Also play on this Mac is on.

## Models and Providers

- Added DeepSeek as a Text to Speech provider, with your own DeepSeek API key or through Enconvo Cloud, in a female (Stella) or male (Tide) voice that reads Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean and six more languages, and plays aloud while it is still being made.
- Added DeepSeek as a speech-to-text provider for Transcribe and Dictation, with your own DeepSeek API key or through Enconvo Cloud, which detects 21 languages on its own and takes recordings of any length.
- Added DeepSeek OCR as an OCR provider, with your own DeepSeek API key or through Enconvo Cloud, for images and PDF, Word and PowerPoint files, with Text or Rich layout recognition.
- Added Parallel as a Web Search provider: add your Parallel API key in Settings › Credentials › Parallel, pick Turbo, Fast, Basic or Advanced as its Search Mode, and agents search with it, passing the question behind each search so Parallel ranks pages by it.

## Settings, Chats and the Dynamic Island

- Added Ask AI to the Settings search: ask a question in the search field, press Return (or ⌘Return after a keyword), and the answer opens beside the sidebar with cards for the settings it names, which take you to each setting and turn a switch on or off in place.
- Changed Mail Alerts (important mail alerts on the Dynamic Island and your phone) to be off until you turn it on in Automations › Important mail; if you never turned it on or set it up, it is now off after updating, and Mail Alerts you turned on or set up keep working.
- Fixed the Dynamic Island sometimes staying hidden after you left a full screen app, until Enconvo was relaunched, and staying on screen over a full screen app opened while it faded out with Hide When Collapsed on.
- Fixed code and monospace blocks in an agent's cards showing near-black text on a dark panel in the dark theme (and light text on a white block): gray text and backgrounds a card writes now follow the theme.
- Fixed a new Work plan starting on its own while an earlier plan from the same chat was still running, so the same work ran twice; it now waits for Start, and when you ask the agent to change or widen the running work, its new plan stops the old one first.
