# Enconvo 2.5.6 Beta 6

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.6 Beta 6. This build improves Gmail setup and
makes the getting-started guide clearer.

## Highlights

- **Gmail is easier to connect**, including on Macs with the retired Gmail
  plugin installed.
- **A clearer getting-started experience**, with visible sign-in progress
  and a welcome from your AI, Mavis by default.

## Connectors and Getting Started

- Fixed Gmail missing from Connectors and the guide's Connect your apps step
  when the old Gmail plugin was installed.
- Changed Gmail connection to replace the retired Gmail plugin automatically.
- Added a loading state after returning from website sign-in, so the guide
  clearly shows when your account is still loading.
- Changed the guide's welcome and tour invitation to use your AI's name,
  with updated Mavis voice greetings.

## iPhone Companion

These companion improvements require an updated iPhone app; the Mac installer
does not update the iPhone app.

- Added a full-screen photo and video viewer with zoom, playback controls,
  sharing and saving.
- Added video thumbnails and downloadable file previews in iPhone chats.
- Fixed generated images not appearing in Mac conversations viewed on iPhone.
- Added Avatar outfit, pose and photo requests during Live Talk.
- Added Show Me a Few Looks, Preview Voice and Avatar actions in chats and
  voice tasks.
- Added automatic Avatar motion updates over Wi-Fi, with a Retry option
  when a download fails.
- Added a choice above the iPhone home composer to run a new chat on the
  iPhone or on a paired Mac, with the Mac's agents, files and Live Talk.
