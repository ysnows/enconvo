# Enconvo 2.5.10 Beta 2

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.10 Beta 2. This build moves Agent teams into Projects with Playbooks and worktrees, lets you mark up what the side panel shows with Doodle, lets agents call you and call others for you, adds Compare answers to any chat, and brings Kokoro voices, your own Avatar characters, a new Knowledge Base, Memory Concepts, and four more plan sign-ins on the iPhone.

## Highlights

- **Projects with members and Playbooks**: Agent teams now live in Projects. Give a Project a Lead and members, or start from a template, set its Boundary, and let tasks follow the built-in Build, Fix a bug, Review and Research Playbooks, or your own, step by step on the Board.
- **Boundaries for Connectors and knowledge bases**: a Project's Boundary now picks which Connectors, Connector accounts, MCP servers and knowledge bases its agents can use, and they are refused the rest.
- **Doodle in the side panel**: draw, circle and comment on a PDF, picture, file, artifact or web page beside the chat, and your numbered marks go with your next message.
- **Agent Phone**: when a task needs you while you're away from your Mac, your iPhone rings and you answer by voice; with Twilio, agents can also call your phone number, or call someone for you, such as a restaurant, and report back in the chat.
- **Compare answers**: send your next message to 2 to 6 agents at once and see their answers side by side, with where they agree and differ and a recommendation.
- **Worktrees for git Projects**: each chat can work in a git worktree of its own, on a new branch, an existing branch or a pull request, so chats changing code at the same time no longer overwrite each other's files.
- **Coding agents with their own options**: new Claude Code, Codex and Grok Build chats pick their permission mode, Antigravity chats pick their model and thinking level and get your plan's Claude models, Grok Build asks its questions and has its plans approved in the chat, and each agent's settings page gains its own options.
- **Claude Code with Claude Artifacts and Connectors**: Claude Code chats signed in with a Claude subscription can publish pages, designs and decks as artifacts on claude.ai and use your claude.ai Connectors, and Claude Code's settings add Claude in Chrome, Output Style and more.
- **A new Knowledge Base**: it opens in the Chat Window with Ask, a table view and Library views, and opens in under a second with thousands of items.
- **Memory Concepts and Map**: Memory files your Memories under the people, projects, places and topics they are about, each with an overview, and a Map view shows your Memories and knowledge base items by how they relate.
- **Kokoro voices**: free, offline voices in English, Mandarin and Japanese for reading aloud and Live Talk, on Macs with Apple silicon and on iPhone.
- **Your own Avatar characters**: create a plush character in Settings › Avatar, describe one and let your AI model draft it, or add characters from the OpenAvatar gallery and from files.
- **Move a chat between windows**: Open in Chat Window in the Smart Bar and the Dynamic Island, and Continue in Smart Bar in the Chat Window (⇧⌘O), move the chat with its running reply and unsent text; the Chat Window also takes on the Smart Bar's translucent look.
- **Faster, steadier Computer Use**: agents can swipe as on a trackpad, scroll in about half the time and hear clearly whether anything moved, with dozens of fixes for clicks, keys, checkboxes and sliders.
- **Agents read more of your files**: they read and edit text saved in older encodings such as GBK, Big5 and Shift_JIS, and a search of file contents looks inside PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, EPUB, Pages, Numbers and Keynote files.
- **Twilio, Telnyx and Bland Connectors**: let agents send SMS and WhatsApp messages, place calls, manage phone numbers and AI voice agents, and check call logs and usage in your accounts.
- **Pollinations images**: generate and edit images with FLUX, GPT Image, Seedream, Nano Banana and more on your own Pollinations key, with tags showing which models run on free Pollen.
- **Fewer freezes on the Mac**: Enconvo no longer stops for seconds when a slow app launches or moves its windows, when you select text in a busy app, at launch, or when your microphone switches, and no longer quits when you start Dictation with AirPods.
- **Sign in to Antigravity on the iPhone**: Phone chats can use your Antigravity plan's Gemini and Claude models with no API key, each with its thinking levels as Reasoning effort.
- **Sign in to Mistral on the iPhone**: Phone chats can use your Le Chat plan's models with your Mistral account, with no key to paste.
- **Sign in to Grok on the iPhone**: Phone chats can use your Grok plan's models with no xAI API key.
- **Sign in to GitHub Copilot on the iPhone**: Phone chats can use the models your Copilot plan offers, signed in with a code on GitHub.
- **Projects on the iPhone**: a Project's Lead chat shows the plan with Start and Drop, its progress, and what members wait on, answered right there with Allow in this Project, and a Live Activity shows a Project at work on the Lock Screen.
- **On-device dictation on the iPhone**: Nemotron 3.5 Multilingual and SenseVoice Small turn your speech into text on the iPhone, free and private, in Dictation and in Live Talk.
- **Any file from the iPhone**: drop photos and files on a chat from an app beside Enconvo, and a Mac chat takes files of any size, sent a few MB at a time and picked up again after a dropped connection.
- **iPhone Duo layouts**: on the outer display and the open inner display, chats, docs, designs, sources and files sit side by side, and Live Talk gets layouts of its own.

- Changed each Agent team without a Project to get a Project of its own, a new folder in Documents named after the team, with the team's Boundary moved into the Project's.
- Added Project settings, opened from a Project's menu or the gear on its Board: one place for the Project's name and purpose, its Lead and members with their roles (start from a template, or remove them all), its Board's branch, checks and finishing, its Request budget and Permission mode, its Boundary and its instructions.
- Added Project templates to New Project: start from Research Desk, Content Studio, App Builders or Trip Planner to fill in the Project's purpose and members, and its Lead chat opens once it is made.
- Changed a chat's @ menu to list the Projects that have members, in a Projects group, so you can hand the next message to a Project's Lead.
- Changed the Lead chat, its Board and Automations to speak of a Project, its members and its Lead instead of an Agent team, from the composer and the members card to approvals, budget notes and Stop all tasks.
- Changed what agents and Enconvo's notices say about Projects: a request handed to a Project, the end of its Work plan, a refused call's Boundary and the errors a Lead chat shows now speak of the Project, its members and its Lead instead of an Agent team.
- Changed renaming a Project to rename its Lead chat too, so the Board, the iPhone app and its Live Activity show the new name.
- Added questions and notes between a Project's members: a member working on a task can ask another member something and wait for the answer, which that member gives in its finished task's chat when it has one, or send another task of the plan a note that reaches it after its current step, and the Lead sees both.
- Added members' questions, answers and notes, and each Playbook step with its member, round, time and result, to a plan's Run log.
- Added Playbooks to Projects: in Project settings › Playbooks, the built-in Build, Fix a bug, Review and Research Playbooks each list their steps and parts, you choose which member fills each part (or use the suggested members), and Enconvo warns when a member would review its own work.
- Added your own Playbooks: copy a built-in to change its steps in the Workflow editor, add a saved one or import a file, export it, or remove it from a Project, and each Playbook shows how its tasks went, with how many finished on the first try and the time and points a task took.
- Added Playbooks to a Project's Board: the Lead can give a task a Playbook and adjust it for that task, the plan and the task's card show the step it is at with a progress bar, the task's details list each step with its member, rounds and time and open each member's step chat, and a retry picks up from the step that failed.
- Added Member steps and Project check steps to the Workflow editor: declare Member slots under Workflow & Inputs to make a workflow a Playbook, then add steps that the member filling a slot does, or that run the Project's check on the task's branch.
- Added Manage Playbooks to a Board task's details, which opens the Project's settings at Playbooks.
- Changed a Playbook task's list of steps to say which step wrote the answer above it, and how each review or check after it judged the work.
- Added what each review step found to a Playbook task's steps in its drawer, such as No findings or 2 findings, and the lead now reads it too.
- Added the points each step of a Playbook task spent to its steps in the drawer, and the model its member answered on when you point at a step.
- Added Save to Playbook to a started Playbook task's drawer, which keeps the lead's changes for that task in its Playbook, or in a copy added to the Project when the Playbook is built in.
- Changed a Playbook task's members to know the task's other parts and who fills them, and a member's question to the member of an earlier part to go on in that part's chat, so it answers with its whole step in mind.
- Changed Board cards with more than two actions to show two and keep the rest, such as Skip and Send back, under a More button, and a failed task's card now gives two lines to why it failed.
- Fixed the Board's toolbar cutting off its running count in a narrower window: it keeps the dots and shows the count in their tooltip.
- Fixed the claims check holding a member's result for the Lead when its answer only described the member's own work, when the check had found a record for the claim, when the claim came from another task's result, or when the member declined to say another member approved the work.
- Added ports of its own to each Task worktree of a Project's Board: its prep and check commands and its agent's commands run with PORT and a database key of their own, so two tasks no longer fight over the Project's dev server port, and the task drawer shows its range.
- Added Projects at work to the Dynamic Island: a Project whose members are working is one row and one chip with its members' faces, how many tasks are done and one status, it turns amber when a member needs your OK or an answer, and clicking it answers that member's question in place.
- Added Allow in this Project to a member's approval on the Dynamic Island, so one answer lets every member of the plan run the same action without asking again.

## Boundaries

- Added a Boundary to each Project, in Project settings: the agents of the Project's chats and its members keep to its folders and Mac control.
- Added Connectors and MCP servers to a Project's Boundary: pick the ones its agents can use, and the agents are never offered the rest and are refused when they reach for one, with an MCP server you added that runs on your Mac also needing Run code.
- Added Connector accounts to a Project's Boundary: for a Connector with several accounts, pick the ones its agents can use and the default among them, and a call with any other account is refused.
- Added knowledge bases to a Project's Boundary: pick the ones its agents can search and change, and a lookup in any other, or across every knowledge base, is refused; Memory stays available to every agent.
- Added the Boundary to a Project's Lead chat header: once you narrow it, the Lead chat and its Board show what the Boundary keeps and whether it is sealed, with Edit Boundary one click away, and a plan names the Boundary it runs inside.
- Added what Enconvo can't limit for a Coding agent in a Project's narrowed Boundary: its member details say it can read your whole Mac and keeps its own MCP servers and plugins, with Leave out of this Project and Undo, and each of its tasks opens with the same note.
- Fixed a Project's Boundary letting a file path through when a tool named it in a less common field or gave it as a relative path: every such path is now checked against the Project's folders, and a relative one is refused.
- Changed Coding agents in a Project with a narrowed Boundary to be told the Project's folders in each task, so they keep the files they read and change inside them.
- Changed Projects with a narrowed Boundary to let their agents call your other Agents, which now work inside the same Boundary instead of being refused.
- Changed chats made inside a Project's narrowed Boundary to keep it: a chat one of its agents starts by calling another Agent keeps it for your own later turns, and when you delete the Project, the chats that stay keep it instead of becoming ordinary chats.
- Fixed agents inside a narrowed Boundary being able to start a Coding agent, or another Agent's chat outside the Boundary, through the routes that run them directly.
- Added which Project Boundaries use each Connector, Connector account, MCP server and knowledge base: their pages name the narrowed Boundaries that keep them, and deleting a Project with a narrowed Boundary now says its chats keep it.
- Fixed a call that a Boundary refused folding away with the steps around it: it now stays in sight in the chat, with the line that names the Boundary and Add folder.

## Reliability

- Fixed Enconvo freezing for a few seconds when an app that answers slowly launches, activates, or moves its windows: Enconvo now reads a newly launched app's windows in the background and stops asking a slow app until it catches up.
- Fixed Enconvo freezing for a few seconds after you selected text in an app that was busy or slow to respond: the PopBar now reads the selection in the background.
- Fixed Enconvo freezing for a few seconds at launch, or when you turned Launch at Login on or off: it now registers with macOS in the background.
- Fixed Enconvo freezing while System Settings opened after you pressed Allow in the Computer Use permission prompt.
- Fixed Enconvo freezing for a few seconds at launch or while your microphone switched, such as when AirPods connected: the meeting reminder now checks and watches the microphone in the background.
- Fixed Enconvo freezing for a few seconds when macOS was busy: the Dynamic Island now checks for fullscreen apps in the background.
- Fixed Enconvo freezing for a few seconds when the Dynamic Island followed the mouse to another display while macOS was busy.
- Fixed Enconvo quitting when you start Dictation, Voice Command, voice input in a chat, or a recording with Bluetooth AirPods as the microphone: the microphone now follows the AirPods into their call mode, and a microphone Enconvo can't open ends only that recording instead of closing the app.
- Fixed chat replies and other updates pausing while a file or save panel that a plugin opened was showing.
- Fixed chat replies and other updates pausing while Check for Updates showed "You're up to date!" or an update error.
- Fixed Enconvo writing a lot to disk while a browser agent was working, and on other requests inside the app: Enconvo no longer saves its own internal answers to its web cache.
- Fixed attaching an .ico icon or a BMP, TIFF or HEIC image to a chat breaking the chat with "The image data you provided does not represent a valid image": the AI model now gets such images as PNG or JPEG and sees them directly, and an image no model can read is left out instead of failing every later reply.
- Fixed Return, ⌘Return, Esc, ⌘W and Delete typed in the Chat Window or another Enconvo window going to the Dynamic Island while it stayed open, which sent the island's message, ran its selected command, answered or dismissed its question, removed its context, or folded it instead of acting in the window you typed in.
- Fixed the Dynamic Island taking keyboard focus back from the Chat Window and Enconvo's other windows every couple of seconds while it stayed open, so what you typed there went to the island.
- Fixed the Smart Bar's Actions list (⌘K) staying open after the bar closed, which made Return in Settings and other Enconvo windows run the selected action and kept the arrow keys from working there.
- Fixed every key doing nothing in the Chat Window and Enconvo's other windows after you left Settings while a hotkey field was still waiting for keys, which also kept Enconvo's hotkeys off until you went back.
- Fixed Return and ⌘Return doing nothing in the Chat Window's message box, and in Enconvo's other windows, after you used Dictation with Press Return after dictation turned on, which also kept that setting from sending what you dictated there.
