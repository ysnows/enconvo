# Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 1

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 1. This build adds calls between Enconvo users with shared screens and agents on the line, lets you pair your other computers and work on them from here, and adds Email, Dashboards and Slides. Projects get goal checks, Autonomy and a Careful workflow, a chat can change its agent, and it fixes a long list of problems with custom OpenAI-compatible providers along with freezes on the Mac.

## Highlights

- **Calls between Enconvo users**: start one from the Dynamic Island or the Chat Window, ring people or your other devices, and bring agents onto the call.
- **Join a call from a browser**: anyone with the invite link can join as a guest without installing Enconvo, talk and type with the people and agents on the call, and share their screen from a computer.
- **Cameras, chat and reactions on calls**: turn your camera on, pick your microphone and speaker, write in the call's chat, raise your hand and send quick reactions.
- **Draw on a shared screen**: strokes land on the same spot for everyone with the drawer's name, and an agent can ring the spot it means once the person sharing allows it.
- **Agents that see a shared screen**: ask one about the screen on a call, and the person sharing decides whether it may look.
- **Call notes**: turn on Notes during a call and, when it ends, the people there get a shared Doc with the summary, decisions, action items and transcript.
- **Pair your other computers**: from Settings › Connections, use a computer's chats, terminal and screen from here while they keep running there, and choose what each one can reach.
- **Your other computers in the sidebar**: expand a computer to see its chats and Projects with what needs you, and the Run on chip picks which computer a new chat runs on.
- **Hand work to another computer**: your agent here can start a chat on a paired computer and read the answer, and you can send files to it from its window.
- **Email**: add your own mailboxes over IMAP and read, search and answer them in the Chat Window, with a summary and suggested replies on each conversation.
- **More in Email**: Snooze, Send Later, Undo Send, Remind Me If No Reply, templates, sorting rules and sending from your aliases.
- **Email Automation and agents**: Enconvo reads new mail in the mailboxes you pick, tells your phone what needs you and can send a morning report, and agents you allow can search and draft there, always asking before sending or deleting.
- **Dashboards**: the agent makes a page of numbers and charts you keep, from a CSV, TSV or JSON file, and Check a number shows the row behind any figure.
- **Dashboards that stay up to date**: a dataset can read a connected service's tool on Refresh or on a schedule, with filters, comments on numbers, and PDF, image, HTML and zip downloads.
- **Slides**: the agent makes a deck you present beside the chat and download as a PowerPoint with real text boxes, shapes, tables and speaker notes, or as a PDF.
- **Edit decks in their panel**: retype words, drag elements, reorder slides, use Presenter view, leave comments that @Enconvo answers, and apply a design system to the whole deck.
- **Import a design system**: an agent can turn a folder, a tokens file or an npm package into a system with its exact colours, type, fonts and real components, and a check says what it still lacks.
- **Goal checks for Projects**: when a plan ends, the lead says whether each criterion is met and which tasks show it, an independent check questions weak claims, and lessons you keep go to Memory.
- **Autonomy and a Careful workflow**: choose how often a Project checks in, or have a member write a spec for you to approve before any code is written.
- **A Workflows tab in Projects**: see the Project's check and every workflow its lead can use, name one with @ in the Lead chat, and edit in drafts you publish or roll back.
- **Follow a plan as it runs**: a line above the Lead chat shows goal, progress, cost and time, and the run view shows each task's steps, findings, proof and cost by member.
- **Clone a repository into a new Project**: paste a GitHub link or any Git address in Create project, or pick one of your repositories.
- **New ideas, in your own words**: a New idea dialog takes screenshots, files, a priority and dictation, and an opened idea is a page with its chat with the lead beside it.
- **Change a chat's agent**: switch from the Chat Window's header, the Smart Bar or the App Sidebar, continue in a new chat with a Coding agent, or pick the agent when you fork.
- **Plugin hooks and installing from a link**: Claude Code and Codex plugin hooks such as context-mode's run once you allow them, and an Agent can install a plugin from a repository, zip or npm link in chat.
- **Enconvo Fast, Balanced and Ultra**: Enconvo Cloud's own models for quick turns, everyday work and hard tasks.
- **Translation styles**: keep Standard, switch to Sound Native, or describe your own in Custom.
- **Custom providers that just work**: dozens of fixes for OpenAI-compatible gateways, relays, Azure, LM Studio, OpenRouter and Gemini 3, with the server's own words when something fails.
- **Fewer freezes and a security fix**: Enconvo no longer stalls on busy apps when you switch to them, and a web page can no longer sign Enconvo in to another account without asking.
- **Calls on the iPhone**: start one from Home, turn on your camera, switch to the back camera, and read the Transcript and the call's chat.
- **Share your iPhone's screen on a call**: everyone sees it live, and anyone can draw on it to point at what to tap, with the strokes in a small window over whatever app you're in.
- **Link cards on the iPhone**: a link shows its title and domain, and a Google Maps or Apple Maps link shows the destination, how you'd travel and a button to open the map.
- **Change a chat's agent on the iPhone**: the agent under the chat's title opens a list to switch or to continue with a Coding agent, and Fork from here can start with the agent you choose.
- **A clearer iPhone sidebar**: a blue dot marks a Mac chat's unread result, running chats show a turning ring, and Search shows each result's Mac or agent mark.
