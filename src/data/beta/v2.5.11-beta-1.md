# Enconvo 2.5.11 Beta 1

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.11 Beta 1. This build adds Ideas to Projects, gives every chat Work plans and a Board, adds seven built-in agents, draws interactive apps and many new kinds of answers and charts in chat, brings most of the terminal commands of Claude Code, Codex and Grok Build into their chats, and brings the iPhone app to iPad and Mac Screen to the phones. Update the iPhone app to 1.2.13 and the Android app to 1.4.1 to try the phone changes.

## Highlights

- **Mac Screen and Take Control**: see your Mac's screen live on your iPhone or iPad from Remote, then Take Control to click, scroll and type on it.
- **Ideas for Projects**: write down what you want a Project to do, or talk it through with the lead first, then start an idea to have the lead plan it in a chat of its own and follow its tasks on the idea's card from Not started to Done.
- **Work plans and a Board in every chat**: name your other Agents with @ in any chat and Mavis gives each its part in a Work plan, with one Board for the tasks of all your chats outside a Project and a start page that shows what needs you.
- **Bring any Agent into a Project with @**: naming an Agent in a Lead chat adds it to the members, a Project takes as many members as it needs, and the Lead picker in the chat's header changes the lead and the members.
- **A Board that works like Linear**: arrow keys and single-key shortcuts, picking several cards for one action, Display options, Group by Agent, hidden lanes, and a calmer look with filled cards.
- **Seven built-in agents**: Coder, Designer, Reviewer, Researcher, Writer, Planner and Advisor join Mavis, each with its own role instructions, and the Designer works on the design canvas with your design system.
- **Interactive apps in answers**: when an answer needs real logic, such as a simulator whose sliders recompute a chart, a filter, a game or something to drag and draw, the Agent writes a small app right in the chat, with a Fix button if it fails.
- **New kinds of answers**: diagrams, quizzes, flashcards, checklists, step-by-step walkthroughs, tabs, schedules, option cards and maps on Apple Maps, drawn right in the conversation.
- **Nine new charts**: funnel, scatter, bubble, heatmap, Gantt, waterfall, Sankey, calendar and treemap charts, plus target lines and marked points on line charts.
- **Questions on cards**: the Agent asks what it needs before a task as questions you answer by picking options or typing, and offers candidates such as names for you to tick and send back.
- **Claude Code as in its terminal**: `/plugin` and a Plugins section in Claude Code's settings, plus `/rewind`, `/tasks`, `/context`, `/model`, `/mcp`, `/resume`, `/config` and 18 other commands in the chat, as in Claude Code's terminal.
- **More of Codex in its chats**: `/model`, `/permissions`, `/resume`, `/new`, `/delete`, `/import` from Claude Code or Cursor, `/app` to continue in the Codex app, `/copy`, `/cd`, `/mention` and file search in `@`.
- **Grok Build as in its terminal**: Plan mode, steering while it works, `/resume`, `/rewind`, `/model`, `/usage`, `/export` and more.
- **A background tray**: the subagents, commands and monitors a chat has running show above the message box with how long they've run, their latest output and a stop button.
- **Projects follow a moved folder**: point a Project at its folder's new place in Project settings, and its chats and worktrees come along.
- **The Dynamic Island's History holds every chat**: date groups, scrolling through every chat, a title search and pinning, with a button that opens all chats in the Chat Window.
- **File Search understands paths and your folders**: type a path such as ~/.claude to look in that folder, and a Search Folders setting with My Folders in Search In looks only where you choose.
- **Skills from GitHub**: paste a link to a repository or a skill's folder to install it, and Check for updates offers Update all when the repositories move on.
- **Skills and Plugins in Desktop Sync**: the skills you install or make and the plugins you install sync between your computers, each with its own switch under Settings › Sync.
- **OpenCode and OpenCode Go**: add your OpenCode Console API key once to chat with OpenCode's pay-as-you-go models or the models of your OpenCode Go subscription.
- **Chat Window opacity**: an Opacity slider in Settings › Chat Window takes the window anywhere from clear glass to solid, previewing as you drag.
- **Upgrade your license**: Plus and Pro license owners can upgrade to Pro or Teams in Settings › Account and pay only the difference.
- **The iPhone app on iPad**: the chat list stays beside the chat, the side panel opens beside it with a divider to drag, the menu bar has the Mac's shortcuts, Apple Pencil draws in Doodle, and side panel tabs open in windows of their own.
- **xAI voices on the phones**: xAI on your own account, with an API key or the Grok sign-in, and xAI on Enconvo Cloud now read aloud, take dictation and run Live Talk in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps.
- **Answers on the phones look like the Mac's**: charts, quizzes, flashcards, checklists, walkthroughs, tabs, timelines, flowcharts, maps and calculators that work out their results as you change them.

## Projects and Work Plans

- Changed @ in a Project's Lead chat, on the Mac and the iPhone: naming members no longer sends your message straight to them; Mavis, the lead, plans the work and gives each member you named its part in a Work plan, and files can now go with the message.
- Changed a Work plan for members you name with @ in a Project's Lead chat or a chat with Agents to start right away, so you no longer press Start after every message that names who should do the work; a plan whose member can't run yet still waits for Start.
- Added @ that brings an Agent into a Project: naming one of your Agents with @ in a Lead chat adds it to the members, with a line you can undo, and gives it its part, unless you turn off Anyone you @ joins in the header's Members view; in a Project without members, the first Agents you name become its members.
- Changed a Project's members so one is enough and there is no limit of six: add as many Agents and Coding agents as the Project needs, in Project settings or from the Lead chat's header.
- Added a Lead picker to the header of a Project's Lead chat, so you can change the Project's Lead and add, remove or give roles to its members right from the chat, as an ordinary chat lets you pick its Agent.
- Changed Projects made without members so they work like any other: their chats have the Lead and Members picker with Chat and Board, a Board, and the start page with the Lead's card, and the last member can leave from the header.
- Added the Project's Board to a Lead chat's start page: before the first message, cards for Needs you, To review, Running and New results take the members' place, clicking a card lists its tasks under it with Allow once, Decline, Try again or Review (clicking it again folds the list), and a task opens the Board right at it.
- Changed a Project's lead so it does the work no member fits itself, the Project's files included, without stopping after a set number of steps and handing the rest to a member.
- Changed Work plans in a Project whose folder isn't a git repository: the tasks that change files now take turns in the folder on their own, while tasks that only read run beside them, and the plan's card shows which task each one waits for, before and after Start.
- Changed the Work plan card to a simpler layout: each task is one line with its member on the right and its Done when line beneath it, Approve up front folds into one line, and the Permission mode and cost share the footer beside Start.
- Added task chips to Lead chats and task chats: each Board task number, such as T-65, shows the task's state and name (the number alone when the reply names it again), hovering previews it, a click opens its detail in the side panel beside the chat, and ⌘-click opens it on the Board.
- Added automatic retries for Board and Work plan tasks that a passing AI model error cuts short, such as an overloaded or rate-limited model, a server error or a dropped connection: the task runs again in its chat after a pause, picking up where it stopped, up to three times, its card says while it waits, and it fails and notifies you only when the model keeps failing.
- Removed the "You allowed … once" lines that piled up above a Lead chat's message box after you answered its members' approvals.
- Fixed a long command in a Lead chat's approval card filling the window: it now scrolls in a box of its own height.
- Fixed a Claude Code or Codex task in a Work plan handing on its chat's warnings, such as Codex's MCP startup notice, and its notes from before its last step together with its answer, so the task's card, the Lead and the tasks after it get only the answer; Paste and Copy on a Coding agent's reply leave such warnings out too.
- Fixed a forked chat's copy of a Work plan card starting or changing the plan of the chat it came from: the copy now says to start and change the plan there, with Open chat.
- Added a way to point a Project at its folder again after the folder was moved or renamed: Project settings marks the missing folder, and Change… picks its new place, keeping the Project's chats and worktrees.
- Fixed pointing a Project at its moved folder when a worktree made inside it (such as Claude Code's `claude -w`) moved along: git now works in that worktree again, and the worktree Enconvo took in from it follows to its new place instead of showing as missing.
- Fixed a Claude Code or Codex chat in a Project, including one that was replying at the time, refusing new messages with "The folder no longer exists" after the Project was pointed at its folder's new place; the chat now follows the folder and goes on with its conversation.
- Fixed Claude Code forgetting what it remembered about a Project after the Project was pointed at its folder's new place.
