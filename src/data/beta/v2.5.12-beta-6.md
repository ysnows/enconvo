# Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 6

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 6. This build adds a captions history to calls, picture in picture on the phones, voice cloning in Text-to-Speech, a Make something row for new chats, and videos and files that open right in friend chats, and makes the Dictation HUD one card. Please scroll back through a call's captions, clone a voice, and try Make something in a new chat.

## Highlights

- **Captions history in calls**: click or tap the live caption to scroll back through who said what, with translations, and copy one line or all of them, on the Mac, iPhone and Android.
- **Picture in picture for calls on the phones**: the iPhone's small call window gains Switch and tucks to the side of the screen, and the Android app shrinks a call to a picture with Switch, Mute and Leave.
- **Voice cloning in Text-to-Speech** with Qwen3, ElevenLabs, Fish Audio and Gemini: upload or record samples, name the voice and pick it from the Voice menu.
- **Make something**: pick Slides, Doc, Design, Film, Dashboard or Web page under a new chat's message box, and the Artifacts page gains a New menu for the same kinds.
- **Videos, PDFs and text files open right in friend chats** on the Mac, iPhone and Android.
- **Mac Screen in Live Talk on the phones**: open your Mac's screen in a small floating window over the call.
- **One Dictation card**: the pill grows into a card with your words above its controls, and shorter, softer chimes start listening sooner.
- **Clipboard History, Bookmark Search and File Search** open without flashing, and each appears once.

## Video Conference

- Changed the Video Conference page so only the list of calls scrolls, while the search box, buttons and Take notes stay in place.
- Changed the Video Conference icon to a video camera everywhere: the Dynamic Island, the menu bar menu, the Chat Window sidebar, the iPhone and Android apps, and the Call buttons on the conference pages.
- Changed the iPhone's picture in picture for calls so it shows whoever was big when you left the call, yourself included, and the small call window in the app gains a Switch button that moves between following the speaker, each person whose camera is on, and you.
- Added tucking the iPhone's small call window to the side of the screen: throw or drag it past an edge and a small handle stays there, and a tap or drag on the handle brings it back.
- Added picture in picture for calls in the Android app: leaving a call shrinks it to a small picture of whoever was big, with Switch, Mute and Leave buttons on it; Switch skips anyone whose camera is off, and on Android 12 it shows once you make the picture bigger.
- Added a captions history to Video Conference calls on the Mac, iPhone and Android: click or tap the live caption to scroll back through who said what and when, with the translation over the original words, jump back to the latest line, and copy one line or all of them.

## Friend Chat

- Fixed text in friend chats on the Mac not being selectable, so a message can now be selected and copied.
- Added videos that play right in a friend chat on the Mac: a video shows its first frame and length before it downloads, a click plays it in its bubble, and a corner button opens it in the viewer.
- Changed PDF and text files in friend chats on the Mac to open in the window with one click, without a Download step first, and added Save As… on a file's corner and in its right-click menu.
- Added videos, PDFs and text files that open right in a friend chat on the iPhone: a video shows its first frame and length before it downloads and plays in the app, and Save puts it in Photos or Files.
- Added video posters in Android friend chats: a video shows its first frame and length, and plays full screen in the app with Save and Share.
- Changed Android friend chat PDFs and text files to open in the app's own viewer, with download progress you can stop and a Try again when a file can't be read.
- Fixed friend chat PDFs that can't be read on iPhone and Android, so the viewer says so instead of showing a blank page or a spinner that never ends, and added swipe down to close the Android viewer.

## iPhone and Android

- Fixed new Mac chats on the iPhone and in the Android app taking a few seconds behind a spinner: your first message now shows the moment you send it, as in the Smart Bar, while your Mac sets the chat up.
- Added Mac Screen to Live Talk on iPhone and Android: while your Mac is online, a Mac Screen tool beside the call's other tools opens the Mac's screen in a small floating window over the call, where a tap clicks, double-tap or pinch zooms, and the window can be dragged or tucked into a corner; Full Screen opens the usual Mac Screen with Back to Live Talk, and the call keeps going the whole time.

## Make Something and Voices

- Added a Make something row under a new chat's composer: pick Slides, Doc, Design, Film, Dashboard or Web page to set the chat to that kind, try one of three examples, start a blank one beside the chat, or open your existing ones, and the Artifacts page gains a New menu for the same kinds.
- Added voice cloning to Text-to-Speech: Clone Voice on the Voice row of Qwen3, ElevenLabs, Fish Audio and Gemini opens a page where you upload or record samples of one speaker (and, for Gemini, record a consent line), name the voice and save it, and cloned voices appear at the top of the Voice menu with rename and delete; on Enconvo Cloud each account keeps up to 3 cloned voices per provider.

## Dictation

- Changed the Dictation HUD into one surface: the pill grows into a card that shows your words above its controls, Clean up moves to a small chip on the card, and hovering opens the settings in the same card.
- Changed the sounds that start and end Dictation, the Live Talk connect cue and the recording bar to shorter, softer chimes, so dictation starts listening sooner after you press the keys.

## Clipboard History, Bookmark Search and File Search

- Fixed Clipboard History, Bookmark Search and File Search flashing each time they open: the list, its item count and its action buttons stay as they are, and only new items are added.
- Removed the separate web versions of Clipboard History, Bookmark Search and File Search, so each appears once: a Shortcut or Alias set on an old version moves to the native list, and each list's Shortcut and Alias are now in its Settings card on the Plugins page.
- Changed the Smart Bar list so the highlight jumps straight to the next row when you move with the arrow keys, without sliding.

## Projects

- Fixed picking a Coding agent such as Claude Code as the Lead of a Project without members: a Work plan that was never started no longer blocks it, and is set aside when the Coding agent takes over.
