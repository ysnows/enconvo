# Enconvo 2.5.15 Beta 5

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.15 Beta 5. This build gives each Agent a Tools & Skills setting, lets Claude Code, Codex, Grok Build and Antigravity lead a Project with members, adds one-click website publishing on Enconvo Cloud, shows Cloud prices in Settings, makes Answer my calls safe from callers, gives Android a new one-row composer, and lets an account made with Hide My Email add its own email and password.

## Highlights

- **Tools & Skills for each Agent**: keep Auto-discover, or give an agent exactly the tools, skills and MCP servers you choose.
- **Coding agents lead Projects**: Claude Code, Codex, Grok Build or Antigravity can plan work for a Project's members and read their results.
- **One-click website publishing**: Publish puts a page online at your own name.site.enconvo.com link.
- **Cloud prices in Settings**: see each model's points next to its name, and Enconvo Cloud Fast speech recognition is now free.
- **Safer Answer my calls**: a caller can't make your agent act, with a passphrase for numbers that give tasks and a Likely scam warning.
- **A new Android composer**: one row with camera, quick chips and a plus panel with your recent photos.

## Agents and Projects

- Added a Tools & Skills section to each Agent's settings: keep Auto-discover, where built-in tools are always ready and installed skills, plugins and MCP tools are found when a task needs them, or switch to Only what I choose to give the agent a fixed set; you can also turn off individual built-in tools, pin skills so they are always listed, and stop suggestions from the Enconvo Store.
- Added a What can it use? choice to Create New Agent, with Everyday, Coding, Research and Writing presets for an agent with a fixed set of tools.
- Changed Projects so Claude Code, Codex, Grok Build or Antigravity can lead a Project with members: it sees the team and the Board each turn, plans the work for members in a Work plan, and reads their results as your Agents do.
- Changed Projects led by a Coding agent to work like any other Project: other chats and automations can send it requests, you can @ its members and Workflows, its chat shows the members card, and it gets Ideas.
- Added picking fast mode and ultracode in a new Claude Code chat before its first message.
- Fixed Claude Code models, effort levels and fast mode taking around ten seconds to appear in a new chat; they now show at once, also after Enconvo restarts.
- Fixed Claude chats that kept failing a request and retrying at every step after an earlier reply's reasoning was refused, which made each reply start many seconds late.
- Added subagent deliverables to the main chat: what a subagent makes, such as a design or a doc, shows under its row as it is made and opens beside the chat, the agent hands it over as a card in its answer, and the Members and Chat details cards list every deliverable of the chat and its subagents.
- Fixed Full access still asking to approve a command that removes its own scratch folder under /tmp at the end, after running inside it.

## Chat Window

- Changed New chat in the Chat Window to start in the project you last picked under the message box, so you no longer choose it again each time; pick No project to go back to plain chats.
- Changed Worktree rows in the Chat Window sidebar to fold and unfold like the main branch row: a click shows the chats inside, even when there is only one, and an empty Worktree unfolds to a New chat row.
- Changed the device at the foot of the Chat Window sidebar to show only its name before any device is paired, instead of an Add a device line under it.
- Fixed Esc in the Chat Window so it stops a reply only when pressed in that chat, not while typing in the sidebar, its search or elsewhere in the window.
- Fixed screenshots dragged from their floating thumbnail not being added in Friend chats on the Mac.

## Model Fallback

- Changed the Fallback panel in Settings › AI Model to show the chain as it works: The model in use, what happens when it can't be used, then your numbered backups with Ready or Paused, and a click on a backup's model name switches it to another model of that provider.

## Picture in Picture

- Changed the Picture-in-Picture window on Mac so it no longer grows when you hover it, and when more tasks wait the cards behind it peek out on top with a Show all button that says how many there are.
- Changed the Picture-in-Picture window on Mac to update live: browser tabs refresh about three times a second instead of once, and app windows stream smoothly while they change.
- Fixed dragging the Picture-in-Picture window on Mac making it flicker and jump in steps, most of all on a non-Retina screen; it now follows the pointer and stays where you drop it.
- Fixed live views in the Picture-in-Picture window on Mac staying black for pages that another browser extension adds its own widget to; such a page now says it has no live preview.

## Calls

- Changed Answer my calls so a caller can't make your agent act: the summary is written without tools, a call's chat asks before anything that sends, pays, deletes or runs commands even with Full access, and Settings › Agent Phone shows what a caller can reach.
- Added Ask for a passphrase for Numbers that can give tasks: a caller must say your passphrase before the call can take tasks, since a faked caller ID can pass for those numbers.
- Added a Likely scam badge and warning to calls your agent answered that look like scams.
- Fixed the Calls section on the Mac, iPhone and Android showing only a weekday and a masked number: each call now shows when it rang, the full number or the contact's name with how many contacts share it, and the section folds on the phones too.

## Remote Screen and Pairing

- Changed pairing with another computer so the Mac being paired opens its Dynamic Island on the request, with the number to compare and Allow, instead of a notification that sent you to Settings to find Pair a computer.
- Fixed Return in another computer's screen pasting this Mac's clipboard there when an input method offered the clipboard as a suggestion: only ⌘V pastes now, and the Return goes through.
- Fixed dragging on another computer's screen letting go of the mouse button right away, so text and items can be selected by dragging again.
- Changed the cursor on another computer's screen to follow your pointer at once while you are in control, instead of waiting for the other computer.
- Fixed the top edge of another computer's screen being out of reach in a small viewer window, so its Dynamic Island now opens there too.
- Fixed being unable to leave full screen while controlling another computer's screen: ⌃⌘F now goes in and out, and resting the pointer on the top edge brings the bar down.
- Fixed typing on a computer's screen from Android with keyboards that compose a whole word (Samsung Keyboard, SwiftKey): each letter now reaches the computer as you type, and Return no longer drops the word before it.

## Publishing and Enconvo Cloud

- Added one-click website publishing: a page artifact's Publish button puts it online at your own name.site.enconvo.com link on Enconvo Cloud, with an optional password, and Update ships the newer version, Put back restores an earlier one, and agents can publish a page or a site folder for you when you ask them to share it.
- Added Cloud prices next to models and providers in Settings: model lists show each model's points per million tokens with a cost meter, Enconvo Cloud providers for speech, voices, search, OCR, images and video show their price under the name and on their page, and speech models show their price per minute.
- Added plan benefit notes in Settings: the Recording and Live Captions model pages show your free minutes left this month or that your plan makes them unlimited, and the Enconvo Cloud model page shows the Pro and Max discount on boost models.
- Changed Enconvo Cloud Fast speech recognition to be free for everyone, with no points used, and Settings marks it Free next to its name.

## iPhone and Android

- Changed the Android composer to a single row with a camera button, a row of quick chips above it for the computer, project, Compare answers and Skills, and a plus panel that opens where the keyboard was.
- Changed the Android composer so that once you type or add a photo, the mic and plus fold away and Send stands alone, with a + tile after your photos to add more.
- Changed the agent button in the Android composer to carry a smaller avatar with a quiet phone badge, so it reads as a call.
- Changed the Android composer's mic and plus into matching round buttons and moved plus to the start of the row with the camera inside its panel, so the row is lighter.
- Added recent photos to the Android plus panel in the downloadable APK, so one tap puts a photo in your message.
- Added a choice in iPhone and Android Settings › Avatar to show the composer's call button as the avatar or the voice bars icon, also switchable by holding the button; voice chats still show the avatar.
- Changed the Active chats card on iPhone and Android Home: it has a grabber at the top, and swiping it up opens the full list under your finger.
- Added folding to the Recent group in the iPhone and Android sidebars, so a tap on its heading hides or shows your recent chats, and the app remembers it.
- Fixed Home on Android so the layout no longer shakes when All opens or closes the Active chats panel.

## Account and Settings

- Added an Email and Password section to Settings › Account on Mac, iPhone and Android, so an account made with Apple's Hide My Email can add its own email and set or reset a password.
- Added Continue with Apple to Enconvo Feedback, so accounts made with Sign in with Apple can sign in there too.
- Changed Invites so invite codes can no longer be used from temporary or agent email addresses.
- Fixed the Authentication cards on a provider's settings page overlapping their text when the Settings window is narrow, and the API key card now shows its description in your language.

## Dictation

- Added Telegram to the calls Dictation mutes you in: in a call in the Telegram app for Mac, you're muted while you dictate and unmuted when you finish or pause.
- Fixed Bluetooth headphones such as AirPods blasting music louder than your volume for a moment after dictation or Voice Command ends; the volume now comes back gently once the headphones have switched back from their microphone.
