# Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 4

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.12 Beta 4. This build adds voice messages to Friend Chat, Phone chats in the Chat Window, Dictation Draft and Live Desktop, and brings Notes back to conference calls. Projects get new built-in workflows, Settings opens inside the Chat Window, and Computer Use, conferences and Recording get a long list of fixes. Please try a voice message with a friend, a Draft by voice, and a call with Notes on, and tell us what feels off.

## Highlights

- **Voice messages in Friend Chat** on the Mac, iPhone and Android: record up to a minute and send it, or turn it into text you can edit first, with Hold to Talk on the phones and a new Emoji button.
- **Phone chats on the Mac**: chats started on your iPhone or Android phone show in a Phone chats group in the Chat Window sidebar, continue on the Mac, and delete on both sides at once.
- **Dictation Draft**: hold the Dictation hotkey with ⇧, say what to write or change, and review the draft before it goes in; Edit selected text by voice becomes Draft too.
- **Smart spacing and snippets by voice in Dictation**: dictated text gets the spaces around it right, and saying a snippet's name inserts its text.
- **Dictation keeps what it couldn't transcribe** in History with a Retry button, names a silent microphone, and gains a microphone test in Settings › Dictation.
- **Live Desktop**: a board behind your windows shows what your agents, coding sessions and automations are doing today, with an OpenAvatar character that reacts as things run, need you, finish or fail.
- **New built-in Project workflows**: Ship, Planner, Sprint, Compound and Verify setup replace Build, Careful, Fix a bug, Review and Research, and a Use workflows switch decides whether the Lead runs tasks through them.
- **A Project's Workflows tab is one list**, with each workflow's icon, the faces of its members, its run and failure counts, and the missing member picked right in its row.
- **Compare answers and Use style in a Project's Lead chat**.
- **Notes are back in conference calls on the Mac**: AI notes from the call go into a Doc you share or keep, with an optional audio or video recording, and the iPhone can turn them on from More.
- **Calls in Recordings**: every call you record on your Mac gets its own entry, and a call with Notes on shows its transcript, who was there, its video and its notes Doc.
- **Friend calls open in the Conference window on the Mac**, with a ringback tone while it rings and friends' messages shown as toasts during the call.
- **Do Not Disturb and a mute hotkey for calls**: silence calls on the Mac or iPhone for 1 hour, until 8:00 tomorrow, or until you turn it off, and mute or unmute from any app with Control-Option-M.
- **Ask to unmute for organizers, and Stop incoming video** to save data on a slow connection while voices and shared screens keep going.
- **Conferences hold up on poor connections**: they switch to a better route much sooner, keep someone audible while their connection reconnects, and hold a phone guest's place for up to 5 minutes while they're away.
- **Settings opens inside the Chat Window** from the sidebar menu, the model picker, the points pill and other links, with Back to return where you were.
- **Share links are ready at once**: the link can be copied while the chat uploads, large screenshots go in as smaller images, and Update link sends only what changed.
- **Points history loads in a fraction of the time**, with filters, a model filter, a date picker and each day's points used.
- **Soniox moves to its new v6 models** everywhere Enconvo uses it, and saved v5 choices move over on their own before Soniox retires v5 on December 15, 2026.
- **Private plugins from GitHub and any git server**, using the GitHub account already signed in on your Mac or your SSH key.
- **Computer Use does what it reports**: right clicks, double clicks, held keys, drags, menus, sheets and popovers no longer go out as a plain click that claims success.
- **Hide Enconvo (Command-H) now sticks**, instead of the island, side bar or a panel bringing Enconvo back a moment later.
- **Video Conference on Android**: join a call from its link, keep it going while you use other apps, and switch between the earpiece and the speaker.
- **Calls on the iPhone** gain a Speaker button, arrows beside Mute and Camera, friends' messages at the top of the call, and use less data and battery in the background.
- **Sign in on the iPhone and Android inside the app**, with Google or a 6-digit code emailed to you.
