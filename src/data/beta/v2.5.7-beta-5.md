# Enconvo 2.5.7 Beta 5

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.7 Beta 5. This build turns push-to-talk Voice Command into a spoken conversation, lets voice calls look things up and control your Mac on their own, adds Spotify and Apple Music, and reworks Agents and Manage Models.

## Highlights

- **Push-to-talk Voice Command answers aloud**: what you dictate goes into a voice call in the chat you used last, and her answer is spoken and shown in the same Dynamic Island view.
- **Voice calls do more on their own**: a call in a Hub searches the web, reads pages and files, runs about 30 quick Mac controls, and starts and manages tasks itself.
- **Spotify and Apple Music**: new plugins and Dynamic Island buttons let agents and voice calls play, find and organize music, and the Avatar can dance along.
- **Agents you can pause**: Agents settings is now a card gallery, an agent can be paused and resumed, and its settings fit on one page.
- **Manage Models rework**: models are added in a form that knows their limits, deleted models can be restored, and OpenRouter lists load faster with the right abilities.
- **Free voice for members**: Soniox dictation and Live Talk voice calls no longer use Cloud points for members.

## Voice Command and Dictation

- Changed push-to-talk Voice Command so a press opens a voice call in the chat you used within the last 10 minutes, with no ringing or greeting; what you dictate goes to the call and is answered aloud, a press while she talks stops her, the microphone stays off between presses, and the call hangs up after 10 quiet minutes.
- Changed push-to-talk Voice Command so what you say and her answer show in the same Dynamic Island view at the same size, with only the Screen Doodle and Live Screen buttons; while she answers the island says Thinking and then Speaking, then Done once she has finished, and it stays half-expanded until you click outside it, while Live Talk, voice previews and the chat's microphone stay free to use between answers.
- Fixed push-to-talk Voice Command failing to start its call when the chat you used last runs a different Agent; the call now joins that chat as its Agent, and words sent to the chat instead no longer leave a silent call behind.
- Fixed push-to-talk Voice Command so she no longer speaks an update while you are still holding the key, and a press you take back right away no longer switches the chat's Agent.
- Changed dictation and Voice Command with Soniox so your words appear sooner when you press again within a minute, by keeping the speech connection ready between presses.
- Changed voice input with Soniox to be free for Cloud Plan, Lifetime and trial members, so dictation no longer uses Cloud points for them.
- Fixed dictation asking members for Cloud points after a negative balance; members now dictate free through Soniox.

## Voice Calls

- Fixed voice calls on the Mac and iPhone saying they would do something, such as booking a hotel, and never starting it; the call now starts, changes, asks about and stops tasks itself, and answers and starts tasks sooner because it no longer waits for a separate routing step or for a task title.
- Added web search, web page reading, and finding and reading files on your Mac to voice calls in a Hub, so a quick question is answered in the call in a few seconds without starting a task, and the call can open and quit apps on your Mac on its own.
- Added tool cards to voice call chats on the Mac and iPhone, so a web search, web page, file search or file read the call runs itself shows between what it said before and after, with what it found.
- Added about 30 quick Mac controls and checks to voice calls in a Hub, such as Dark Mode, mute and volume levels, play and pause, Stage Manager, hiding other apps, clearing notifications, showing hidden files, locking the screen, sleep, battery level and free disk space; each one says whether it worked or the Mac already was that way, and why when it couldn't.
- Changed quick Mac actions in voice calls so the screen locks or the Mac sleeps only after the call has told you, and a call from your iPhone changes your Mac only when you name the Mac or the computer.
- Changed the quick volume, mute and Dark Mode controls in voice calls to finish faster.
- Fixed quick Mac action replies in voice calls always being in Chinese; they now follow the language you speak.
- Fixed Hide Other Apps also hiding the app you were using; it now keeps that app and Enconvo in view and no longer needs Automation access to System Events.
- Changed Live Talk voice calls to be free for members, covering speech recognition, the model and voice replies.
- Fixed voice calls switching to Traditional Chinese after a short reply such as “好啊”.
- Fixed messages sent in the Dynamic Island while a voice call's task was running staying queued with a Send button that only queued them again until you reopened the chat; the chat now shows the running task's reply and offers Steer.
- Fixed the Dynamic Island opening the call's chat by itself right after you hang up a Live Talk call; it now stays closed until you open it.

## Spotify, Apple Music and Mac Apps

- Added a Spotify plugin that lets agents search for songs, albums, playlists, artists and podcasts, play them, pause, skip, check what's playing, and change the volume in the Spotify app on your Mac, with no Spotify account sign-in.
- Added playlist tools to the Spotify plugin, so agents and Spotify voice calls can list your playlists and their songs, open, create, rename or delete a playlist, and add or remove songs.
- Added an Apple Music plugin that lets agents play songs, albums, artists and playlists from your Music library, find music in the Apple Music catalog, pause, skip, check what's playing, and change the volume, shuffle and repeat in the Music app on your Mac.
- Added Spotify and Apple Music buttons to the Dynamic Island that start a voice call which plays, pauses, skips and finds music in that app on its own, so a request like “play 晴天” needs no task.
- Added the app's icon to Spotify and Apple Music voice calls in the Dynamic Island, with a tooltip saying the call can control that app, and a Connect prompt that sets up the app's plugin before the call when it isn't connected yet.
- Added a Mac Apps tab to Plugins that lists the apps on your Mac agents can use, such as Apple Calendar, Notes and Spotify, and shows when one isn't installed.
- Fixed connecting a Mac app in Plugins staying on Connecting when the running Enconvo was older than the plugin; it now asks you to update Enconvo.
- Added Dance Along with Music to the Avatar's menu, below Perform: when it is on, she dances by herself while Spotify or Music plays, whichever you open first, moving through a mix of her dances in time with the song's beat; she starts about half a second after you press play and stops as soon as you pause, waits while she talks or does a motion you ask for and then dances on, stops when you move the pointer onto her or choose Stay Still until the music pauses, and stays still for speech, including Spotify podcasts and audiobooks. It is off by default and stays on across restarts once you turn it on.

## Agents

- Added Pause and Resume for agents, so you can put an agent on hold without losing its settings: a paused agent doesn't reply in its channels or run its scheduled tasks, and it's hidden from agent pickers. A paused agent stays paused when you reset its settings and on your other Macs.
- Changed the Agents settings page into a card gallery with Active and Paused sections, a search box in the title bar, and Select mode for pausing, resuming, or deleting several agents at once.
- Changed the New Agent button in Settings → Agents to open the new agent form directly, without the OpenClaw and Hermes options, which stay in those providers' setup guides.
- Changed an agent's settings into one page with an outline to jump between sections, settings grouped into rows with a short explanation each, and a Used by section that shows the agent's shortcut, IM channels and scheduled tasks.
- Removed the Tools picker from an agent's settings; tools already picked for an agent keep working.
- Changed an agent's settings to leave out Dynamic Context, Live Screen, Enable Memory, Run Mode and Permissions; agents keep the values already set for them.
- Removed Tutor Mode from agents.
- Added a tooltip to an agent's Working Folder that shows where its files are stored, including the default folder, and that each chat gets its own folder there.
- Added a Delete option for built-in agents in the agent list.
- Fixed deleted built-in agents such as Latin Teacher and Spanish Teacher coming back after an app update.

## Models

- Changed Manage Models so models are added and edited in a form that fills in the limits and abilities of models it knows.
- Added a Deleted Models view to Manage Models, where deleted models can be restored one at a time or all at once.
- Changed Manage Models so its window opens right away with a placeholder while the list loads, and long model lists such as OpenRouter's show about three times faster.
- Changed Manage Models so model names are larger with the provider part dimmed, each model sits on its own row with a divider, and reopening a list shows its models at once instead of a placeholder.
- Changed Manage Models so a failed refresh shows why it failed, along with the list from the last successful refresh.
- Fixed Manage Models for OpenAI-compatible and duplicated providers so it lists, adds, and deletes that provider's own models instead of OpenAI's.
- Fixed the built-in GPT-6 models in Manage Models so they can be deleted.
- Fixed a deleted built-in model added back by its ID in Manage Models being marked Edited with the values the form filled in.
- Fixed Manage Models labels, messages and errors showing in English when the app uses another language.
- Fixed OpenAI providers with no API key listing GPT-6 models they could not use.
- Fixed OpenAI-compatible providers pointed at OpenRouter so each model's context window, max output, prices, and abilities come from OpenRouter's model list.
- Fixed OpenRouter models taking tool use, image input and other abilities from Enconvo's model registry when OpenRouter's own list says otherwise.
- Fixed Manage Models for OpenRouter showing no error when a refresh failed.
- Fixed the Model Name list in provider settings not following a switch between API Key and a subscription; it now shows the new method's models right away and picks one it can use, and switching back brings back the model you had.
- Fixed dropdowns in settings, such as Model Name, stalling for about half a second when opened on long lists like OpenRouter's; they now open right away on the selected option, and search also matches model IDs.
- Changed the API Type dropdown in provider settings to half the width.
- Fixed Enconvo Cloud sometimes creating and charging for an image twice when the provider's server stalled.

## PopBar

- Added information buttons for PopBar extensions, such as word and character counts, which show their result for the selected text right on the PopBar.
- Added button groups for PopBar extensions, so grouped buttons and buttons an extension makes from the selected text open in a menu on the PopBar.
- Added keyboard control to the PopBar when it is shown with its hotkey: the arrow keys and Tab move between buttons, Return runs one, and groups open from the keyboard.
- Added a result card to the PopBar for text a PopBar extension gives back, where you can read, edit, copy it, or put it in place of the selection, with a Result setting per extension and Show Last Result in the More panel.
- Added 17 more PopBar extensions to the catalog that use these, including Word Count, Character Count, Calculate, Brackets, Convert and Unix Time.
- Added about 40 more PopBar extensions to the catalog from other PopClip authors' repositories and gists, including Bob, PoorText, Password Generator, Currency Converter, BoltAI, Raycast and DevDocs.
- Removed eight PopBar extensions that PopClip's own directory no longer lists from the catalog; copies already installed keep working.
- Fixed PopBar extension options that accept a typed value, and options renamed in an extension update, so their saved values carry over.
- Changed the PopBar's Customize menu to clearer names (New AI Command, Browse Extensions, Add Commands, Edit Buttons, Settings), and Add Commands now opens the Add Buttons page in Settings.
- Changed the PopBar's Add Buttons page in Settings into a compact grouped list, with your PopBar and search kept at the top, and removed the Knowledge Base buttons from it; ones already on your PopBar keep working.
- Changed the PopBar to close again when you press a key, while it stays open as you type in it or use it with the keyboard after opening it with its hotkey.
- Changed the Inline Mode switch in the PopBar's small SmartBar to a compact icon with a mini switch that takes about half the space.
- Fixed the model button in the PopBar's small SmartBar floating away from the close button; it now sits right next to it.
- Fixed the extra gap under selected text in SmartBar and chat replies, so the answer now starts right below the text you asked about.

## Dynamic Island

- Added an All Screens option for the Dynamic Island, now the default, so the island shows at the top of every display and opens on the one your mouse is on.
- Fixed the Dynamic Island sometimes disappearing after switching desktops or Spaces, or jumping to the wrong display on setups with more than one screen.
- Fixed the Dynamic Island bouncing as it resized when it followed the mouse to another display; it now appears there at that display's size right away.
- Fixed the Dynamic Island on displays with a notch so the character and the task count no longer touch the pill's edges while tasks are running.

## iPhone Companion

- Added Fast mode to the model sheet for Mac chats on iPhone: supported OpenAI, Anthropic and Enconvo Cloud models now offer Standard, Fast and Ultrafast speeds above the reasoning levels, saved per model as on the Mac, and the chat's header shows Fast when it is on.
- Changed Read Aloud on iPhone to use ElevenLabs on Enconvo Cloud by default, starting to speak as the voice is made, and to read in the ElevenLabs voice picked on the Mac.
- Fixed Read Aloud on iPhone sometimes staying silent with its button stuck on Stop; when the sound doesn't start, it now starts over once and otherwise stops.
- Fixed voice calls on iPhone sometimes staying in Napping and not waking when you spoke; tapping her, the Napping status or a call button, or unmuting, now wakes the call, a microphone that stops restarts on its own, and unmuting a napping call on the Mac wakes it too.

## Commands and Extensions

- Fixed built-in commands deleted from the launcher coming back after an app update.
- Changed reinstalling an extension so it brings back the built-in commands you deleted from it.
