# Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 5

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.18 Beta 5. This build renames Team mode to Teamwork and makes its runs hold up: a member that stops on an error says why and offers Resume, every task has a short name, runs with more than six tasks no longer hang, and a task for an offline computer waits for it. Settings › Devices lists every computer, phone and server on your account, Dictation on Enconvo Cloud uses no points, Voice Command can send a picture of your window, and a page made in chat can install as a Mac app. Please test a Teamwork run in a Project chat, including a member that hits an error or a usage limit, Settings › Devices, Dictation and Draft, and Install as App.

## Highlights

- **Teamwork, formerly Team mode**: a member that stops on an error shows why with Resume and one fix for everyone a limit stopped, every task has a short name and a line on what it is doing, and runs with more than six tasks no longer hang.
- **@ an Agent in Teamwork**: name an Agent in a Project chat and it takes the part you gave it, or the whole run, while your saved team stays as it is.
- **Settings › Devices**: one list of every computer, phone and server on your account, with Pause, Remove from account and Ask first when pairing.
- **Dictation on Enconvo Cloud uses no points**: speech-to-text, Quick Memory voice notes and fast-model Polish, Draft, Translate and Edit are free up to a generous daily limit.
- **Voice Command sees your window**: hold ⇧ with your hotkey and a picture of the window you're in goes with your words.
- **Draft sees what you see**: Draft sends a screenshot of the app's window by default, and its panel shows and lets you turn off everything that goes to the model.
- **Install as App (Beta)**: a page made in chat, or a Space, installs as a Mac app with its own window and Dock icon.
- **Project settings in the Project**: they open right in the Settings page, with Save and Discard once you change something.

## Teamwork

- Changed the name of Team mode to Teamwork (团队协作 in Chinese) in the top bar's switch, the Team sheet on the iPhone and iPad, and every message about it, so it is no longer taken for the Enconvo Teams plan; `/teamwork` turns it on or off, and typing `/team-mode` still finds it.
- Added a clear reason and Resume for every Teamwork member that stops on an error, in the tray, its Task chat and the run's card on a phone, with one fix for all the members a usage limit or the same problem stopped, such as going on with another account or another model until the limit resets.
- Changed a Teamwork Lead that stopped on an error to wait for its limit to reset or for your Resume instead of being woken by its members' posts into turns that fail the same way, and it reads what they posted when it goes on; pressing Resume twice, or Resume and Resume all at once, now resumes it once, and stopping the run while its Resume compacts the chat, or while a message for it is on its way, keeps it stopped.
- Changed every task in a Teamwork run to carry a short name of its own, with a line under it saying what it is doing now or what it is for, in the run's list and Flow, the tray above the message box and on a phone, and its Task chat takes that name.
- Changed the folded tray above the message box to keep up to three tasks still running, waiting on you or stopped on an error in view under its title, each with what it is doing, how long it has run and Stop, and a line that opens the tray when more are running; this covers Teamwork tasks, Subagents, background commands and Claude Code tasks.
- Fixed Teamwork runs with more than six tasks hanging with a task that never started: a member waiting for its next turn no longer takes one of the six running slots, a task that does wait for a slot says "Waiting for a free slot" and starts as soon as one frees, and a member called while all six are busy takes its turn then, with the results of work it left running kept even if Enconvo restarts meanwhile; tasks and members waiting for a slot take the next one in the order they started waiting, Resume, Resume all, a model switch and Try again on a task on another computer also wait their turn instead of running a seventh task, keep what you picked even if Enconvo restarts meanwhile, and are not passed by a new message for the same member, tasks of a stopped run no longer hold slots that ready tasks wait for, and the Lead sees which tasks wait for a slot without a turn of its own spent on it.
- Fixed Teamwork members after Enconvo restarts: a turn the restart cut runs again once every time, where a member that had already come back from one restart stopped as failed at the next, and a member stopped on an error keeps waiting for your Resume instead of trying its failed turn again by itself.
- Fixed a Teamwork task handed to your other computer while it was offline failing at once and later running there as a plain task: it now says "Waiting for <computer> to come online", starts there with its name and its team role once that computer is back, and stops there, or never starts, once you stop it, move it to This Mac or delete its Project.
- Added @ an Agent in a Project chat with Teamwork on: the Lead plans the work as usual and the named Agent takes the part you gave it, or every role for this run when you hand it the whole request, while the Lead and your saved team stay as they are; a line in the chat says who runs what, Change moves a role to another Agent or back to the saved team, and an Agent that isn't signed in takes nothing and offers Sign in or Run with the saved team.
- Fixed a Coding agent such as Claude Code or Codex, leading a Project with Teamwork on, doing parts of the work itself: it now gives every part to the team's members, as Enconvo's own Lead does.
- Fixed a Project's Lead saying it wrote things down in Ideas: it now says Todos, the Project's Todos tab, and knows an item there can be a to-do, reminder, goal, bug or idea.
- Removed Task workflows from Projects, now that a Project's team works by its Instructions and its Members: a Project's Settings page no longer has Task workflows or their switch, the Lead no longer gives tasks a workflow or lists workflows in the @ menu, and the phones no longer list a Project's workflows; a task that was following a workflow ends with a note and runs as an ordinary task when you try it again, and a saved workflow with member slots now asks you to name an Agent for those steps instead.

## Projects

- Changed a Project's settings to show right in its Settings page instead of a separate window, with Save and Discard appearing once you change something, a check before you leave with unsaved changes, and unsaved changes kept for the next time you open the settings if you leave another way.
- Changed where a Project's Settings open: a gear at the top right of the Project's bar, or Project settings in a chat's ⋯ menu once the chat has messages.
- Changed the Automations row in a Project's Settings to open the Automations page showing only that Project's automations; remove the Project filter there to see them all.

## Models and Errors

- Fixed backup models taking a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini subscription's usage limit (such as "You've hit your limit") for a busy service: the reply now moves to your backup model even when busy services don't switch, and the model waits until its limit resets (half an hour when the service doesn't say) instead of being tried again two minutes later.
- Fixed agents and Teamwork members stopping with "Overloaded" or rate-limit errors when many of them work at once: a busy model service is now asked again for up to about a minute and a half, with waits that grow and are spread out so the agents don't all come back at the same moment.
- Added a step down for an overloaded Claude model on Anthropic or Enconvo Cloud when no backup models are set: Claude Fable goes on with Opus and Opus with Sonnet on the same account, the chat says so, and the original model is used again after two minutes.
- Fixed a failed AI request's error in a chat showing and saving your API key and your message when the service echoed them back: the error now keeps the status, the address and the service's own words and hides the key and what you wrote, also when you copy it or have it read aloud, and errors saved before this fix are hidden the same way on the Mac, the iPhone and Android.

## Devices and Pairing

- Added Settings › Devices, one list of every computer, phone and server on your account with what each may use on this Mac, what this Mac may use there, whether it syncs, and how many of your plan's computers are in use.
- Added Pause for another computer or server in Settings › Devices, which stops it taking new work from your other devices while it stays signed in and paired.
- Added Remove from account in Settings › Devices, which signs a lost or sold computer out, unpairs it everywhere, takes it off Desktop Sync, and erases a Mac's API keys and sign-ins (and its chats, if you choose) when it next connects, with Sign out everywhere as a stronger option.
- Added Ask first when pairing a computer, so every part starts off and the other computer asks you on this Mac the first time it needs one.
- Changed pairing a computer so it starts with Chats and agents, leaving Terminal, Screen and Files off until you turn them on.
- Changed the Device limit notice so one press signs out a computer that is idle or paused and activates this Mac.
- Changed the primary computer's amber star to a Primary computer badge wherever it is shown.
- Changed Connections so it no longer sits under Settings › Agents; your devices are in Settings › Devices, and pairing settings open from its Add a device menu.
- Changed Settings › Sync on a computer that was removed from your account so it says so and offers the quickest way to activate it again, instead of a plan upgrade.

## Dictation, Draft and Voice Command

- Changed Dictation on Enconvo Cloud to use no points: speech-to-text, transcribing a recording again from Dictation History, Quick Memory voice notes and their summaries, and Polish, Draft, Translate and Edit on a fast model are free, up to a daily fair-use limit well beyond everyday use.
- Fixed Dictation using points with Enconvo Cloud Fast when you stopped talking before its live connection had opened: Dictation now waits a moment for the connection and transcribes your words on it for free, and never sends the recording off to be transcribed again; if the connection can't open, the recording is kept in Dictation History and Dictation tells you.
- Fixed Quick Memory voice notes using points with Enconvo Cloud Fast when nothing was heard: a note with no words is no longer sent off to be transcribed again, and a recording the live connection never heard is kept in Dictation History instead.
- Fixed recordings and files transcribed with ElevenLabs Scribe showing one block of text: the transcript now shows each speaker's turns with their times, Talk time and the speaker timeline, as with the other transcription models.
- Fixed transcribing long recordings with ElevenLabs on Enconvo Cloud: meetings over about nine minutes failed with "413 Payload Too Large" and recordings of an hour and a half or more stopped after a minute; recordings are now compressed before they upload and wait as long as transcribing takes.
- Added what goes to the model to Dictation's Draft: click the app at the end of the Draft panel's header to see the model, the window screenshot, the app's window title, text box, address and visible text, and the selected text that go with your draft, turn each off or on, and change the model for drafts without leaving the panel.
- Changed Draft to send a screenshot of the app's window by default, so drafts can see what you see; turn it off in the Draft panel or in Settings › Dictation › Draft.
- Changed the Draft panel's header to read "Edits all text" or "Edits selection" instead of "Whole field" or "Selection", with the DRAFT label set tighter.
- Added ⇧ to Voice Command: hold it with your hotkey, or tap it while Voice Command listens, and a picture of the window you're in goes with your words, with the app, its window title, its address and the text you selected; the HUD shows the window in place of Screen Doodle and Live Screen, a click on it shows what goes, and Settings › Voice Command picks another key or turns it off.

## Chat Window and Apps

- Added Install as App on the Mac (Beta): a page made in chat, or a Space, installs as an app of its own with its own window, Dock icon and menu bar, flies into the Dock as it installs, and always shows the newest version; Spaces kept in the Dock move to apps in the same place.
- Added ⌘W in the Chat Window's side panel: it closes the tab you're looking at, then the panel with its last tab, and the window only once the panel is closed, as a browser does; ⇧⌘W still closes the window at once.
- Changed Continue in Smart Bar in a chat's ⋯ menu to read Smart Bar, so it fits on one line.

## iPhone, iPad and Android

- Fixed dragging and text selection in Remote Screen's Trackpad mode on iPhone and Android: hold a finger still until the phone taps, then slide, and the computer's button stays down at the pointer, so text selects and windows move; let go without sliding and it still right-clicks, and the Trackpad hints now say so.
- Fixed a thin grip bar staying in the middle of an iPhone or iPad chat after its side panel closed, where it also caught sideways swipes.
- Fixed your camera arriving turned on its side for everyone else in a call made from an unfolded iPhone Duo: the picture now follows which way is up, as it does when the phone is folded.
- Fixed Share Camera in a voice call sending its picture turned on its side from an unfolded iPhone Duo or an iPhone held sideways, with the preview turned on the Duo too: what the call sees and the preview now follow which way is up.
