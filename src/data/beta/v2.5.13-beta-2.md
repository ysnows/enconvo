# Enconvo 2.5.13 Beta 2

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.13 Beta 2. This build lets agents call people from your own iPhone number and answer the calls you miss, adds quick tools to Live Talk, GitHub triggers for Automations, Muse Code and Grok Bot as Coding agents, several accounts per Connector, and a much smarter App Sidebar beside your browser, Finder and other apps. Update the iPhone and Android apps to try the phone changes.

## Highlights

- **Your iPhone in Agent Phone**: agents can call people from your own iPhone number through your Mac, with a picture-led setup guide that turns on Calls from iPhone for you.
- **Answer my calls**: when you miss a call to your iPhone, an agent can answer it on your Mac, take a message, and send the call to Updates.
- **Calls from**: when an agent calls you, it can ring from Your iPhone, Twilio or Telnyx, and you can ask for a call through any of them or the EnConvo app.
- **Recorded calls with your friends**: an agent's calls to your friends are recorded into Recordings, and the agent waits for their reply before it says goodbye.
- **Quick tools in Live Talk**: during a call the assistant can search your Memory and past chats, remember things, read your Calendar and Reminders, search your mail, and use read-only MCP tools.
- **GitHub triggers for Automations**: run an automation when an issue or pull request opens, a comment mentions you, CI fails, or a release is published, and reply on GitHub after you approve.
- **Add to Ideas**: each GitHub issue, Discord or Telegram message, email or webhook an Automation hears can become an idea on a Project's Ideas board, ready for its lead to reproduce or research.
- **Muse Code**: Meta's coding agent joins the Coding agents, with diffs, approvals, skills, its own sessions to continue, and Continue in Terminal.
- **Grok Bot**: chat with your own xAI Grok Bot routine in Enconvo, and let it reply to your chats, notify your devices and ask an agent on your Mac through Enconvo's MCP server.
- **Enconvo Capabilities for Coding agents**: turn off Enconvo's plugins for Claude Code, Codex, Grok Build, Muse Code or Antigravity, all at once or one at a time.
- **Subagent steering**: agents can message, interrupt and steer running Subagents, choose their model, effort and instructions, and nest them three levels deep, with Claude Code or Codex as Subagents.
- **Several accounts per Connector**: connect more than one Notion, Webflow, Linear or other account, pick a default, and set a default account for each Workspace.
- **Workspaces by agent**: ask an agent to list, create, rename, recolor, reorder, delete or restore your Workspaces.
- **Shopify across stores**: the Shopify plugin asks which store, names it in every change, and runs a change for several stores once per store.
- **App Sidebar page card**: beside a browser, App Sidebar shows what it can read on the page, such as a video's captions and chapters, and reads ahead so answers start sooner.
- **Built-in App Sidebar profiles**: YouTube, Bilibili, GitHub, Reddit, X, Gmail and more, plus Finder, chat apps, git clients, code editors, terminals and Word, each with starters and an Insert button.
- **Finder file jobs**: compress, convert and resize images, compress PDFs, convert video and audio, zip, unzip and batch rename the selected files from App Sidebar.
- **Transcribe YouTube audio**: summarize a YouTube video without captions by transcribing its audio, with timestamps.
- **Pull request and Device tabs**: see a branch's pull request with its checks and reviews (⌃⇧P), or watch and control an iOS Simulator or Android Emulator (⌃⇧M), beside the chat.
- **Spaces in the Dock and menu bar**: keep a Space in the Dock in its own window, or show it from its own menu bar icon.
- **Dictation to Draft**: tap ⇧ while dictating to turn what you said into a Draft's instruction, and let Drafts read the app you're in and optionally a screenshot.
- **Remove Filler Words**: Translator and Fix Spelling & Grammar drop ums, stutters and false starts from dictated text.
- **Contrast and Chat Width**: make borders and secondary text stronger or softer, and let chats grow wider in a large window.
- **A livelier agent overlay**: Computer Use and Browser Use clicks ripple across the window, and a soft light runs around its edge while the agent works.

## Agent Phone and Calls

- Added Your iPhone to Settings › Agent Phone: agents can call other people from your own iPhone number through your Mac, so the people they call see your number.
- Added a picture-led setup guide for Your iPhone that shows how your Mac and iPhone connect, walks through one step at a time, and turns on Calls from iPhone in FaceTime for you in one click.
- Added Calls from to Settings › Agent Phone › Tasks awaiting your input: when an agent calls your number, it can ring from Your iPhone, Twilio or Telnyx, and you can ask an agent in a chat to call you through any of them or the EnConvo app.
- Added Answer my calls: when you don't pick up a call to your iPhone, an agent can answer it on your Mac, take a message, and send the call to Updates.
- Added recordings to an agent's calls to your friends: the friend hears and sees that the call is recorded, and when it ends the recording is in Recordings and opens from the call card in the chat.
- Changed agents to call your Enconvo friends and teammates on Enconvo first, and to call their phone number only when you ask for a phone call or that call doesn't reach them.
- Changed an agent's calls to your friends so the agent chats freely when asked, such as telling a story or explaining something, instead of refusing anything outside its brief.
- Fixed the person an agent calls through Your iPhone not hearing the agent: Enconvo Audio now plays the agent's voice through a device of its own, so the call's echo cancelling no longer removes it, and each call picks Enconvo Audio as the Phone app's microphone and puts your choice back afterwards; Settings asks you to add Enconvo Audio again once.
- Fixed agent calls through Your iPhone not going out on macOS 27, where the call prompt shows as a notification.
- Fixed agent calls through Your iPhone staying on after the other person hung up; the call now ends as soon as they hang up.
- Fixed Add again on Your iPhone in Settings › Agent Phone giving no word on macOS's password window and staying on Enconvo Audio is missing after you typed the password.
- Fixed an agent's call to a friend hanging up before the friend could answer; the agent now waits for their reply before it says goodbye.
- Fixed an agent's call to a friend showing as a conference with "You're the only one here" after the agent hung up; the friend's screen now shows it as a call with the agent, and the call ends for them when the agent hangs up.

## Live Talk

- Added quick tools to Live Talk calls: the assistant can now search your Memory and past chats, save something you ask it to remember, read your Calendar and Reminders, search your mail, and use read-only tools of your MCP servers right in the call, without starting a task.
- Changed Live Talk's updates during a long task: they now tell what the task has found so far, not only what it is doing.
- Fixed slower replies in the first turns of a Live Talk call.
- Fixed Live Talk remembering the whole sentence it was cut off in when you talk over it with a Mac or Gemini voice; it now remembers only what you heard.

## Automations

- Added GitHub triggers to Automations: an automation can run when an issue or pull request is opened, an issue is labeled, a comment mentions you, CI fails, or a release is published in a repository you connect, only for the people you choose, with events kept sealed for your Mac until you allow outside triggers there.
- Added Reply on GitHub to an automation's Then options: after a run, Enconvo asks you before posting its reply as a comment on the issue or pull request that started it.
- Added Add to Ideas to Automations: each GitHub issue, Discord or Telegram message, email or webhook an Automation hears becomes an idea on a Project's Ideas board, where its lead can reproduce a bug and plan the fix, or research a request and close it with a reason or plan it with designs, then asks you before anything changes.

## Coding Agents

- Added Muse Code, Meta's coding agent, as a Coding agent: install it and sign in with Meta from its settings, then chat with it in a project folder, where it edits code with diffs, runs commands, asks before acting, offers its skills and /compact in the / menu, and its own sessions show up to continue under the names you gave them.
- Added Continue in Terminal and Copy command to Muse Code chats: the chat opens with `muse resume` in its folder, and the turns you run there come back into the chat when you quit Muse Code and send from Enconvo again.
- Added Enconvo Capabilities to each Coding agent's settings: turn off Enconvo's plugins for Claude Code, Codex, Grok Build, Muse Code or Antigravity altogether, or one plugin at a time, such as messaging Enconvo agents, image creation, memory or Computer Use, so that agent can't see or use it.
- Changed Muse Code to keep working after it updates itself, and to say when Enconvo needs an update to work with a newer Muse Code.
- Changed Muse Code chats to work without a Meta sign-in when Muse Code runs on its local model, and to take their first message as their title when they start from the Smart Bar or the iPhone and Muse Code doesn't name them.
- Changed Muse Code's approval questions to show the change an edit would make, and to pass what you type with Deny to Muse Code in the same turn, so it can act on it right away.
- Fixed a Muse Code chat continued with `muse resume` typed in a terminal by hand not showing as running in that terminal, and long Muse Code chats taking much longer to reopen than short ones.
- Fixed a Coding agent chat whose first message was refused, such as when the agent isn't signed in, taking that message as its title; it stays New Chat.
- Fixed links to an agent's settings, such as Muse Code's, opening "This plugin isn't installed" instead of the agent's page.
- Fixed a step you denied still reading as done, such as "Edited notes.txt", and a Coding agent reply stopped from the iPhone or Android app before it showed anything leaving no line in the chat.
- Fixed coding agents' steps and "Thought for" lines staying in English in chats shown in another language.
- Fixed Coding agent chats in a client's Workspace saving their replies, asking their questions, calling other agents and loading skills in Personal; they now stay entirely inside that Workspace.
- Fixed a Coding agent's Enconvo Capabilities sometimes not applying to its open chats when they were changed in Personal and in a client's Workspace at the same time.

## Grok Bot

- Added Grok Bot, xAI's agent that runs in the cloud, as a Coding agent: connect your own Grok Bot routine in its settings, then chat with it in Enconvo, where each message goes to the routine and its reply comes back to the chat, even when it arrives late.
- Added Enconvo's MCP server for Grok Bot: connect it in Grok Bot's settings, add its address in Grok Bot's Plugins, and Grok Bot signs in with your Enconvo account, so it can reply to your chats, notify your Mac and your paired phones, and ask an agent on your Mac, which you approve each time unless you always allow it; each request stays listed in the Grok Bot on this Mac chat, and disconnecting Grok Bot, or your Mac going offline, withdraws a request still waiting.
- Added a length limit to Grok Bot messages: a message over 32,000 characters is refused with a note to shorten it, and attached files and images stay on your Mac while only the text is sent.
- Fixed Grok Bot losing the answer to a request it sent your Mac when you approved it after about a minute; Grok Bot now gets the answer once it comes, and the request still runs once.
- Fixed tapping a Grok Bot notification on iPhone and Android opening nothing: it opens the Grok Bot chat the notification is about, or your Mac's chats when it names none, and clicking it on the Mac opens that chat too.
- Fixed the agent picker on the Mac, iPhone, and Android to show Grok Bot as Not set up, instead of Not installed, until its routine is connected.
- Fixed Grok Bot staying connected for replies after its webhook was cleared in Credential Management, and its settings showing or putting back an old webhook after it was changed.
- Fixed Grok Bot's Test saying it couldn't reach Enconvo when the routine or Enconvo was only turning away too many messages; it now says so.
- Fixed the Files tab of a Grok Bot chat on iPhone and Android offering files from your Mac; it now says the files Grok Bot makes stay in xAI's cloud, without Sort or search.
- Fixed Context & Usage in a Grok Bot chat on iPhone and Android asking you to send a message to fill it; it now says xAI's cloud doesn't report the tokens Grok Bot's replies use.
- Fixed the note box in Grok Bot's request to your Mac saying the note goes to Enconvo; it now says it goes to Grok Bot, which gets your note as the answer.
- Fixed Grok Bot's notes, requests, records and delivery errors showing in English on the Mac, iPhone, and Android when the app uses another language, its note button saying it sends to an agent, the Grok Bot on this Mac chat keeping the name it was first given in another language, and a stray space before an agent's name in Chinese.

## Agents and Subagents

- Added agent-to-agent steering: an agent can now add a message to a running Subagent's task, interrupt it, or reach another agent's chat in the same Project, and stopping a chat now stops every Subagent below it.
- Added model, reasoning effort, instructions, name, and fork options when an agent starts a Subagent, with Subagents nesting up to three levels and Claude Code or Codex available as Subagents.
- Added more to Subagents in the chat: starting one shows the model and effort it runs on, a message to one says whether it steered the running task, waited, interrupted it or started a new turn, a Subagent's chat marks each message with the agent that sent it, and the chat's Lineage shows the whole tree, three levels deep.
- Added Workspace management to agents: when you ask, an agent can list, create, rename, recolor, reorder, delete and restore your Workspaces, and it confirms before deleting one.
- Changed Lineage in a Coding agent's chat Details to show the Enconvo chat that started it.
- Fixed the mark that says which agent sent a message: it now always names the agent that really sent it, also on queued /loop commands and on messages steered into a Grok Build or Muse Code turn, and an open Lineage shows a new Coding agent Subagent without reopening it.
- Fixed agents, Coding agents, Workflow steps and Automations being able to answer a question meant for you, write or edit a chat's messages, change a Coding agent's Enconvo Capabilities, or start another agent's run by sending the call through the Local API's request route, another command, several wrapped commands or a context read; all of them now refuse those calls the same way.
- Fixed agents, Workflow steps and Automations being able to start a workflow's repair agent as another agent, such as a Coding agent, outside your chat's Permission mode; the repair agent now always works as the chat's own agent, within its mode, and its task shows who sent it.

## Connectors and Plugins

- Added several accounts to Connectors that sign in on your Mac, such as Notion, Webflow and Linear: connect another account from the Connector's page, pick the default, disconnect one, and name an account in a request so the agent uses that one.
- Added a default account per Workspace for every Connector, so in a client's Workspace the agent uses that client's account unless you name another.
- Changed the Shopify plugin to work across several stores: the agent asks which store when you don't name one, names the store in every change it shows, and runs a change meant for several stores once per store.

## App Sidebar

- Added a page card to App Sidebar beside a browser: it shows the page you're on with what can be read, such as a video's length, captions and chapters, an article's length even on older page layouts or a PDF's pages, and reads the page ahead so the first answer starts sooner.
- Added built-in profiles for YouTube, Bilibili, GitHub, Reddit, Hacker News, X, Amazon, Gmail, Outlook and Stack Overflow in App Sidebar, each with starters and answers that respect the available page content, including video chapters you can click to jump to.
- Added built-in App Sidebar profiles for Finder, chat apps including WeCom and Teams, git clients including SourceTree, code editors including Windsurf, terminals, notes apps, Pages and Word, with starters for each and an Insert button that puts a reply, commit message or command into an identified text field without sending or running it, with clear guidance when insertion is unavailable.
- Added file jobs for Finder in App Sidebar: compress, convert and resize images, compress PDFs, convert video and audio, zip and unzip, and batch rename the selected files, with smaller PNG copies, clear unsupported-format messages and starters that follow your selection, each shown in a card with before and after sizes and Reveal in Finder; your originals stay untouched.
- Added per-site settings to App Sidebar's site pill: turn page reading on or off, choose the answer language and summary style, add your own instructions, and edit the starters.
- Added Transcribe the audio for YouTube videos without captions in App Sidebar: when you pick it, Enconvo transcribes the video's audio and summarizes it with timestamps.
- Added a Now on row to App Sidebar when you move to another page during a chat, so you can ask about the new page in one click.
- Added a card in App Sidebar beside Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc and other Chromium browsers without the Enconvo Companion extension, with an Install button; once the extension connects, the card turns into the page card and clears its setup reminder.
- Changed the App Sidebar handle to stay hidden until you move the pointer to the right edge of an app window, with a short guide the first time; turn off "Show handle only at window edge" in Settings or the handle's menu to keep it always visible.
- Changed the App Sidebar handle's hover card to show the page you're on, such as a video's length and captions.
- Changed App Sidebar to read pages without the browser's debugger, so the "started debugging this browser" bar no longer appears while it reads your tab.
- Fixed App Sidebar opening a second tab with the same page when you asked about the page you had open, and the original YouTube tab sometimes going dark after a summary; your tab is now read as it is and never reloaded or changed.
- Fixed App Sidebar showing an update prompt when an older Enconvo Companion cannot read the page.

## Dictation and Voice

- Added a quick way to turn a dictation into a Draft: while dictating, tap ⇧ (your Draft modifier) once, even after letting go of the Dictation key, and what you said so far becomes the Draft's instruction.
- Added Send app context and Include a screenshot to Settings › Dictation › Draft: a Draft now reads the app you're in, its window title, web address and visible text, and optionally a screenshot of its window, so the draft fits what you're working on.
- Added Remove Filler Words to Translator and Fix Spelling & Grammar, on by default: dictated text loses its ums, ahs, stutters and false starts, so translations and corrections read like written text.
- Changed Dictation to paste sooner after you stop: a short dictation no longer waits on the snippet check unless its words are the name of one of your snippets, and the text field is read while the last words are finished.
- Fixed Voice Clone recording so pressing Record goes straight to the reading screen and stays there through the countdown, the microphone starting and saving, instead of flashing back to the upload screen; the countdown can now be canceled, and the same holds for Gemini's consent recording and the Qwen3 clone dialog.

## Chat Window and Settings

- Added a Pull request tab to the chat side panel (⌃⇧P): see the branch's pull request on GitHub with its checks, review and comments, or create one from a Worktree chat.
- Added a Device tab to the chat side panel (⌃⇧M): watch and control an iOS Simulator or Android Emulator next to the chat, after a one-time setup.
- Added Keep in Dock and Show in Menu Bar to a Space's menu in the Chat Window sidebar: the Space gets its own icon in the Dock that opens it in a window of its own, or its own menu bar icon that drops it down, both with Ask about.
- Added Contrast and Chat Width to Settings › General: make borders, dividers and secondary text stronger or softer across Enconvo, and let messages and the composer grow wider in a large window.
- Changed the Agent overlay for Computer Use and Browser Use: a light wave now ripples out from each click and gently bends the window beneath it, a soft light runs around the window edge while the agent works, and the dot grid lights up only around the agent cursor.
- Fixed the Chat Window's chat list sometimes showing a finished chat as still running when the Dynamic Island or Smart Bar was open too.
- Fixed the send button showing an X after a message couldn't be sent, which looked like a stop button that never went away.
- Fixed the first message of a new chat disappearing when it couldn't be sent, such as when the chat's folder no longer exists; it now stays, with the reason under it.

- Changed the speaker's small picture over a full-screen shared screen in the iPhone app's calls so you can drag it to any corner, out of the way of what's being shown.
- Changed the Android app downloaded from enconvo.com to check for updates every time you open it or come back to it, instead of at most every six hours.

- Fixed the iPhone and Android apps showing the steps and to-do lists of Muse Code, Grok Build and Antigravity as plain text instead of tool steps.
- Fixed the iPhone app's chat showing an agent's messages to its Subagents and its look at them as plain steps; they now show as Subagent lines with whether the message steered the task, waits for it to finish, interrupted it, or started a new turn, each Subagent shows the model and effort it runs on and whether it is nested, and a message another agent sent says who it is from.
- Fixed the phone Companion failing to load chats, Agents and Projects with "Enconvo on your Mac isn't answering" when the Mac's connection dropped for a moment while it was busy.
- Fixed Mac chats on Android so a brief connection drop or a busy Mac no longer shows an error or clears the messages, a reply keeps streaming once the Mac is back, and a chat opened before shows again without loading.
- Fixed the iPhone app hiding an agent's approval request, such as calling a friend, behind a one-line bar when you came back to the chat right after sending; the request now opens with its Allow and Deny buttons.
- Fixed the iPhone chat covering the call controls when you answer a scheduled call from your agent; the call's buttons now stay on top, as in a Live Talk call you start yourself.
- Fixed the "Calling you" card in an iPhone chat still saying Ringing after you picked up; it now follows the call as it does on the Mac.
