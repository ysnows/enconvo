# Enconvo 2.5.15 Beta 1

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.15 Beta 1. This build adds Ukrainian and Russian to the interface and sixty languages to meeting captions, puts a calculator in the Dynamic Island and smarter lookups in the Smart Bar, lets agents open parallel chats and move a chat into a worktree, and makes messages from your phone show the moment you send them.

## Highlights

- **Ukrainian and Russian**: two new interface languages on the Mac, iPhone and Android, and every language the speech engine supports in meeting captions and translation.
- **Calculator in the Dynamic Island**: type a sum, a conversion or a time on Home and the answer appears under the island.
- **Weather, flights and places in the Smart Bar**: ask "weather in London", a flight number or "time in Chernivtsi".
- **Parallel chats**: an agent can open a new chat for a separate task that shows in your chat list like one you started.
- **Chats move into worktrees**: move a chat into a new or existing worktree with its files, and merging brings it back.
- **Background work shows**: a violet spinner marks chats whose reply is done while subagents or background tasks still run.

## Languages

- Added Ukrainian and Russian as interface languages on the Mac, iPhone and Android apps, in Settings › General › Language.
- Added every language the speech engine supports to meeting captions and translation, Ukrainian included, sixty in all and listed by name.

## Smart Bar and Dynamic Island

- Added the calculator to the Dynamic Island: type a sum, a conversion or a time on Home and the answer appears in a card under the island; press Return to copy it, or Command-Return to ask your agent instead. Turn it off, or turn on Weather and Flights, in Settings › Dynamic Island › Home.
- Added weather, flight and place lookups to the Smart Bar calculator, off by default: turn on Weather and Flights in Settings › Smart Bar, then type "weather in London", "Tokyo feels like", a flight number like "BA008" or "time in Chernivtsi".
- Added splitting a total to the Smart Bar calculator, as in "25% off $60 split by 3".
- Added Calculator switches to Settings › Smart Bar, to turn the calculator off or its online lookups on.
- Fixed the Smart Bar calculator not knowing the time in Kyiv, Lviv, Odesa, Kolkata, Kathmandu, Doha and other cities.

## Chats and Agents

- Added parallel chats: when you ask an agent to open a new chat for a separate task, it opens one that shows in your chat list on the Mac, iPhone and Android like a chat you started, with a note at the top saying which chat started it, while reviews and tests for that task still run as its subagents; a chat an agent started can also be moved to your chat list from its card.
- Added moving a chat between the main checkout and a worktree on the Mac, iPhone and Android: from the chip after its title or the chat menu on the phone, or by asking its agent, it can move into a new or existing worktree with the files it changed, and merging the worktree brings the chat back to the main checkout to go on, with a line in the conversation at each move.
- Added a violet spinner to chats in the sidebar on the Mac, iPhone and Android whose reply has finished while subagents or background tasks they started are still running, with a blue dot inside it when there is a new reply to read.
- Fixed the background tray over the message box showing new subagents only after the agent finished writing their tasks, which could take a minute; they now show as soon as the chat's subagent card does, on the Mac and the phone.
- Fixed agents and subagents in Full access being asked to approve a sync or delete into a folder the same command had just set to a fixed path, as if it could delete the whole disk.
- Fixed Full access letting a few deletes that empty the working folder or the home folder run without asking, such as a ?* or ** glob, a folder reached through a link, or rsync --delete into a folder named by a variable that may be empty.

## iPhone and Android

- Fixed messages sent from the iPhone and Android apps into a Mac chat while its reply is running showing only after a delay; they now show under the reply the moment you send and steer it, as Return does on the Mac.
- Fixed messages with photos or files sent from the iPhone and Android apps into a Mac chat showing only several seconds after sending; they now show with their photos the moment you send.
- Changed tapping a subagent in a Mac chat on the iPhone and Android apps to open its steps in a sheet over the chat that a swipe down closes, instead of leaving the chat for the subagent's own thread.
- Changed All above Home's active chats in the iPhone and Android apps to open a panel of every active chat right on Home, grouped by what needs you, what is running and what has results, which you pull down to close.
- Added a fold to the Pinned group in the iPhone and Android apps' sidebars: tap Pinned to hide or show your pinned chats, and the app remembers your choice.
- Changed answering an Agent's call on Android, from the ring notification or the lock screen, to connect the call in the background without unlocking or opening the app, like a phone call, with an ongoing call notification that ends the call or opens its chat.
- Added pairing the iPhone and Android apps with a Linux computer that has only a terminal, such as a server: run enconvo companion pair --phone and scan the QR code it draws with your phone's camera, or open the link it prints on the phone, and the computer shows in the app's list of computers.
- Fixed the suggested next message over the message box in a project's lead chat on the iPhone and Android apps staying on screen for a moment after sending; it now goes away the moment you send.

## Recordings and Transcription

- Fixed the app freezing, with every Settings page stuck, while a long audio file dragged into Transcription is being converted for transcription.
- Fixed confirm dialogs, such as deleting a recording with a long file name, spilling the title and buttons out of the dialog; long names now wrap inside it.
