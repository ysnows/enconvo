# Enconvo 2.5.5 Beta 31

Thanks for testing Enconvo 2.5.5 Beta 31. This build updates the official
DeepSeek lineup with a faster multimodal option.

## Highlights

- **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is now available** through your own DeepSeek API key or
  Enconvo Cloud, with image understanding, a 1M-token context window, and tool
  use.
- **Get more DeepSeek usage with Enconvo Cloud**: the Pro plan offers all
  DeepSeek models at half price for up to 5M points of usage, while the Max plan
  offers them at quarter price for up to 20M points of usage.
