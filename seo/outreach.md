## Facts to use

- Native macOS app (not Electron). macOS 14 or later, Intel and Apple Silicon.
- Works in any app, using screen and selected-text context.
- Model choices:
  - OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini and other providers with the user's own key.
  - Existing ChatGPT, Claude or Grok subscriptions.
  - Local models through MLX, Ollama or LM Studio.
- MCP servers, plugins, skills and workflows.
- Free download with every core feature. One-time licenses: Standard $49,
  Premium $99, Teams from $99 for 3 seats. Optional Enconvo Cloud: Plus
  $10/month or $96/year, Pro $50/month, Max $100/month.
- 30-day money-back guarantee on licenses.
- Site: https://www.enconvo.com/ · Docs: https://docs.enconvo.ai/
